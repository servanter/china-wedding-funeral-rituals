# Foundations of Astrological Matchmaking

## 1. Definition

Matchmaking by astrology judges the fortune of a marriage from the birth data of both parties. Four methods are common:

| Method | Basis |
| --- | --- |
| **Eight-character matching** | The generation and overcoming, and the preferences, of the four pillars of each party |
| **Zodiac matching** | The six harmonies, six clashes and three harmonies among the zodiac animals |
| **Nayin matching** | The mutual generation and overcoming of the nayin agents of the sixty combinations |
| **Star-fate matching** | The position of the constellations and the palace of fate |

| Zodiac relation | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| **Six harmonies** | *zi-chou, yin-hai, mao-xu, chen-you, si-shen, wu-wei* |
| **Six clashes** | *zi-wu, chou-wei, yin-shen, mao-you, chen-xu, si-hai* |
| **Three harmonies** | *shen-zi-chen, hai-mao-wei, yin-wu-xu, si-you-chou* |

## 2. Historical Origins

- The Han already knew the doctrine of *nayin*, found in musical pitch theory and the arts of calculation.
- In the Song, matchmaking became a custom; the *Record of the Dream of the Eastern Capital* describes the rites of exchanging cards and matching horoscopes.
- In the Ming and Qing, matching by the eight characters was the orthodox method and books of fate flourished.
- Today the practice is often simplified to “zodiac compatibility”, though matching the eight characters survives in the countryside.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Exchanging horoscope cards**: the two families exchange the birth data.
2. **Casting the eight characters**: setting out the four pillars, and determining the day master and what the five agents favour and forbid.
3. **Checking generation and overcoming**: examining the zodiac, the nayin and the day master for mutual generation and overcoming.
4. **Determining what is fitting and what is to be avoided**: judging the fortune of the match and discussing how to avoid what is inauspicious (choosing a day, postponing the date).
5. **Issuing documents**: recording the chosen auspicious day and hour on the marriage contract and the date card.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **North China**: attention falls on zodiac compatibility, and the six clashes are avoided.
- **South of the Yangtze**: the eight characters and nayin matter, and a “matchmaking sheet” is carefully prepared.
- **Ethnic-minority areas**: the marriage is generally decided by the people’s own divination (chicken divination, sheep-bone divination, consulting a shaman), which differs from Chinese fate calculation and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Documents**: horoscope cards, marriage contracts, date cards, books of fate.
- **Objects**: the abacus, almanacs, the fate chart.
