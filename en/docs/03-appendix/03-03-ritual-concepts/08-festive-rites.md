# The Festive Rites (Jiali): An Outline

## 1. Definition

The festive rites are those by which “the people are brought close”, taking their sense from auspicious occasions and standing opposite the mourning rites. According to the “Office of the Great Ancestor” chapter of the *Rites of Zhou*, they comprise six classes:

| Class | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| **Rites of food and drink** | Banquets, the village drinking ceremony |
| **Rites of capping and marriage** | The capping ceremony and the wedding (**marriage belongs here**) |
| **Rites of archery** | The archery ceremony, pitch-pot |
| **Rites of entertainment** | The entertainment ceremony, the feast |
| **Rites of sacrificial meat** | Distribution of sacrificial meat |
| **Rites of congratulation** | Congratulations and celebrations |

The marriage book of this corpus is precisely the “wedding” section of the festive rites.

## 2. Historical Origins

- The *Rites of Zhou* established the six classes of festive rite, and the *Yili* preserves the chapters “Capping of a Knight”, “Wedding of a Knight”, “Village Drinking Ceremony”, “Village Archery”, “Feast” and “Great Archery”.
- Dynastic ritual codes all contain a section on festive rites: the *Great Tang Code of the Kaiyuan Reign*, the *New Ritual Code of the Zhenghe Reign*, the *Collected Rites of the Ming* and the *Comprehensive Rituals of the Great Qing* alike.
- The Song *Family Rituals* simplified the capping and wedding ceremonies and extended them to commoners.
- In modern times the capping ceremony declined while the wedding alone flourished.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

Taking the most important of the festive rites as examples:

1. **Capping**: divining the day — divining the guest — the three cappings (plain cloth cap, leather cap, ceremonial cap) — toasting the guest — presentation to the mother and to the brothers — announcing at the ancestral temple.
2. **Wedding**: nacai — wenming — naji — nazheng — qingqi — qinying, followed by presentation at the ancestral temple and meeting the parents-in-law.
3. **Village drinking ceremony**: choosing the guest — receiving the guest — presenting, returning and toasting the cup — the general round — seeing the guest off, so as to order seniority and make rank clear.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Han Chinese areas**: the stages of nacai, bowing in the hall, sharing the cup and the return visit are widely preserved.
- **Ethnic-minority areas**: each people has its own ceremonial order and does not classify its rites as festive; their wedding systems must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Documents**: marriage contracts, gift lists, invitations, ceremonial formulas.
- **Objects**: caps and robes, the sharing-cup, the auspicious steelyard, vessels for the feast, vessels for the village drinking ceremony.
