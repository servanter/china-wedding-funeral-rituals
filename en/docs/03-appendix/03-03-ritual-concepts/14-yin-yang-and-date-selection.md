# Yin-Yang, the Five Agents and the Culture of Date Selection

## 1. Definition

**Yin and yang** explain change through the unity of opposites; the **five agents** (metal, wood, water, fire, earth) explain relations and transformation through generation and overcoming. Combined with the **sexagenary cycle** used for reckoning time, these produced the art of choosing auspicious days, the theoretical basis for selecting dates for weddings and funerals.

| Concept | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| **Yin and yang** | The division of heaven and earth, male and female, life and death |
| **Generation among the five agents** | Wood generates fire, fire earth, earth metal, metal water, water wood |
| **Overcoming among the five agents** | Wood overcomes earth, earth water, water fire, fire metal, metal wood |
| **Heavenly stems** | *jia yi bing ding wu ji geng xin ren gui* |
| **Earthly branches** | *zi chou yin mao chen si wu wei shen you xu hai* |
| **Common spirit-killers** | The god of joy, the three malevolences, the year breach, the month breach, clashes |

## 2. Historical Origins

- In the pre-Qin period the yin-yang school (Zou Yan) advanced the doctrine of the succession of the five powers.
- The Han *Huainanzi*, “Astronomical Training”, treats yin-yang and the five agents systematically, and the excavated “day books” already display the art of choosing days.
- In the Tang and Song almanacs and the arts of calculation spread, and choosing days for weddings and funerals became a custom.
- The Qing *Book of the Concordance of Times and the Distinction of Directions* is the officially compiled summation of the art of date selection, by which that art was standardised.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Casting the eight characters**: setting out the four pillars of stems and branches from the year, month, day and hour of birth.
2. **Determining what the five agents favour and forbid**: judging these with the day master as the basis.
3. **Choosing the day**: fixing a “yellow-road” auspicious day from the almanac, avoiding the month breach, the three malevolences and the like.
4. **Fixing the direction**: choosing the “direction of the god of joy” to determine the direction in which the bride’s escort sets out.
5. **Avoiding the killers**: steering clear of the inauspicious directions and hours obtained by calculation.
6. **Writing it down**: recording the chosen date, hour and direction on the date card, the invitations or the obituary.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Han Chinese areas**: the art of choosing days is universal, and is taken especially seriously for weddings and funerals.
- **Ethnic-minority areas**: each people has its own divinatory system (the *bimo* divination of the Yi, Tibetan astrological reckoning, Miao chicken divination), whose principles differ from the Chinese and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Documents**: almanacs, yellow calendars, date cards, horoscope cards.
- **Objects**: the geomantic compass, the abacus, books of fate.
