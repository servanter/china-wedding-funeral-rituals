# Contemporary Change in Wedding and Funeral Custom

## 1. Definition

Contemporary change in wedding and funeral custom has been shaped by three forces: **urbanisation and population mobility** (the venue moving from the family compound to the hotel and the funeral parlour), **the reform of customs and simplified ceremonial** (doing away with extravagance and old abuses), and **the safeguarding of intangible heritage and cultural self-awareness** (the performance and revival of traditional ritual).

| Aspect | Traditional form | Contemporary form |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wedding venue | Family compound, lineage hall | Hotel, wedding venue, travel wedding |
| Wedding ceremonial | Six Rites, bowing in the hall, sharing the cup | A Chinese-Western blend, retaining the bows and the tea service |
| Funeral venue | Lying in state at home | The farewell hall of a funeral parlour |
| Mode of burial | Interment | Cremation, interment of ashes, ecological burial |
| Notification | Obituary, announcing the death at the door | Group chats, text messages, electronic obituaries |
| Documentation | Genealogy, ceremonial register | Digital albums, online memorial pages |

## 2. Historical Origins

- From the middle of the twentieth century funeral reform was promoted, gradually replacing interment with cremation.
- The Marriage Law of 1950 abolished such old customs as arranged marriage and child brides, and established freedom of marriage.
- Since the twenty-first century a system for safeguarding intangible heritage has been established, and many customs related to weddings and funerals have entered the lists at various levels (such as the *Ha Festival* of the Jing people).
- In the past decade the reform of customs has continued, and simplified weddings and funerals and ecological burial have gradually become accepted.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Simplification**: elaborate stages are trimmed, while the symbolic core is retained (the bows, the farewell salute).
2. **New forms of wedding**: collective weddings, travel weddings and charitable weddings have appeared.
3. **Transformation of burial**: cremation is widespread, and ashes interment, sea burial and tree burial are promoted.
4. **Electronic custom**: electronic invitations, online mourning and electronic mourning couplets are becoming normal.
5. **Heritage performance**: traditional rites are restored and performed at festivals and heritage events.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Cities**: ceremonial is simplified, venues have moved outward, and the degree of digitalisation is high.
- **Villages**: traditional ceremonial is better preserved, and often stands in tension with the demand for simplification.
- **Ethnic areas**: traditional rites are valued under heritage protection, and some have shifted from lived practice to cultural performance.
- **Record faithfully**: this coexistence of old and new in the midst of change is precisely an important subject for the study of contemporary custom.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Documents**: marriage certificates, electronic invitations, electronic obituaries, memorial letters, cremation certificates.
- **Objects**: urns, ecological burial vessels, wedding decorations, online memorial pages.
