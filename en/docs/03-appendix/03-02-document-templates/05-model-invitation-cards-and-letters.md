# Model Invitation Cards and Letters

## 1. Definition

The invitation card (*qingtie*, colloquially "joy card") invites guests to the feast and the ceremony. In form it divides into the "single card" (one sheet per person), the "double card" (folded) and the "combined card" (several people on one card). Apart from the wedding invitation, there is the "death-announcing card" for funerals. Today there are also printed invitation letters and electronic invitations.

**Format**: red note in vertical columns from the right; first the matter ("respectfully appointed on…"), then the place, then "humbly we prepare the joy feast and await your honoured presence", ending with the signature and date. The appellation follows the honorific/humble norms (see "Honorific and Self-Deprecating Terms").

## 2. Historical Origins

- The Song already had named invitation "cards", as the banquet records of the *Dream of the Eastern Capital* show.
- Ming–Qing encyclopaedias printed all kinds of card models; the format settled, valuing "the card must be in regular script, the wording refined".
- After the Republic, Western-style invitation letters entered, running alongside the Chinese card.
- Today electronic invitations are common, but formal occasions still value the paper card.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Draw the list**: arrange the invitation list by closeness and generation.
2. **Fix the seating**: by the list, set the seats and places.
3. **Write the card**: one card per person or per household; appellation and layout follow the *Book of Ritual Correspondence*.
4. **Deliver**: by a special person; avoid asking another to pass it on; elders must be visited in person.
5. **Receive the card and reply**: the receiver may offer oral thanks or send a reply card.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **North China**: the card text is plain, valuing a spoken second invitation.
- **Jiangnan**: the card is elegant, with envelopes and gilding.
- **Fujian, Guangdong**: the joy card must be "all red"; the "combined card" can invite a whole family.
- **Ethnic minorities**: mostly notify by word of mouth, or by song-contest and instruments (e.g., blowing the lusheng to announce); paper cards are a later arrival; record separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Documents**: invitation card, death-announcing card, reply card.
- **Objects**: red note, envelope, gilding, card box.

**Model example (traditional invitation card)**:

```text
        Respectfully appointed on ○ year ○ month ○ day (lunar ○ month ○ day)
        for the marriage of our son ○○
        at noon we humbly prepare the joy feast
        and await the honoured presence of
        Mr ○○ and family

                                so-and-so ○○ respectfully appointed
```

**Model example (modern invitation letter)**:

```text
Respected Mr/Ms ○○:
  We respectfully set ○○:○○ (week ○) at noon on ○ month ○ day, ○○○○ year,
  at the ○○ hall of the ○○ hotel, for the wedding of our son ○○ and Ms ○○,
  and humbly prepare the joy feast, awaiting your presence.

                                              ○○○  respectfully invite
```
