# Model Betrothal Letter and Gift List

## 1. Definition

The betrothal letter (*pinshu*) is written at the presenting of the bride-price (sending the betrothal gifts, the great ceremony), stating the intent "to present the coinage and complete the wedding". The gift list (*lidan*, also "gift catalogue", "dowry list", "reply list") itemises the names and quantities of the betrothal gifts and the return gifts — the proof of the exchange of goods.

**Format**: the gift list runs in vertical columns from the right, listing item by item, ending with the total and the signature; the betrothal letter opens with "with reverence" and ends with the year-month and signature.

## 2. Historical Origins

- The *Yili* "Ceremony of a Scholar's Wedding", in presenting the bride-price, uses "dark-red and black silk, a pair of hides" — the weightiest gift — but has no gift list.
- From the Han and Tang, gentry and commoner bridal wealth grew rich, hence the need to list the items.
- The Song "sending the bridal wealth" and "return tray" both came with a gift list, as the *Dream of the Eastern Capital* records.
- Ming–Qing daily-use encyclopaedias printed gift-catalogue models; names like "four-colour gift" and "eight-colour gift" settled.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Agree the gift items**: the two families first discuss the amount of bridal wealth and the return-gift items.
2. **Make the gift list**: the groom's family lists the betrothal gifts, copied fair for use.
3. **Write the betrothal letter**: make a letter stating the intent of presenting the bride-price.
4. **Deliver the gifts**: led by the matchmaker; the gift-bearing poles are checked against the list.
5. **Bride's family replies**: the bride's family receives the gifts, makes a reply card (thank-you card) and returns gifts.
6. **Archive and check**: each side keeps a copy of the list for later checking.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **North China**: values the "four-colour gift" (wine, meat, noodles, sugar); the list is brief.
- **Jiangnan**: the gift-bearing poles are called "tray-poles", valuing the number of "trays"; the items are fine.
- **Fujian, Guangdong**: called "the great ceremony"; the list is in red vertical script, with a separate "return-gift" list.
- **Ethnic minorities**: mostly deliver goods on the spot, speaking through song; where notches on wood or knotted cords keep count, record separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Documents**: betrothal letter, gift list, reply card.
- **Objects**: gift-bearing poles, food boxes, red paper, brush and ink.

**Model example (gift list)**:

```text
Respectfully listed, the betrothal gifts, set out below:
  joy cakes        ○○ jin
  wine             ○○ jars
  pork             ○○ jin
  silk satin       ○ bolts
  gold ornaments   a pair
  tea              ○○ packs
  Set out above, presented to
  the honoured co-parents-in-law of the ○ household, to keep.
                  so-and-so   respectfully made
```

**Model example (betrothal letter)**:

```text
With reverence: of the rites of marriage, presenting the coinage is the weightiest.
Now we present this unworthy ceremony, respectfully offered to your household,
to complete the good of the two surnames.
We pray you look upon and accept it.
      ○ year ○ month ○ day   so-and-so   respectfully made
```
