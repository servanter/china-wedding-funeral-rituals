# Model Memorial Address and Mourning Announcement

## 1. Definition

The **memorial address** (*daoci*) is read at the memorial meeting (the farewell ceremony); it tells the deceased's life and conduct, expresses grief and comforts the family. The **mourning announcement** (*aiqi*, also "life-account") is the text detailing the deceased's life story, used as material for the grave inscription and elegy, sent with the obituary to relatives and friends.

**The difference**: the memorial address values feeling, read aloud; the mourning announcement values fact, written to be handed down.

## 2. Historical Origins

- Antiquity used the "eulogy" (*lei*, listing virtues to fix the posthumous title) and "lamentation" to recount the dead.
- Han–Wei had the "life-account" (*xingzhuang*), the basis for requesting a title and writing a biography.
- Ming–Qing the custom of attaching a mourning announcement to the obituary spread.
- Since the Republic the memorial-meeting system was set, and the memorial address became a common form.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Write**: the family or funeral home writes the memorial address and mourning announcement.
2. **Review**: before the meeting, send to the family to verify facts and appellations.
3. **Silence**: the memorial meeting opens; all observe silence.
4. **Read**: the principal sacrificer or a unit representative reads the memorial address.
5. **Family acknowledges**: a family representative gives the acknowledgement speech.
6. **Archive**: keep the mourning announcement with the name register.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Cities**: the memorial address is often drafted by the unit or funeral service, uniform in format.
- **Countryside**: still mainly reads the elegy and performs the three-presentation rite.
- **Ethnic minorities**: mostly express grief through their own sacrificial words and mourning songs (e.g., the Zhuang "crying-funeral song", the Hani "burial tune"); record separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Documents**: memorial address, mourning announcement, life-account, elegy, mourning couplet.
- **Objects**: portrait, spirit tablet, dirge equipment, name register.

**Model example (memorial address)**:

```text
Relatives and friends, guests all:

  Today, with hearts full of grief,
  we are here to see off Mr/Ms ○○○.

  Mr/Ms ○○○ was born on ○ month ○ day, ○ year, in ○○,
  and passed away from illness at ○ hour ○ minute on ○ month ○ day, ○ year, aged ○○.
  All his/her life diligent and honest, sincere to others,
  dutiful to the family, kind to neighbours, an example to the younger.

  The person is gone, but the face remains.
  May Mr/Ms ○○○ go well on the way!
  We pray the family restrain grief and take care.

      ○○○○ year ○ month ○ day
```
