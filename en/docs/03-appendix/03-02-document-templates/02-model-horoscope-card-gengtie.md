# Model Horoscope Card (Gengtie)

## 1. Definition

The horoscope card (*gengtie*), also called the "eight-characters card" (*bazitie*), the "natal card" (*niangengtie*) or the "dragon-phoenix card", writes the couple's birth horoscope (the sexagenary cycle of year, month, day and hour) for matching. The groom's family issues the "male card" (*nangeng*, also *qianzao*); the bride's family the "female card" (*nügeng*, also *kunzao*). Once exchanged it is called "swapping cards" (*huantie*), and the match has its proof.

**Format**: red note, vertical columns from the right; at top the native place, name and birth order; in the middle the four pillars of the birth moment; below the year-month-day and the signature.

## 2. Historical Origins

- The *Yili* "Ceremony of a Scholar's Wedding" has "asking the name" — asking the woman's name and birth time to take home and divine good or ill — the source of the horoscope card.
- Tang-dynasty Dunhuang letter-manuals already show the "marriage letter" and the natal format.
- From the Song and Yuan, "swapping the horoscope card" and "matching the eight characters" became a step before betrothal.
- In the Ming and Qing, woodblock-printed dragon-phoenix card forms spread with the encyclopaedias, and the format unified.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Groom's family makes the card**: the groom's family first writes the male card and places it in the card box.
2. **Go-between delivers**: ask the matchmaker to carry it to the bride's family.
3. **Bride's family replies**: the bride's family writes the female card in reply.
4. **Match the horoscopes**: ask a calendar-master (fortune-teller) to calculate the mutual production and restriction, the fitting and the taboo, of the two horoscopes.
5. **On a good omen, fix the match**: if the divination is auspicious, "swap the cards" to fix the betrothal.
6. **On ill omen, return the card**: if the divination is ill, politely return it and discuss another match.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **North China**: the card mostly writes the four pillars, with native place and three generations appended.
- **Jiangnan**: the card adds dragon-phoenix and eight-trigram patterns; a paired double card is valued.
- **Fujian, Guangdong**: writes "great fortune" and "a match made in heaven" at the head of the card.
- **Ethnic minorities**: mostly do not use the horoscope card, but fix marriage by divination, song-contest or an intra-group rite; record separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Horoscope card**: red note, dragon-phoenix pattern, gold dust; kept in a card box.
- **Matching documents**: marriage contract, gift list.
- **Objects**: card box, brush and ink.

**Model example**:

```text
Male  ○ year ○ month ○ day ○ hour  born
Female ○ year ○ month ○ day ○ hour  born
```
