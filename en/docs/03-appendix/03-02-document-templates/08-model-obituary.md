# Model Obituary

## 1. Definition

The obituary (*fugao*), also "death notice" (*fuwen*) or "death card" (*futie*), is the document telling relatives and friends of the death. It contains the deceased's name, status, the year-month-day-hour of birth and death, the age attained, the mourning schedule (encoffining, receiving condolences, funeral, burial) and the bereaved's signatures. The signatures follow the mourning grade, using self-references like "orphan son", "mourning son", "orphan-and-mourning son".

| Self-reference | When |
| --- | --- |
| **Orphan son** (*guzi*) | father dead, mother living |
| **Mourning son** (*aizi*) | mother dead, father living |
| **Orphan-and-mourning son** (*gu'aizi*) | both parents dead |

## 2. Historical Origins

- The *Liji* "Tan Gong" records the rite of "announcing death to the lord" and "to the village" — the notice must be timely and orderly.
- From Han–Wei, death was announced mostly by word of mouth, with bamboo slips as well.
- Ming–Qing obituary format settled: white paper in vertical script, the character "obituary" (*fu*) at the head, the signatures strict.
- After the Republic, the "mourning announcement" and "life-account" were added; today there are electronic obituaries (see "Modern Electronic Obituaries and Memorial Messages").

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Fix the chief mourner**: set the chief mourner, the hostess and the funeral team.
2. **Make the obituary**: write the notice, marking the name, birth-death, age attained, the mourning schedule.
3. **Sign**: the filial son, filial daughter, filial grandson sign by mourning grade and by age.
4. **Deliver**: send separately to relatives, friends, neighbours and the work unit.
5. **Post**: post at the house gate or a public place.
6. **Acknowledge**: after the rites, send a thank-you card (see "Model Thank-You Card and Letter of Acknowledgement").

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **North China**: the notice is brief, valuing word-of-mouth notice.
- **Jiangnan**: the notice details the life-account, with a mourning announcement attached.
- **Fujian, Guangdong**: white paper, black characters, valuing the calligraphy of the "obituary" character.
- **Ethnic minorities**: mostly announce by gunshot, horn, drum or a special person; paper obituaries are a later arrival; record separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Documents**: obituary, mourning announcement, life-account, thank-you card.
- **Objects**: white paper, plain envelope, obituary board.

**Model example**:

```text
                    OBITUARY

  The late father, the honoured ○○, styled ○○, by illness medicine failed,
  on ○ year ○ month ○ day at ○ hour ○ minute passed away peacefully in his bed,
  at the age of ○○ years.
  We have chosen ○ month ○ day at ○ hour to encoffin,
  ○ month ○ day at ○ hour to receive condolences,
  ○ month ○ day at ○ hour to bear to the grave and bury at ○○.

  Respectfully we announce this.

                The orphan son ○○ in tears respectfully declares
                The filial daughter ○○
                The filial grandson ○○

                      ○ year ○ month ○ day
```
