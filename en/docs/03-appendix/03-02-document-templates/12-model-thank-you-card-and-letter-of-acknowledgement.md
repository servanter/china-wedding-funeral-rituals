# Model Thank-You Card and Letter of Acknowledgement

## 1. Definition

The **thank-you card** (*xietie*) is the card sent after the funeral (or wedding) to thank those who came to the feast and ceremony, for those who attended in person to condole and sent funeral gifts. The **letter of acknowledgement** (*daxieqi*) is the written thanks, more detailed than the card, used mostly on formal occasions or for a group's thanks.

**Format**: the thank-you card is white paper (mourning) or red (joy), vertical from the right; first the matter, then the thanks, ending with the name and date. The phrasing is mostly "respectfully received" and "respectfully we declare our thanks".

## 2. Historical Origins

- Han–Wei letters already show the "thanks announcement" form, for thanking a gift or a condolence visit.
- Ming–Qing funerals had the "thanking for filial piety" custom: the filial son visits to thank and leaves a thank-you card.
- Daily-use encyclopaedias carried many thank-you-card models, and the format settled.
- Today the thank-you card fades; mostly oral or WeChat thanks replace it.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Register**: register the mourners' names and the funeral gifts.
2. **Make the card**: write the thank-you card or letter of acknowledgement.
3. **Send by list**: send by the registered list.
4. **Visit to thank**: the filial son visits in person to thank.
5. **Thanks feast**: after the rites, hold a "thanks feast" for those who helped.
6. **Archive**: keep with the favour book, as a record for future exchange.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **North China**: values the personal visit to thank; the card is brief.
- **Jiangnan**: the card is refined, often with the mourning announcement attached.
- **Fujian, Guangdong**: after the funeral, "thank the filial piety" — a red card thanks the mourners.
- **Ethnic minorities**: mostly oral thanks or thanks with wine and tea; paper cards are a later arrival; record separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Documents**: thank-you card, letter of acknowledgement, favour book, obituary.
- **Objects**: white or red paper, plain envelope, brush, seal.

**Model example (thank-you card, mourning)**:

```text
                THANK-YOU CARD

  On the death of the late father, the honoured ○○,
  you came in person to condole, and gave a generous gift;
  our grief is extreme; respectfully we declare our thanks.

                  The orphan son ○○ in tears thanks

                      ○ year ○ month ○ day
```

**Model example (letter of acknowledgement)**:

```text
  Our father unhappily departed our care; we are honoured that all you relatives and friends
  came in person to condole, gave generous gifts, and offered comfort;
  beyond grief, we are endlessly grateful. Respectfully by this letter we declare our thanks,
  and pray for your seasonal good fortune.

                              ○○ respectfully declares
```
