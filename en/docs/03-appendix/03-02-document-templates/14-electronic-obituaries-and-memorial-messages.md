# Modern Electronic Obituaries and Memorial Messages

## 1. Definition

The **electronic obituary** is the death notice issued through WeChat groups, Moments, SMS or email; the **memorial message** is the short online text of grief and remembrance, or for an online memorial meeting or memorial page. Its traits are fast spread and wide reach, but one must be extra careful with accurate appellations, correct facts, and the deceased's privacy and the family's wishes.

**Writing principles**: first, publish only with the family's consent; second, verify time, place and appellation; third, do not attach sensitive content like photos of the deceased in illness; fourth, keep the wording solemn — no emojis or frivolous language.

## 2. Historical Origins

- The Republic already saw death announced by telegram — the embryo of electronic notice.
- The late 20th century: mostly one-by-one phone notice.
- The 2000s: SMS and QQ-group notice grew.
- After the 2010s: WeChat and WeChat groups became the main vehicle for notice and remembrance; paper obituaries still run in parallel at formal occasions.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Family agrees**: fix the publisher, the reach and the wording; avoid publishing ahead of the family.
2. **Draft and verify**: confirm the deceased's name, birth-death time, the farewell ceremony's time and place.
3. **Publish**: post in the family group, unit group or Moments, noting the contact person.
4. **Tally replies**: register those who grieve and will come to condole.
5. **Send thanks**: afterward, send a unified electronic thank-you or card.
6. **Archive**: keep the electronic document with the favour record.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Cities**: electronic obituaries are current; paper obituaries are mostly for formal rites.
- **Countryside**: still mainly door-to-door notice and posted obituaries, with group announcement as supplement.
- **Generational difference**: the elder value word-of-mouth and door-to-door; the younger are used to group notices.
- **Ethnic minorities**: mostly inform by their own traditional means (e.g., gunshot, horn); electronic obituary is supplementary; record separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: phone, WeChat group, Moments, online memorial page.
- **Documents**: electronic obituary, memorial message, electronic couplet, electronic thank-you card, farewell-ceremony notice.

**Model example (WeChat-group obituary)**:

```text
[Obituary]
  Our father, Mr ○○○, by illness medicine failed,
  passed away at ○ hour ○ minute on ○ month ○ day, ○○○○ year, aged ○○.
  We respectfully set the farewell ceremony at ○○ hall of the ○○ funeral home,
  ○ hour ○ minute (week ○) on ○ month ○ day.
  Respectfully we announce this, grievingly telling relatives and friends.
  Contact: ○○○ (phone: ○○○○)
                                  The family ○○○ respectfully declares
```

**Model example (online memorial message)**:

```text
Respected relatives and friends:

  Our father ○○○ passed away peacefully on ○ month ○ day.
  As the rites are kept simple, we inform you online; please understand.
  The bits of a life, kept in the heart; your care, endlessly grateful.
  May the dead rest, the living treasure themselves.

                                          ○○○ in tears declares
```
