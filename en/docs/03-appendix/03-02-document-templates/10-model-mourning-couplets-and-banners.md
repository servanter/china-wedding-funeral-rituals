# Model Mourning Couplets and Banners

## 1. Definition

The **mourning couplet** (*wanlian*) is a funeral couplet for the dead, written on white paper or plain silk, in two lines — the right is the upper line, the left the lower. The **mourning banner** (*wanzhang*, also "mourning scroll") is a funeral phrase written on a whole length of silk, mostly four characters or a single phrase, hung on the two sides of the mourning hall. Both follow the tone and parallelism rules of the couplet.

**Rules**: the two lines have equal characters, matching parts of speech, and harmonious tones — the upper line ends on a departing tone, the lower on a level tone; the appellation follows the relation and mourning grade.

## 2. Historical Origins

- The mourning couplet arose in the Song; Su Shi and Lu You are said to have written some.
- The Ming saw the great rise of couplets, and the mourning couplet became a custom, valuing "fitting the person, the matter, the feeling".
- The Qing mourning couplet peaked; Liang Zhangju's *Couples Talk* specially carries mourning couplets.
- The mourning banner spread with the silk trade; after the Republic it is used with wreaths.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Compose**: compose by the deceased's status and relation, minding tone and parallelism.
2. **Write**: on white paper or plain silk, mostly regular script; the upper and lower lines must not be reversed.
3. **Sign**: at the end of the couplet or the right of the banner, the sender's name and appellation.
4. **Hang**: on the two sides of the mourning hall by closeness; the banner in the centre or to the sides.
5. **Dispose**: after the rites, burn, or the family keeps.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **North China**: the couplets turn vernacular, valuing plain grief.
- **Jiangnan**: fine parallelism, dense allusion.
- **Fujian, Guangdong**: the banner uses four-character auspicious phrases (e.g., "virtue matches Meng's mother").
- **Ethnic minorities**: mostly song in place of couplet — drum words, mourning songs, recitative; record separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Documents**: mourning couplet, mourning banner, elegy, obituary.
- **Objects**: white paper, plain silk, brush, paste, wreath.

**Model example**:

```text
[For a father]
  Upper: The tree would be still, but the wind does not cease
  Lower: The child would serve, but the parent waits no more

[For a mother]
  Upper: The kind mother gone, no shadow to be seen
  Lower: A thousand calls, the child cannot call her back

[For a teacher]
  Upper: Tireless in teaching, a model of the master
  Lower: The grace of teaching endures, opening the later generation

[For a friend]
  Upper: A life's friendship like hand and foot
  Lower: Half a life's confidant, apart by yin and yang

[Banner (four characters)]
  Virtue shines forever   Face remains
  Star sinks in the boudoir (for a woman)   Grief for a good teacher
```
