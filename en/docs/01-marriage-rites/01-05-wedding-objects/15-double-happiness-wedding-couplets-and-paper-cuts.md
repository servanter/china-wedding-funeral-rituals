# Double Happiness, Wedding Couplets and Paper Cuts

## 1. Definition

Wedding decoration is dominated by **the character “happiness”, wedding couplets and paper-cut window flowers**, producing the visual atmosphere of “red and festive”.

**Principal forms**:
- **“囍” (double happiness)** — the character *xi* written double, pasted on doors, windows, walls and objects; the most typical written symbol of a wedding (popularly connected with the story of Wang Anshi and “double happiness arriving at the door”, which is legend).
- **Wedding couplets** — posted on either side of the door and hall, as “a hundred years of harmony, five generations of flourishing”, “dragons and phoenixes bringing prosperity, mandarin ducks wing to wing”.
- **Paper-cut window flowers** — patterns of dragons and phoenixes, mandarin ducks, pomegranate, grape, lotus and the chubby child.
- **Others** — red silk hangings, red lanterns, congratulatory banners.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Song**: records of the character “囍” and of wedding decoration increase.
- **Ming and Qing**: wedding couplets and paper-cut window flowers became general, and paper-cutting formed regional schools (as with Yu county in Hebei and Shaanxi paper-cutting).
- **Modern times**: printed matter replaced hand-cut paper, and cities mostly used printed happiness characters.
- **Today**: paper characters, balloon arches and LED lights are used together; paper-cutting is protected as intangible heritage.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Preparing the characters and couplets**: someone is asked to write the couplets and cut the window flowers.
2. **Posting**: on the day before the wedding the happiness characters, couplets and window flowers are posted, and red silk and lanterns hung.
3. **Decorating the bridal chamber**: small happiness characters are pasted on the bed, cupboards, mirror and door handles.
4. **Keeping them**: the characters are left in place for a time (in some regions until the full month or after the New Year).
5. **Removal**: tearing them off at random is avoided (in some regions they are left until they fade).

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **North China**: happiness characters cover doors, windows and the courtyard, with window flowers and red lanterns.
- **Jiangnan**: wedding couplets insist on calligraphy, and the paper-cut patterns are fine.
- **Fujian and Taiwan**: happiness characters are used together with “the eight immortals banner” and coloured silk.
- **Ethnic minorities**: some peoples decorate weddings with embroidery, brocade and coloured ribbons; these must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Decorations**: the double-happiness character, wedding couplets, paper-cut window flowers, red lanterns, coloured silk, congratulatory banners.
- **Related documents**: the text of the wedding couplets, the inscriptions on the banners.
