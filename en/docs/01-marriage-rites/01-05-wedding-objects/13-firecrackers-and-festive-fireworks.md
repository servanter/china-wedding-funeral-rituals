# Firecrackers and Festive Fireworks

## 1. Definition

Firecrackers and fireworks are **the sound-makers by which a wedding drives off evil, announces the good news and raises the occasion**. Every key point of the wedding has its firing of crackers, whence the name “**festive crackers**”.

**Principal points**:
- **Sending out the chair / setting out for the bride** — the “chair-sending crackers”.
- **Reaching the bride’s gate** — the “gate-arrival crackers”.
- **Raising the chair for the return** — the “chair-raising crackers”.
- **Reaching the groom’s gate** — the “reception crackers”.
- **Bowing in the hall, entering the bridal chamber** — the “bowing crackers” and the “chamber crackers”.
- **Opening the banquet and seeing off the guests** — the “banquet-opening crackers” and the “farewell crackers”.

**Rules**: strung together, in even numbers, and continuous (“not broken off”); stopping midway is avoided, and “duds” are avoided.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Tang and Song**: firecrackers (burning bamboo) were used at the year’s end and at celebrations; with the spread of gunpowder they became the “cracker”.
- **Ming and Qing**: firing crackers at weddings became general, commonly recorded in the gazetteers.
- **Modern times**: cracker manufacture became industrial; cities gradually restricted or banned their use.
- **Today**: most cities ban or restrict them, and weddings use electronic crackers and firework tubes instead; the countryside still fires them widely.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Preparing the crackers**: strings of crackers and fireworks (in pairs) are prepared.
2. **Firing on the chair’s departure**: fired as the party sets out for the bride.
3. **Firing on arrival**: fired at the bride’s gate and the groom’s gate.
4. **Firing at the bowing**: fired during the ceremony to raise the occasion.
5. **Firing at farewell**: the “farewell crackers” are fired as the banquet ends and guests leave.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **North China**: large quantities of crackers, insisting on “one string to the end”.
- **Jiangnan**: small crackers and “firework tubes” are common, and cities are mostly bound by restrictions on firing.
- **Fujian and Taiwan**: “crackers” and “sky-soaring crackers” are used together, the reception array being lively.
- **Ethnic minorities**: some peoples fire guns, blow horns or beat drums instead of crackers; these must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: crackers, firework tubes, electronic crackers (the contemporary substitute).
- **Related documents**: the wedding card, the list of insignia.
- **Local regulation**: contemporary regulations on the setting off of fireworks and crackers.
