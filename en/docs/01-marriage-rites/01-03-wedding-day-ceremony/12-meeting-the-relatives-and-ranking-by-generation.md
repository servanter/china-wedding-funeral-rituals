# Meeting the Relatives and Ranking by Generation

## 1. Definition

“Meeting the relatives” (acknowledging kin) is the rite in which the new wife, after the wedding, greets her husband’s kin one by one; “ranking by generation” is the process, within those greetings, of establishing the order of generations and changing her forms of address.

The key act is the **change of address**: the new wife ceases to be “Miss so-and-so” and addresses her husband’s kin as “father, mother, uncle, aunt, elder brother, sister-in-law”. Once the form of address changes, the social relation is formally established. The person greeted must give a “gift of meeting” (the money of the changed address) as the consideration for confirming the relation.

## 2. Historical Origins

- The *Shihunli* has the rites of “presentation to the parents-in-law” and “presentation to the paternal uncles and elder brothers”, the ritual basis of the new wife’s greeting of the husband’s elders one by one.
- Zhu Xi’s *Family Rituals*, after “presentation to the parents-in-law”, lists “presentation to the wife’s various kin”, laying down the order of the greetings.
- In the Ming and Qing popular practice these became “ranking by generation” and “acknowledging kin”, an important stage on the day after the wedding.
- The name “ranking by generation” is connected with the lineage’s system of generations: on entering, the new wife must fix her place in the lineage (whose daughter-in-law, whose aunt by marriage), so that the rite is in effect **the registration of her lineage identity**.
- Contemporary weddings generally accomplish it at the banquet by “offering tea and changing address”, simplified in form but retaining the function.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Preparing the gifts**: the new wife prepares incense, candles, tea and fruit for the greetings.
2. **Setting the order**: the greetings are ordered by generation and closeness — usually the direct elders first (grandparents, parents), then the collateral seniors, then the same generation.
3. **Bowing**: she kneels (or bows) to the person and offers tea (“offering tea”).
4. **Changing address**: she addresses the person by the husband’s family term, and not again by the old one.
5. **Gifts given**: the person greeted gives the “gift of meeting” or the “money of the changed address” (red packet, jewellery).
6. **Return gifts**: the new wife offers the return gifts she has prepared (kerchiefs, shoes and socks).
7. **Registration**: an elder of the lineage enters the new wife into the roll of lineage members (some lineages keeping a register).

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **North China**: called “ranking by generation”, insisting that “for three days there is no senior and junior, and on the fourth the ranks are set” — that is, the “no senior or junior” of the roughhousing ends and the generations are formally established.
- **Jiangsu and Zhejiang**: called “meeting the kin” or “meeting the generations”, with “offering tea and changing address”.
- **Fujian and Guangdong**: called “acknowledging kin”, usually after the bowing to the ancestors, a solemn ceremony in which the elders give gold ornaments.
- **Taiwan**: called “acknowledging kin”, often combined with “bowing to the ancestors”.
- **Ethnic minorities**: some peoples have specific rites of acknowledging kin, such as the Yi “wine of acknowledging kin” and the Miao “song of acknowledging kin”. Their systems of kinship terms differ from the Han (as with the special terms under systems of cross-cousin marriage), and the manner of changing address differs with them, and must be understood in the light of each kinship system.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Tea set, incense and candles**: the implements of offering tea and acknowledging kin.
- **The gift of meeting (red packet, jewellery)**: given by the elders.
- **Return gifts (kerchiefs, shoes and socks, sweets)**: offered by the new wife.
- **The list of kin**: some lineages keep a “list of kin” for the new wife to identify and address them.
- **The lineage genealogy**: the new wife may be entered once she has joined the lineage.
