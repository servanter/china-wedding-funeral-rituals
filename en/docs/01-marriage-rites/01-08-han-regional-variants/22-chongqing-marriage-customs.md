# Chongqing Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

Chongqing marriage custom belongs to the **Ba-Yu type**; the “bridal lament”, “sitting up the night” and the lively banquet are its markers. Its principal features:

1. **“Proposal”, “matching the eight characters”, “betrothal”** — the negotiation is clear.
2. **“Bridal lament”** — girls lament before marriage, telling of parting.
3. **“Sitting up the night”** — kin and friends keep the bride company and sing the night before.
4. **“Reception, bowing in the hall, roughhousing”** — the sequence is complete, and the roughhousing is lively.
5. **“Return visit”** — the third-day homecoming.
6. **“Courtyard banquet”** — the wedding banquet is large, laid out in the courtyard or on the threshing ground.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Pre-Qin**: the folkways of Ba are linked to the tradition of the soul-summoning songs of the *Chuci*.
- **Ming and Qing**: the migration of “Huguang filling Sichuan” brought the wedding customs of Hubei, Hunan and Jiangxi, fusing them with the local.
- **Today**: hotel weddings and village courtyard banquets go side by side.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Proposal and matching the eight characters.
2. Betrothal (exchange of cards, sending the gift).
3. Bridal lament and sitting up the night (before the wedding).
4. Reception: bridal chair and band; bowing in the hall, sitting in the curtain, roughhousing.
5. Return visit.
6. Meeting the kin.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **The main city and near suburbs**: city weddings tend to be simple.
- **Western Chongqing (Jiangjin, Yongchuan)**: close to southern Sichuan, with similar customs.
- **North-eastern Chongqing (Wanzhou, Fengjie)**: close to Hubei, with the influence of Chu custom.
- **South-eastern Chongqing (Qianjiang, Xiushan)**: a Tujia and Miao area, where Han custom is influenced.
- **The various peoples**: the Tujia and Miao marriage customs form their own systems and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: bridal chair, dragon-and-phoenix candles, veil, dowry chests.
- **Documents**: the horoscope card, gift list, invitations, marriage letter.
- **Song-books**: manuscripts of bridal-lament songs.
