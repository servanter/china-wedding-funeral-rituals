# Guangxi Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Han Chinese of Guangxi are mainly distributed in **eastern and south-eastern Guangxi (Yulin, Wuzhou, Guigang) and northern Guangxi (Guilin, Liuzhou)**; their marriage custom shows the **triple influence of the Cantonese, Hakka and Hunan-dialect areas**. Its principal features:

1. **The vestige of the “three letters and six rites”** — the south-east is close to Cantonese usage, with a fuller sequence.
2. **“Sending the gift”, “reception”, “return visit”** — the basic sequence is complete.
3. **“Coming of age”** — in the east there is the custom of coming of age (close to Guangdong).
4. **“Bridal lament”** (some regions) — girls lament before marriage.
5. **The influence of multi-ethnic cohabitation** — Han custom and the Zhuang, Yao and Dong customs permeate one another.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **From the Tang and Song onward**: migrants from the central plains and from Guangdong and Hunan entered Guangxi in succession, forming Han settled areas.
- **Ming and Qing**: the lineage system of eastern Guangxi was developed, and the gazetteers record wedding ceremonies in detail; northern Guangxi is close to Hunan.
- **Today**: city weddings predominate; the countryside keeps the old rites.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Proposal and matching the marriage.
2. Betrothal (exchange of cards, the minor gift).
3. Sending the gift (the great engagement).
4. Reception: bridal chair and band; bowing in the hall and sitting in the curtain.
5. Return visit: the third-day homecoming.
6. Meeting the kin: the affines meet.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **South-eastern Guangxi (Yulin, Wuzhou, Guigang)**: close to Cantonese usage, with elaborate observances.
- **Northern Guangxi (Guilin, Liuzhou)**: close to Hunan, with the influence of Hunan custom.
- **Southern Guangxi (Nanning, Qinzhou, Beihai)**: both Cantonese and local colouring.
- **Western Guangxi (Baise, Hechi)**: fewer Han people, mostly living among the Zhuang and Yao.
- **The various peoples**: the Zhuang, Yao, Dong, Miao, Mulao and Maonan marriage customs form their own systems and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: bridal chair, dragon-and-phoenix candles, veil, dowry chests, the implements of coming of age.
- **Documents**: the horoscope card, gift list, marriage letter, genealogy.
