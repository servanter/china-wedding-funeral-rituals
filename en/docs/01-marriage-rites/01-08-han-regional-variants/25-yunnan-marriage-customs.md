# Yunnan Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Han Chinese of Yunnan are mostly **descendants of early-Ming garrison and reclamation migrants**; their marriage custom preserves the surviving Jiangnan and Jiangxi usage of the Ming, and blends with Yunnan’s multi-ethnic environment. Its principal features:

1. **The vestige of the “Six Rites”** — proposal, betrothal, sending the gift and reception are clear.
2. **“Dongjing society” music** (around Dali and Kunming) — at the wedding a Dongjing society is invited to play (with both Confucian and Daoist colouring).
3. **“Bridal lament”** — girls in some regions lament before marriage.
4. **“Reception, bowing in the hall, sitting in the curtain”** — the sequence is complete.
5. **“Return visit”** — the third-day homecoming.
6. **“Six harmonies and one spring” banquets** — the wedding banquet is large, with elaborate hospitality.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Early Ming**: the great army entered Yunnan and opened land for settlement; Jiangnan, Jiangxi and Huguang migrants settled, bringing their native wedding custom.
- **Ming and Qing**: the gazetteers (the Yunnan general gazetteer and the prefectural, county and department gazetteers) record wedding ceremonies in detail; the Dongjing society played music at both weddings and funerals.
- **Today**: city weddings predominate; the countryside keeps the old rites and the Dongjing music tradition.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Proposal and matching the marriage.
2. Betrothal (exchange of cards, sending the gift).
3. Bridal lament (before the wedding).
4. Reception: bridal chair and band (or Dongjing music); bowing in the hall and sitting in the curtain.
5. Return visit.
6. Meeting the kin.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Central Yunnan (Kunming, Yuxi)**: Dongjing music and traditional custom go side by side.
- **North-eastern Yunnan (Zhaotong, Qujing)**: close to Guizhou and Sichuan, with similar customs.
- **Western Yunnan (Dali, Baoshan)**: a multi-ethnic area, where Han custom is influenced by the Bai, Yi and Hui.
- **Southern Yunnan (Honghe, Wenshan)**: with the influence of Zhuang, Miao and Yao custom.
- **The various peoples**: the Bai, Yi, Hani, Dai, Miao and Zhuang marriage customs form their own systems and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: bridal chair, dragon-and-phoenix candles, veil, dowry chests, Dongjing instruments.
- **Documents**: the horoscope card, gift list, marriage letter, genealogy.
