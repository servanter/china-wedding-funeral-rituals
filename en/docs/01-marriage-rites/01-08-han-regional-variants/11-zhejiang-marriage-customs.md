# Zhejiang Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

Zhejiang marriage custom is marked by **the complete vestiges of the “three letters and six rites” and a high degree of lineage participation**. Its principal features:

1. **“Three letters and six rites”** — around Ningbo and Shaoxing attention is paid to the “betrothal letter, gift letter and reception letter” and the sequence of the Six Rites.
2. **“Betrothal”, “nacai”, “reception”** — the stages distinct, the gift list detailed.
3. **“Paying the morning visit”** — on the third day after the wedding the new wife enters the kitchen (the “morning visit”), showing her capacity to run a household.
4. **“Return visit”** — the third-day homecoming.
5. **Lineage and ancestral hall** — in eastern Zhejiang the lineage presides over weddings, and the lineage rules constrain the scale.
6. **“Ten *li* of red dowry”** — in the Ningbo-Shaoxing region the dowry is lavish (the “ten *li* of red dowry” being a marker of the local custom).

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Song and Ming**: Neo-Confucianism and the lineage system flourished in eastern Zhejiang, and weddings followed the *Family Rituals*.
- **Qing**: the “Customs” sections of the gazetteers record “three letters and six rites” and the “ten *li* of red dowry” in detail.
- **Modern times**: under the influence of the Ningbo and Wenzhou merchant groups, weddings insisted on display.
- **Today**: hotel weddings predominate; the “ten *li* of red dowry” has become an item of intangible heritage and wedding display.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Negotiation**: the matchmaker proposes, the marriage is matched, the cards exchanged.
2. **Betrothal (nacai)**: the betrothal gift and gift letter.
3. **Sending the dowry**: the trousseau is sent before the wedding (on a large scale in the Ningbo-Shaoxing region).
4. **Reception**: bridal chair and band; bowing in the hall and sitting in the curtain.
5. **The morning visit**: the new wife enters the kitchen three days after the wedding.
6. **Return visit**: the homecoming.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Eastern Zhejiang (Ningbo, Shaoxing)**: strong lineage colouring; “three letters and six rites” and the “ten *li* of red dowry” typical.
- **Southern Zhejiang (Wenzhou, Taizhou)**: weddings insist on display, the banquets large.
- **Northern Zhejiang (Hangzhou, Jiaxing, Huzhou)**: close to southern Jiangsu custom, with elaborate propriety.
- **Western Zhejiang (Quzhou, Lishui)**: mountain custom, mostly following village usage.
- **The various peoples**: the She marriage custom of Zhejiang forms its own system and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: bridal chair, dragon-and-phoenix candles, veil, wedding steelyard, dowry chests (the objects of the “red dowry”).
- **Documents**: the three letters (betrothal, gift and reception letters), gift lists, marriage letter.
