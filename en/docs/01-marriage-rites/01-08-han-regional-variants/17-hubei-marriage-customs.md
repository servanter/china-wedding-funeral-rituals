# Hubei Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

Hubei marriage custom combines **the ancient rites of the central plains with the folkways of the Chu lands**; “bridal lament” and “sitting up the night” are its local markers. Its principal features:

1. **“Matching the eight characters”, “exchange of cards”, “betrothal”** — the negotiation is clear.
2. **“Bridal lament”** — girls lament before marriage (in western and eastern Hubei alike), weeping in song of parting.
3. **“Keeping the ten sisters company” and “sitting up the night”** — girl friends keep the bride company and sing in antiphon before the wedding (especially in Tujia-influenced western Hubei).
4. **“Reception, bowing in the hall, roughhousing”** — the sequence is complete.
5. **“Return visit”** — the third-day homecoming.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Pre-Qin**: the shamanistic air and song-custom of Chu (the *Chuci*) are the distant source of the song-custom of “bridal lament” and “sitting up the night”.
- **Ming and Qing**: the “Customs” sections of the gazetteers record wedding ceremonies and song-customs.
- **Today**: city weddings predominate; the bridal lament survives in artistic and intangible-heritage contexts.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Proposal and matching the eight characters.
2. Exchange of cards and betrothal.
3. Sending the gift (the betrothal gift).
4. Bridal lament and keeping the ten sisters company (before the wedding).
5. Reception: bridal chair and band; bowing in the hall, sitting in the curtain, roughhousing.
6. Return visit.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Eastern Hubei (Huanggang, Huangshi)**: the customs of lament and sitting-up-the-night songs flourish.
- **Central Hubei (Wuhan, Xiaogan, Jingzhou)**: both ancient rite and Chu custom.
- **North-western Hubei (Xiangyang, Shiyan)**: close to Shaanxi and Henan, with similar observances.
- **South-western Hubei (Enshi, Yichang)**: a Tujia area, where Han custom is influenced by “keeping the ten sisters company” and the like.
- **The various peoples**: the Tujia, Miao and other marriage customs form their own systems and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: bridal chair, dragon-and-phoenix candles, veil, dowry chests.
- **Documents**: the horoscope card, gift list, invitations, marriage letter.
- **Song-books**: manuscripts of bridal-lament songs and dowry songs.
