# Inner Mongolia Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Han Chinese of Inner Mongolia are mostly **migrants of the Qing “Western Passage” and their descendants** (chiefly from Shanxi and Shaanxi); their marriage custom bears a clear **Shanxi-Shaanxi base**, and because of the grassland environment and Mongol-Han cohabitation it tends towards simplicity. Its principal features:

1. **A Shanxi-Shaanxi base** — “exchanging cards”, “the presentation” and the “mother-parting meat” resemble those of Shanxi and Shaanxi.
2. **Simplified and practical** — with a sparse population over a wide area, insignia and bridal chairs were largely dispensed with, and ox-carts and horse-carts used for the reception (in the early period).
3. **Mongol-Han fusion** — some Han weddings were influenced by Mongol custom (a whole sheep as gift; milk tea and dairy foods served to guests).
4. **“Building the awning for guests”** — an awning is put up in the courtyard for the banquet, which is large in scale.
5. **“Return visit”** — the third-day return, without elaborate propriety.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Qing**: with the opening of land beyond the passes, migrants from Shanxi and Shaanxi “went out by the Western Passage” and formed Han settlements around Guihua city, Baotou and the Hetao.
- **Republican period**: migration continued, and village customs differed according to the migrants’ home prefectures.
- **Today**: city weddings combine with Inner Mongolian local character, while the countryside keeps the customs of the presentation and the return visit.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Proposal and matching**: the matchmaker proposes and the eight characters and animals are asked.
2. **Exchange of cards**: the horoscope cards are exchanged and the engagement made.
3. **The presentation**: the betrothal gift is sent (a four-colour gift, a whole sheep or meat).
4. **Reception**: by cart or bridal chair; bowing in the hall and sitting in the curtain.
5. **Feasting**: an awning is built and a banquet held, with Mongol and Han customs of hospitality used together.
6. **Return visit**: the third-day return.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **The Hetao and Guisui (Hohhot, Baotou)**: migrants from Shanxi and Shaanxi concentrated, Shanxi customs clear.
- **The east (Chifeng, Tongliao)**: close to Liaoning and Jilin, with a strong north-eastern type of custom.
- **The west (Alxa, Ordos)**: adjoining Ningxia and Shaanxi, with the influence of Hui and Han cohabitation.
- **The various peoples**: the traditional Mongolian wedding (proposal, engagement, fetching the bride, sending the bride) and Hui marriage custom form their own systems and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: bridal chair or cart, dragon-and-phoenix candles, veil, dowry chests, the whole sheep (as gift).
- **Documents**: horoscope card, gift list, invitations, marriage letter.
