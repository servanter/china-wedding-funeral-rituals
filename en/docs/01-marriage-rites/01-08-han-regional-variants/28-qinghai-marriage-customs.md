# Qinghai Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Han Chinese of Qinghai are concentrated in **the Huangshui region (Xining, Haidong)**, mostly descendants of Ming-dynasty Jiangnan and Jiang-Huai migrants; their marriage custom preserves the surviving usage of the Ming homeland, and is influenced by the Tibetan, Hui, Tu and Salar. Its principal features:

1. **“Proposal — betrothal — sending the gift — fetching the bride”** — a clear sequence, close to the Ming Jiangnan practice.
2. **“Shehuo” and weddings** — the Huangshui “*shehuo*” folk custom and wedding celebrations both belong to village public life.
3. **“Sitting in the curtain” and “roughhousing”** — the sequence is complete.
4. **“Return visit”** — the third-day homecoming.
5. **The influence of multi-ethnic cohabitation** — banquet foodways are influenced by the Hui and Tibetans (for example milk tea and hand-grabbed meat).

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Ming dynasty**: in the Hongwu reign migrants were sent to garrison the frontier (Jiangnan and Jiang-Huai troops and people entering Qinghai), bringing their native wedding custom and the *shehuo* tradition.
- **Ming and Qing**: the “Customs” sections of the gazetteers (the Xining prefectural gazetteer and the county gazetteers) record wedding ceremonies.
- **Today**: city weddings predominate; the countryside keeps the old rites and the *shehuo* folk custom.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Proposal and matching the marriage.
2. Betrothal (exchange of cards).
3. Sending the gift (the betrothal gift, the gift list).
4. Fetching the bride: bridal chair and band; bowing in the hall, sitting in the curtain, roughhousing.
5. Return visit.
6. Meeting the kin.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Xining, Haidong (the Huangshui region)**: the Han are concentrated, with marked Ming-migrant custom.
- **Huangzhong, Datong**: adjacent to Tibetan villages, with Tibetan influence.
- **Haixi, Hainan**: fewer Han people, mostly living among Mongols and Tibetans.
- **The various peoples**: the Tibetan, Hui, Tu and Salar marriage customs form their own systems and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: bridal chair, dragon-and-phoenix candles, veil, dowry chests.
- **Documents**: the horoscope card, gift list, marriage letter, genealogy.
