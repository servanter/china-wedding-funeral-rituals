# Ningxia Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Han Chinese of Ningxia are mainly distributed in **the northern irrigated area (Yinchuan, Wuzhong, Zhongwei) and the southern mountains (Guyuan)**; their marriage custom is close to Gansu and Shaanxi, and shows traces of fusion from long cohabitation with the Hui. Its principal features:

1. **“Proposal — exchange of cards — betrothal — fetching the bride”** — a clear sequence.
2. **“The mother-parting meat”** — the groom’s family sends the “mother-parting meat”.
3. **“Sitting in the curtain” and “roughhousing”** — the sequence is complete.
4. **“Return visit”** — the third-day homecoming.
5. **Hui and Han customs intervisible** — banquet foodways and hospitality are influenced by the Hui (varying by village).

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Ming dynasty**: Ningxia was a key garrison of the nine frontier commands; military-colony migrants (from Jiang-Huai and Shaanxi) brought the wedding custom.
- **Qing dynasty**: the “Customs” sections of the gazetteers (the Ningxia prefectural gazetteer and the county gazetteers) record wedding ceremonies.
- **Today**: city weddings predominate; the countryside keeps the old rites.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Proposal and matching the marriage.
2. Exchange of cards and betrothal.
3. Sending the gift (the betrothal gift, the mother-parting meat, the gift list).
4. Fetching the bride: bridal chair and band; bowing in the hall, sitting in the curtain, roughhousing.
5. Return visit.
6. Meeting the kin.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **The northern irrigated area (Yinchuan, Wuzhong, Zhongwei)**: customs close to Gansu and Shaanxi.
- **The southern mountains (Guyuan, Xiji, Haiyuan)**: close to eastern Gansu, with plain customs.
- **The various peoples**: the Ningxia Hui marriage custom (the ahong officiates, the “Nikah” is recited) forms its own system and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: bridal chair, dragon-and-phoenix candles, veil, mother-parting meat, dowry chests.
- **Documents**: the horoscope card, gift list, marriage letter, genealogy.
