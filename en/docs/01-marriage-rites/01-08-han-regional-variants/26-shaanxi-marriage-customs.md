# Shaanxi Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

Shaanxi is the **homeland of the rites of the Zhou, Qin, Han and Tang**; the Guanzhong marriage custom is reckoned among the types that **preserve more of the ancient rite**, while northern and southern Shaanxi each inherit from their neighbours. Its principal features:

1. **“Proposal — exchange of cards — betrothal — fetching the bride”** — regular sequence, with a clear vestige of the “Six Rites”.
2. **“The mother-parting meat”** — the groom’s family sends the bride’s family the “mother-parting meat”.
3. **“Sitting in the curtain”** — entering the bridal chamber to “sit in the curtain”, presided over by a “whole person” (a woman of full fortune).
4. **“Dressing the bed” and “scattering the bed”** — dates, chestnuts and walnuts are scattered (in northern Shaanxi).
5. **“Return visit”** — the third-day homecoming.
6. **“Roughhousing”** — done for sport (in some regions the “teasing of the parents-in-law” is the fun).

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Zhou, Qin, Han and Tang**: the Guanzhong was the centre of the ritual system; the wedding recorded in the *Yili*’s “Gentleman’s Wedding Rites” left a deep impress here.
- **Ming and Qing**: the “Customs” sections of the gazetteers (the Shaanxi general gazetteer and the prefectural, county and department gazetteers) record wedding ceremonies in detail.
- **Today**: hotel weddings and village old-custom go side by side.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Proposal and matching the marriage.
2. Exchange of cards and betrothal.
3. Sending the gift (the betrothal gift, the mother-parting meat).
4. Dressing and scattering the bed (before the wedding).
5. Fetching the bride: bridal chair and band; bowing in the hall, sitting in the curtain, roughhousing.
6. Return visit.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Guanzhong (Xi’an, Xianyang, Baoji)**: more ancient rite preserved, regular observances.
- **Northern Shaanxi (Yulin, Yan’an)**: a cave-dwelling environment, plain customs, scattering walnuts and dates-and-chestnuts on the bed.
- **Southern Shaanxi (Hanzhong, Ankang, Shangluo)**: close to Sichuan and Chu, with the influence of Sichuan and Hubei custom.
- **The various peoples**: the Hui marriage custom of Shaanxi (Xi’an, Ankang and elsewhere) forms its own system and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: bridal chair, dragon-and-phoenix candles, veil, mother-parting meat, dowry chests.
- **Documents**: the horoscope card, gift list, invitations, marriage letter.
