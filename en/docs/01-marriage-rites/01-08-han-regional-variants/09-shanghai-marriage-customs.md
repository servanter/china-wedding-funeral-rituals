# Shanghai Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

Shanghai marriage custom is a superimposition of **local usage (Songjiang, Jiading, Pudong)** and **that of migrants (Ningbo, northern Jiangsu, Guangdong)**, shaped in modern times by urbanisation into a “**Shanghai school**” of wedding custom. Its principal features:

1. **“Taking the eight characters”, “betrothal”, “passing the door”** — the sequence from negotiation to reception is clear.
2. **“The third-day return”** — the return visit three days after the wedding.
3. **“Drinking the wedding wine”** — the wedding banquet is called “drinking the wedding wine”, with attention to display and propriety.
4. **The modern “civilised marriage”** — in the early twentieth century Shanghai was the first to see the new-style wedding (witness, exchange of rings, bowing).
5. **“Meeting the kin” and “acknowledging kin”** — the kin of both sides meet and the affinal relation is established.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Ming and Qing**: the marriage customs of Shanghai county and Songjiang prefecture are recorded in the gazetteers.
- **After the modern opening of the port**: native-place guilds arranged weddings for fellow provincials; newspaper matrimonial advertisements, civilised marriages and church weddings appeared together.
- **Today**: hotel weddings and wedding photography predominate; the old rites are revived in Chinese-style weddings.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Negotiation**: the matchmaker proposes, and the eight characters are taken and matched.
2. **Betrothal**: the minor and major betrothals (jewellery, betrothal gift).
3. **Passing the door (reception)**: fetched by bridal chair or wedding car; bowing in the hall, sitting in the curtain, roughhousing.
4. **Drinking the wedding wine**: a banquet for the guests.
5. **The third-day return**: the homecoming visit.
6. **Meeting the kin**: the kin of both sides meet and acknowledge one another.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **The urban districts**: Shanghai-school custom, the ceremonies simple, hotel weddings predominating.
- **Pudong, Songjiang, Jiading**: local custom better preserved.
- **The outlying counties (Qingpu, Jinshan)**: with the influence of Suzhou and Jiaxing usage.
- **The various peoples**: the Hui marriage custom of Shanghai (religious ceremony) forms its own system and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: bridal chair, dragon-and-phoenix candles, veil, wedding steelyard, dowry chests, wedding ring (modern).
- **Documents**: horoscope card, gift list, invitations, marriage letter, the certificate of the modern “civilised marriage”.
