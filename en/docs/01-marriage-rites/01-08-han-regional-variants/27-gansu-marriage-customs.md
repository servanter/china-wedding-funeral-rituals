# Gansu Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

Gansu is long and narrow in shape, and its Han marriage custom shows **clear east-west differences**: eastern Gansu is close to Shaanxi, southern Gansu to Sichuan, and the Hexi corridor has both migrant and oasis character. Its principal features:

1. **“Proposal — exchange of cards — betrothal — fetching the bride”** — a clear vestige of the “Six Rites”.
2. **“Bridal lament”** — girls in some regions lament before marriage.
3. **“Sitting in the curtain” and “roughhousing”** — the sequence is complete.
4. **“Return visit”** — the third-day homecoming.
5. **The influence of multi-ethnic culture** — living among the Hui, Tibetan and others, the customs affect one another (for example in banquet foodways).

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Han and Tang**: the Hexi corridor was the throat of the Silk Road, where Han-Tang rites and Buddhist observances met.
- **Ming and Qing**: the “Customs” sections of the gazetteers (the Gansu general gazetteer and the prefectural, county and department gazetteers) record wedding ceremonies.
- **Today**: city weddings predominate; the countryside keeps the old rites.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Proposal and matching the marriage.
2. Exchange of cards and betrothal.
3. Sending the gift (the betrothal gift, the gift list).
4. Bridal lament (before the wedding, in some regions).
5. Fetching the bride: bridal chair and band; bowing in the hall, sitting in the curtain, roughhousing.
6. Return visit.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Eastern Gansu (Qingyang, Pingliang)**: close to Shaanxi, with similar customs.
- **Central Gansu (Lanzhou, Dingxi)**: the customs of the river-valley farming region, moderate observances.
- **Southern Gansu (Tianshui, Longnan)**: close to Sichuan and Shaanxi, with a Sichuan-Shaanxi colouring.
- **Hexi (Wuwei, Zhangye, Jiuquan)**: oasis farming and migrant custom.
- **The various peoples**: the Hui, Tibetan, Dongxiang, Bonan, Yugur and Salar marriage customs form their own systems and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: bridal chair, dragon-and-phoenix candles, veil, dowry chests.
- **Documents**: the horoscope card, gift list, marriage letter, genealogy.
