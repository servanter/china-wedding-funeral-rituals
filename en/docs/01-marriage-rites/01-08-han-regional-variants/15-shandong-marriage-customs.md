# Shandong Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

Shandong is the **land of Confucius and Mencius**, and its marriage custom carries the ritual tradition most deeply: the sequence is regular, and through the “crossing the passes” migration it influenced the custom of the whole north-east. Its principal features:

1. **“Proposal — exchange of cards — betrothal — sending the date — fetching the bride”** — the stages are distinct.
2. **“The mother-parting meat”** — the groom’s family sends the bride’s family “mother-parting meat”, figuring the daughter leaving her mother.
3. **“Urging the trousseau”** — the urging gift is sent before the wedding.
4. **“Bowing in the hall, sitting in the curtain, roughhousing”** — the sequence is complete.
5. **“Return visit”** — the third-day return, with a banquet for the son-in-law at the bride’s family.
6. **A strong custom of “roughhousing”** — the chamber is roughhoused for sport (in some regions).

## 2. Historical Origins

- **From the pre-Qin onward**: the rites-learning of Qi and Lu flourished; the wedding as recorded in the *Yili* and the *Liji* left a deep impress on the people.
- **Ming and Qing**: the “Customs” sections of the gazetteers record wedding ceremonies in detail.
- **Modern times**: migrants carried Shandong custom into the north-east (see the entries for Liaoning, Jilin and Heilongjiang).
- **Today**: hotel weddings predominate; the countryside keeps the material customs of the mother-parting meat and the urging gift.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Proposal and matching the marriage.
2. Exchange of cards and betrothal.
3. Betrothal and sending the gift (the betrothal gift, the mother-parting meat).
4. Sending the date (fixing the wedding day).
5. Fetching the bride: bridal chair and band; bowing in the hall, sitting in the curtain, roughhousing.
6. Return visit.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Central Shandong (Jinan, Tai’an)**: regular and orderly observances.
- **Jiaodong (Yantai, Qingdao)**: coastal customs, with the character of fishing households.
- **South-western Shandong (Jining, Heze)**: close to Henan, insisting on the “exchange of cards”.
- **North-western Shandong (Dezhou, Liaocheng)**: close to Hebei, with similar customs.
- **The various peoples**: the Hui marriage custom of Shandong (Jinan, Linqing and elsewhere) forms its own system and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: bridal chair, gongs, drums and *suona*, dragon-and-phoenix candles, veil, mother-parting meat, urging gift, dowry chests.
- **Documents**: the horoscope card, gift list, invitations, marriage letter.
