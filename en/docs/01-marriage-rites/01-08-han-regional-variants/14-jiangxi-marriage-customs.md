# Jiangxi Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

Jiangxi marriage custom gives **equal weight to lineage and to ritual order**, and differs in appearance between central, southern and north-eastern Jiangxi. Its principal features:

1. **The vestiges of the “Six Rites”** — proposal, betrothal, sending the gift and reception are clear.
2. **“Bridal lament”** — in some mountain districts (western and southern Jiangxi) girls lament before marriage.
3. **Ancestral hall and lineage rules** — the wedding is presided over by the lineage, whose rules constrain its scale.
4. **“Return visit”** — the homecoming on the third day.
5. **“Roughhousing”** — the chamber is roughhoused on the wedding night.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Song and Ming**: Jiangxi was a stronghold of Neo-Confucianism, deeply influenced by the *Family Rituals*; the lineage system was developed.
- **Ming and Qing**: the “Customs” sections of the gazetteers record wedding ceremonies.
- **Today**: city weddings predominate; the countryside keeps the (now artistic) bridal lament and the homecoming custom.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Proposal and matching the marriage.
2. Betrothal (exchange of cards, the minor gift).
3. Sending the gift (the great engagement).
4. Reception: bridal chair and band; bowing in the hall, sitting in the curtain, roughhousing.
5. Return visit: the third-day homecoming.
6. Meeting the kin: the two families’ kin meet.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Central Jiangxi (Nanchang, Fuzhou)**: moderate observances.
- **North-eastern Jiangxi (Shangrao, Jingdezhen)**: close to Huizhou and Zhejiang, with stronger lineage colouring.
- **Southern Jiangxi (Ganzhou)**: a Hakka area, with marked lineage customs.
- **Western Jiangxi (Yichun, Pingxiang)**: close to Hunan, with the influence of Hunan custom.
- **The various peoples**: the She marriage custom of Jiangxi forms its own system and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: bridal chair, dragon-and-phoenix candles, veil, wedding steelyard, dowry chests.
- **Documents**: the horoscope card, gift list, invitations, marriage letter, genealogy.
