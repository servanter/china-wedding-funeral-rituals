# Fujian Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

Fujian preserves **the most complete Han Chinese wedding tradition**, with the lineage, the ancestral hall and the vestiges of the “Six Rites” all surviving together. Its principal features:

1. **“Discussion — sending the betrothal — the major betrothal — reception”** — in Minnan the “sending the betrothal” (*songding*, the minor betrothal) and the “major betrothal” (*dading*, the great engagement) are distinct.
2. **“Wedding cakes”** — the betrothal gift centres on “wedding cakes”, shared out among kin and friends.
3. **“Covering with a rice sieve” and “shading the head with a black umbrella”** — the bride leaving home covers her head with a rice sieve or a black umbrella to ward off evil.
4. **“Crossing the fire”** — she steps over a brazier on entering the house.
5. **“Taking tea”** — the bride serves tea to her elders (“drinking tea”), who give her jewellery.
6. **“Returning to the maternal home (*fanhwaijia*)”** — the homecoming on the third day or some days later.
7. **Lineage and ancestral hall** — the wedding is presided over by the lineage; marriages are recorded in the genealogy.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Tang and Song**: migrants from the central plains entered Fujian, bringing the Six Rites; the Minnan and Fuzhou sub-groups took shape after the Song.
- **Ming and Qing**: the lineage system flourished, and wedding custom is recorded in the gazetteers and genealogies.
- **Modern times**: Minnan people crossed to Taiwan, carrying the custom there (see the entry for Taiwan).
- **Today**: hotel weddings predominate; the old customs survive in Minnan lineage villages and in Chinese-style weddings.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Discussion**: the matchmaker proposes, the family is inspected and the marriage is matched.
2. **Sending the betrothal (minor betrothal)**: rings and jewellery are sent to fix the engagement.
3. **Major betrothal (great engagement)**: the betrothal gift and wedding cakes are sent, and the wedding date fixed.
4. **Reception**: bridal chair and band; covering with the rice sieve, crossing the fire; bowing in the hall and sitting in the curtain.
5. **Taking tea**: the bride serves tea to the elders and receives gifts.
6. **Returning to the maternal home**: the homecoming visit.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Minnan (Quanzhou, Xiamen, Zhangzhou)**: developed lineage halls; the customs of wedding cakes and covering with the rice sieve are typical.
- **Fuzhou (eastern Fujian)**: elegant and antique in its observances, insisting on the “three inspections” (of family standing, character and health).
- **Putian–Xianyou**: distinctive local customs.
- **Western Fujian (the Hakka area)**: strong lineage colouring, with equal weight on secondary burial and ancestor worship.
- **The various peoples**: the She marriage custom of Fujian forms its own system and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: bridal chair, wedding cakes, rice sieve, black umbrella, brazier, dragon-and-phoenix candles, veil, gold ornaments.
- **Documents**: the horoscope card, the gift list, the marriage letter, the genealogy.
