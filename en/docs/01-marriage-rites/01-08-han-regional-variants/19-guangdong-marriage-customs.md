# Guangdong Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The three great sub-groups of Guangdong (**Cantonese, Teochew and Hakka**) each have their own face, but share **lineage participation and a complete vestige of the “three letters and six rites”**. Its principal features:

1. **The vestige of the “three letters and six rites”** — proposal, betrothal, sending the gift and reception are distinct.
2. **“Coming of age (*shangtou*)”** — the bride and groom have their hair dressed (the knotting of the hair, the threading of the face) before the wedding; an essential stage.
3. **“The great presentation (*guodali*)”** — the betrothal gift is sent (wedding cakes, seafood, the three sacrificial animals, gold ornaments).
4. **“The great aunt (*dajin jie*)”** — the woman who accompanies and assists at the wedding (also performing the role of master of ceremony).
5. **“Return visit (third day)”** — the homecoming three days after the wedding; with a “roast pig” (once a marker of chastity, now changing).
6. **“Thanking the matchmaker” and “*lai see*”** — wedding exchanges commonly use the “*lai see*” (red packet).

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Tang and Song**: migrants from the central plains and from Fujian and Jiangxi moved south; the Cantonese, Teochew and Hakka groups took shape between the Song and Ming.
- **Ming and Qing**: lineage halls and lineage estates flourished; the gazetteers and genealogies record wedding ceremonies in detail.
- **Modern times**: close contacts with Hong Kong, Macau and the Nanyang blended Chinese and Western custom.
- **Today**: hotel weddings combine with Chinese ceremony; the “great presentation” and “coming of age” remain common.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Proposal** (the matchmaker, matching the eight characters).
2. **Betrothal** (the civil betrothal, sending the civil betrothal): the betrothal gift and wedding cakes are sent.
3. **Coming of age**: on an auspicious day before the wedding the couple’s hair is combed and faces threaded.
4. **Reception**: bridal chair or wedding car; bowing in the hall and serving tea.
5. **Return visit**: the third-day homecoming, with a roast pig and other gifts.
6. **Thanking the matchmaker**: with a “matchmaker’s *lai see*” and wedding cakes.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Cantonese (Guangzhou, Foshan, Dongguan)**: a complete vestige of the three letters and six rites; the “great aunt” accompanies the party.
- **Teochew (Shantou, Chaozhou, Jieyang)**: solemn ceremonies; even the funeral cortege shows the richness of its ritual custom.
- **Hakka (Meizhou, Huizhou, Heyuan)**: presided over by the lineage; stress on the genealogy and ancestor worship.
- **The Leizhou peninsula and western Guangdong**: both Minnan and local colouring.
- **The various peoples**: the Yao, Zhuang and She marriage customs of Guangdong form their own systems and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: bridal chair, wedding cakes, gold ornaments, the dragon-and-phoenix gown, the implements of coming of age (comb, red cord), the roast pig.
- **Documents**: the horoscope card, the gift list (the great-presentation list), the marriage letter, the genealogy.
