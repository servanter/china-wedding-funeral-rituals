# Jiangsu Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

Jiangsu marriage custom differs **markedly between north and south**: in the south (Suzhou, Wuxi, Changzhou) the proprieties are elaborate and the sequence full; in the north (Xuzhou, Huai’an, Yancheng) it is close to Shandong, Henan and Anhui, and plainer. Its principal features:

1. **The vestiges of the “Six Rites”** — in the south the stages of proposal, matching, presentation and reception are distinct.
2. **“The third-day return”** — the homecoming three days after the wedding.
3. **“Dressing the bed” and “scattering the bed”** — a “woman of full fortune” is asked to dress the bed, and dates and chestnuts scattered.
4. **“Drinking the wedding wine”** — the banquet is grand, with attention to the standard of the table.
5. **The character of the water country** — in some water towns the bride is fetched by boat (according to the conditions of the waterways).

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Ming and Qing**: Suzhou and Yangzhou were great centres of economy and culture, and wedding custom was elaborate; the charitable estate’s rules provided for and regulated marriages within the lineage.
- **Modern times**: under Shanghai’s influence southern Jiangsu custom grew more modern.
- **Today**: hotel weddings predominate; the countryside keeps the return visit and the dressing of the bed.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Negotiation**: proposal, matching, exchange of cards.
2. **Betrothal (presentation)**: the betrothal gift and gift list.
3. **Dressing the bed**: a woman of full fortune dresses the bed and scatters it.
4. **Reception**: bridal chair and band; bowing in the hall and sitting in the curtain.
5. **Drinking the wedding wine**: the banquet for the guests.
6. **The third-day return**: the homecoming.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Southern Jiangsu (Suzhou, Wuxi, Changzhou)**: the most elaborate ceremonies, with attention to the table and the dowry.
- **Nanjing**: a blend of north and south.
- **Central Jiangsu (Yangzhou, Taizhou, Nantong)**: with the characteristics of both, simpler than the south.
- **Northern Jiangsu (Xuzhou, Huai’an, Yancheng)**: close to Shandong and Henan, with attention to “exchanging cards” and the “mother-parting meat”.
- **The various peoples**: the Hui marriage custom of Jiangsu (Nanjing, Yangzhou) forms its own system and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: bridal chair, dragon-and-phoenix candles, veil, wedding steelyard, dowry chests, auspicious quilts.
- **Documents**: horoscope card, gift lists (of betrothal and of the furniture), invitations, marriage letter.
