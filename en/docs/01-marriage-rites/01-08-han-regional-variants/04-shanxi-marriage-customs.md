# Shanxi Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

Shanxi marriage custom is **ancient and substantial in propriety and elaborate in sequence**, and the rich merchant households of central Shanxi especially insisted on display. Its principal features:

1. **The clear vestiges of the “Six Rites”** — proposal, matching, exchange of cards, presentation of the betrothal, request for the date and reception are each distinct.
2. **“Shanxi merchant custom”** — in central Shanxi (Taigu, Qi county, Pingyao) merchant families insisted on heavy betrothal gifts and a full complement of insignia.
3. **“Mother-parting meat” and the urging gift** — material customs similar to those of Hebei.
4. **“Sitting in the curtain” and roughhousing** — on entering the bridal chamber the couple sit in the curtain, the bed is scattered, and kin roughhouse.
5. **“Return visit” and “the month-long stay”** — the third-day return, together with the custom of staying a full month.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Ming and Qing**: the Shanxi merchants were dominant, and the marriage customs of central Shanxi were famed for “propriety and face”, much recorded in the gazetteers and genealogies.
- **Modern times**: with the decline of the Shanxi merchants the scale of weddings shrank.
- **Today**: central and southern Shanxi still keep old customs such as “exchanging cards” and “sitting in the curtain”; cities chiefly hold hotel weddings.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Proposal and matching**: the matchmaker proposes, and the eight characters and zodiac animals are matched.
2. **Exchanging cards**: the horoscope cards are exchanged and the engagement made.
3. **Presentation of the betrothal**: the betrothal gift and gift list are sent.
4. **Request for the date**: the wedding date is fixed.
5. **Reception**: bridal chair and band; bowing in the hall, sitting in the curtain, roughhousing.
6. **Return visit and the month-long stay**: the third-day return, and the custom of staying a full month.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Central Shanxi (Taiyuan, Jinzhong)**: most elaborate in former times, with full betrothal gifts and insignia.
- **Southern Shanxi (Linfen, Yuncheng)**: ancient and substantial in propriety, close to the customs of the Guanzhong plain.
- **Northern Shanxi (Datong, Xinzhou)**: close to Inner Mongolia and Hebei, the sequence simpler.
- **South-eastern Shanxi (Changzhi, Jincheng)**: a mountain region, mostly following village usage.
- **The various peoples**: the Hui marriage custom of Shanxi forms an independent system and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: bridal chair, gongs and drums, dragon-and-phoenix candles, veil, dowry chests, the urging gift.
- **Documents**: horoscope card, gift list (of the betrothal), invitations, marriage letter, genealogy.
