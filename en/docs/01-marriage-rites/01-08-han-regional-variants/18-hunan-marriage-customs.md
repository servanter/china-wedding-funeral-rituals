# Hunan Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

Hunan marriage custom is marked by **well-developed bridal-lament songs and an elaborate sequence**, differing between central-northern and western Hunan. Its principal features:

1. **“Matching the marriage”, “betrothal”, “sending the gift”** — the negotiation is clear.
2. **“Bridal lament”** — girls sing bridal-lament songs before marriage, on thanking their parents and parting from their sisters (famous in Changsha, Yiyang and elsewhere).
3. **“Dowry company” and “sitting up the night”** — girl friends keep the bride company and sing in antiphon the night before.
4. **“Reception, bowing in the hall, roughhousing”** — the sequence is complete.
5. **“Return visit”** — the third-day homecoming.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Pre-Qin**: the song-custom of Chu (the *Chuci*) is the distant source of the bridal-lament tradition.
- **Ming and Qing**: the “Customs” sections of the gazetteers record wedding ceremonies and song-customs; the bridal-lament songs passed orally.
- **Today**: city weddings predominate; the bridal-lament songs have entered the intangible-heritage lists.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Proposal and matching the marriage.
2. Betrothal (exchange of cards, the minor gift).
3. Sending the gift (the great engagement).
4. Bridal lament and sitting up the night (before the wedding).
5. Reception: bridal chair and band; bowing in the hall, sitting in the curtain, roughhousing.
6. Return visit.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Central-northern Hunan (Changsha, Yiyang, Xiangtan)**: the bridal-lament songs are most flourishing.
- **Southern Hunan (Hengyang, Chenzhou)**: close to Guangdong and Jiangxi, with a Hakka colouring.
- **Western Hunan (the Xiangxi prefecture, Huaihua)**: a Tujia, Miao and Dong area, where Han custom is influenced by “keeping the ten sisters company”.
- **Eastern Hunan (Zhuzhou, Liling)**: close to Jiangxi, with similar customs.
- **The various peoples**: the Tujia, Miao, Dong and Yao marriage customs form their own systems and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: bridal chair, dragon-and-phoenix candles, veil, dowry chests.
- **Documents**: the horoscope card, gift list, invitations, marriage letter.
- **Song-books**: manuscripts of bridal-lament songs.
