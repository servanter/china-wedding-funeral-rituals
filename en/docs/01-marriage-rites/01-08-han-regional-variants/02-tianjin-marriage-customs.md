# Tianjin Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

Tianjin marriage custom follows the ritual order of north China, and has in addition **the style of display of a wharf and commercial port**; it insists on “liveliness” and “face”. Its principal features:

1. **“Speaking the match” and “exchanging cards”** — the matchmaker proposes and the horoscope cards are exchanged.
2. **“The presentation” (“great card”, “small card”)** — the groom’s family sends the betrothal gift, itemised on a gift list.
3. **“Urging the trousseau”** — on the day before the wedding the groom’s family sends the “urging gift” (clothing, jewellery, food), hastening the bride’s family’s preparations.
4. **“Great wedding” and “small wedding”** — the great wedding uses the full bridal chair and band; the small wedding is simplified.
5. **“Reception — bowing in the hall — sitting in the curtain — roughhousing”** — the full sequence, with dates and chestnuts scattered at the sitting.
6. **“The fourth-day return”** — the return visit on the fourth day after the wedding (called “*hui si*” in Tianjin).

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Ming and Qing**: Tianjin was a hub of the grain transport and a centre of the salt trade; its merchants and people were prosperous and weddings vied in luxury.
- **Late Qing and Republican period**: concession and Chinese quarters coexisted, and Western and new-style weddings appeared here comparatively early.
- **Today**: hotel weddings predominate; the old rites survive in wedding displays and in the outlying counties.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Negotiation**: speaking the match and matching the marriage.
2. **Exchanging cards**: the horoscope cards are exchanged and the engagement made.
3. **The presentation**: the betrothal gift and gift list are sent.
4. **Urging the trousseau**: the urging gift is sent on the day before the wedding.
5. **Reception**: bridal chair and band, the three bows, and sitting in the curtain.
6. **The fourth-day return**: the return visit on the fourth day.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **The old walled city**: the old rites insisted upon, the sequence complete.
- **The coast (Tanggu, Hangu)**: the marriage customs of fisher and salt workers, with a maritime colouring.
- **Jizhou, Baodi and other outlying counties**: close to the village customs of the capital region.
- **The various peoples**: Tianjin Hui marriage custom (religious ceremony, religious witness) differs from the Han; it must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: bridal chair, gongs and drums, dragon-and-phoenix candles, veil, wedding steelyard, dowry chests.
- **Documents**: horoscope card, gift list, invitations, marriage letter.
