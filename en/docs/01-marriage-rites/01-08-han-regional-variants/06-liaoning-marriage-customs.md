# Liaoning Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Han Chinese of Liaoning are mostly **descendants of migrants who “crossed the passes”** (chiefly from Shandong and Hebei); their marriage custom follows the **Shandong-Hebei type**, settled into its form in the north-eastern environment. Its principal features:

1. **“Proposal — exchange of cards — presentation — reception”** — the sequence follows north China.
2. **“Mother-parting meat”** — the groom’s family sends the bride’s family the “mother-parting meat”.
3. **“Building the awning for guests”** — an awning is put up in the courtyard and tables laid, the banquet large and the atmosphere warm.
4. **“Pressing the bed” and “sitting in the curtain”** — a boy is asked to press the bed before the wedding; on the wedding day the couple sit in the curtain and are roughhoused.
5. **“Return visit”** — the third-day return, with a banquet at the bride’s family’s house.
6. **Uninhibited roughhousing** — the north-east is famous for its boisterous chamber roughhousing (in a spirit of play).

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Qing**: the ban on settlement gradually relaxed, and migrants from Shandong and Hebei poured in, bringing the marriage customs of their home prefectures.
- **Republican period**: the peak of “crossing the passes”, when north-eastern Han society took shape and its marriage customs settled.
- **Today**: weddings are held in hotels or at home; the old customs survive in the villages and in wedding displays.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Proposal**: the matchmaker proposes and the marriage is matched.
2. **Exchange of cards**: the horoscope cards are exchanged and the engagement made.
3. **The presentation**: the betrothal gift, the mother-parting meat and the gift list are sent.
4. **Building the awning and preparing the tables**: the courtyard and the banquet are arranged before the wedding.
5. **Reception**: by bridal chair or cart; bowing in the hall, sitting in the curtain, roughhousing.
6. **Return visit**: the third-day return.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Southern Liaoning (Dalian, Yingkou)**: close to Shandong, the customs resembling those of Jiaodong.
- **Western Liaoning (Jinzhou, Chaoyang)**: close to Hebei.
- **Shenyang and the centre**: city weddings modernised comparatively early.
- **The various peoples**: the Manchu marriage custom of Liaoning (“exchanging the cup”, “bowing to the Dipper” and the like), the Korean and the Hui customs form their own systems and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: bridal chair, gongs, drums and *suona*, dragon-and-phoenix candles, veil, mother-parting meat, dowry chests.
- **Documents**: horoscope card, gift list, invitations, marriage letter.
