# Anhui Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

Anhui marriage custom differs sharply between north and south: in **Huizhou in the south** the lineage leads and the proprieties are strict; in the **north** it is close to Henan and Shandong, and plainer. Its principal features:

1. **Huizhou’s complete “Six Rites”** — proposal, betrothal, presentation and reception all complete, much recorded in genealogies and family rules.
2. **“The third-day return”** — the homecoming three days after the wedding.
3. **“Dressing the bed” and “scattering the bed”** — performed by a woman of full fortune.
4. **Lineage participation** — a wedding must be reported to the lineage elders, who also coordinate the gifts and support.
5. **“Bridal lament”** (in some mountain districts) — the custom of lamenting before marriage.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Ming and Qing**: with the height of the Huizhou merchants, Huizhou weddings insisted on “propriety” and “order”; the gazetteers and genealogies record them in detail.
- **Northern Anhui**: close to the central plains, the marriage custom resembling that of Henan and Shandong.
- **Today**: city weddings predominate; the old Huizhou customs survive in the lineage villages and in the genealogical records.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Proposal and matching the marriage.
2. Betrothal (exchange of cards): the horoscope cards are exchanged.
3. Presentation (the formal betrothal): the betrothal gift and gift list.
4. Reception: bridal chair and band; bowing in the hall and sitting in the curtain.
5. The third-day return.
6. Meeting the kin: the kin of both sides meet.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Huizhou (Huangshan, She county, Jixi)**: the most complete lineage wedding, with strict propriety.
- **Southern Anhui (Xuancheng, Chizhou)**: with both Huizhou and Jiangnan colouring.
- **Central Anhui (Hefei, Lu’an)**: a north-south transition, the ceremonies moderate.
- **Northern Anhui (Fuyang, Bozhou, Suzhou)**: close to Henan and Shandong, with attention to “exchanging cards” and the “mother-parting meat”.
- **The various peoples**: the Hui marriage custom of Anhui (Bozhou, Bengbu) forms its own system and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: bridal chair, dragon-and-phoenix candles, veil, wedding steelyard, dowry chests, auspicious quilts.
- **Documents**: horoscope card, gift list, marriage letter, genealogies and family rules, invitations.
