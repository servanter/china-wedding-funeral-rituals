# Hainan Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Han Chinese of Hainan are mostly **descendants of Minnan migrants** (speaking Hainanese, a Min dialect), so their marriage custom is **close to Minnan** yet has the character of a tropical island. Its principal features:

1. **“Inspecting the house” and “betrothal”** — the bride’s family inspects the groom’s house (“inspecting the house”, to see the family’s circumstances) before betrothal.
2. **“Sending the date” and “reception”** — the wedding day is chosen and the bride fetched, with drums, music and bridal chair (or cart).
3. **“Bridal lament”** — girls lament before marriage.
4. **“Covering the head” and “crossing the fire”** — the bride covers her head with an umbrella or rice sieve and steps over fire on entering.
5. **“Return visit”** — the third-day homecoming.
6. **“Overseas-remittance weddings”** — in modern times Nanyang Chinese sent money home for weddings, forming a “overseas-Chinese-home-town wedding custom”.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Song, Yuan, Ming and Qing**: Minnan people crossed to Hainan in succession, bringing Minnan wedding custom; the Han settlements of northern Hainan took shape.
- **Ming and Qing**: the “Customs” sections of the Qiongzhou prefectural gazetteer and the county gazetteers record wedding ceremonies.
- **Modern times**: the influence of Nanyang Chinese gave the custom an overseas-home-town colour.
- **Today**: city weddings predominate; the countryside keeps inspecting the house, covering the head and the like.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Proposal, inspecting the house, matching the marriage.
2. Betrothal (sending the betrothal).
3. Sending the date (fixing the wedding day).
4. Reception: fetching the bride with drums and music; covering the head, crossing the fire; bowing in the hall and sitting in the curtain.
5. Return visit: the third-day homecoming.
6. Meeting the kin.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Northern Hainan (Haikou, Wenchang, Chengmai)**: the strongest Minnan colouring, with developed lineage halls.
- **Eastern Hainan (Qionghai, Wanning)**: similar customs.
- **Southern Hainan (Sanya, Lingshui)**: with traces of local and Li influence.
- **Western Hainan (Danzhou, Dongfang)**: more complex dialect and custom.
- **The various peoples**: the Li and the Hainan Miao marriage customs form their own systems and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: bridal chair or wedding car, dragon-and-phoenix candles, veil, umbrella, rice sieve, brazier, dowry chests.
- **Documents**: the horoscope card, gift list, marriage letter, genealogy.
