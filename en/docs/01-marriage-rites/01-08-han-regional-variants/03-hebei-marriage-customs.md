# Hebei Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

Hebei marriage custom belongs to the **north-China type**; the proprieties are plain, with attention to material customs such as the gift list and the urging gift. Its principal features:

1. **“Proposal”, “betrothal”, “exchange of cards”** — the matchmaker proposes and the horoscope cards are exchanged.
2. **“The mother-parting meat”** — the groom’s family sends the bride’s family a piece of meat (or a pig’s leg), called the “mother-parting meat”, figuring “the daughter leaving her mother to marry”.
3. **“Urging the trousseau”** — before the wedding the “urging cakes” (a wheaten food) and clothing are sent.
4. **“Pressing the bed”** — before the wedding a boy is asked to sleep a night on the bridal bed, called “pressing the bed”.
5. **“Reception — bowing in the hall — sitting in the curtain — roughhousing”** — the full sequence, with dates and chestnuts scattered at the sitting.
6. **“Return visit”** — the third-day return, at which the bride’s family feasts the son-in-law.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Ming**: Hebei was the directly governed province, and its wedding ceremonies followed the *Collected Statutes of the Ming* and the *Family Rituals*.
- **Qing**: the customs of the directly governed province are recorded in the “Customs” sections of the prefectural and county gazetteers.
- **Modern times**: migrants “crossing the passes” carried Hebei and Shandong marriage customs to the north-east (see the entries for Liaoning, Jilin and Heilongjiang).
- **Today**: cities chiefly hold hotel weddings; in the countryside the customs of the “mother-parting meat” and the “urging cakes” survive.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Negotiation**: proposal and matching the marriage.
2. **Betrothal**: exchange of cards and the minor gift.
3. **The presentation**: the betrothal gift, the mother-parting meat and the gift list.
4. **Urging the trousseau**: the urging cakes and clothing are sent before the wedding.
5. **Reception**: the bride is fetched by bridal chair, and the couple bow in the hall, sit in the curtain and are roughhoused.
6. **Return visit**: the third-day return.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Central Hebei (Baoding, Shijiazhuang)**: the proprieties are more elaborate, with the mother-parting meat and the urging gift.
- **Southern Hebei (Handan, Xingtai)**: close to Henan, with attention to the rite of “exchanging cards”.
- **Eastern Hebei (Tangshan, Qinhuangdao)**: close to the north-east, with similar customs.
- **The Bashang (Zhangjiakou, Chengde)**: close to Inner Mongolia, the proprieties simplified.
- **The various peoples**: the Hui, Manchu and Mongolian marriage customs of Hebei form their own systems and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: bridal chair, dragon-and-phoenix candles, veil, urging cakes, mother-parting meat, dowry chests.
- **Documents**: horoscope card, gift list, invitations, marriage letter.
