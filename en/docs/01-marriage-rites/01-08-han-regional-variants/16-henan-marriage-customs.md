# Henan Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

Henan lies in the central plains, and its marriage custom is reckoned among the types **that preserve most of the ancient rites**, built around “proposal, exchange of cards, betrothal and fetching the bride”. Its principal features:

1. **“Exchange of cards” (exchange of horoscope cards)** — the central procedure of the marriage negotiation.
2. **“Pressing the bed”** — a boy is asked to press the bed before the wedding.
3. **“Dressing the bed” and “scattering the bed”** — a woman of full fortune dresses the bed and scatters dates and chestnuts.
4. **“Bowing in the hall, sitting in the curtain, roughhousing”** — the sequence is complete.
5. **“Return visit”** — the third-day homecoming.
6. **Grand wedding banquets** — the central-plains custom of feasting guests is strong.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Northern Song**: the wedding custom of Bianjing (Kaifeng) is recorded in the *Dongjing menghualu*; the customs of “exchange of cards” and “sitting in the curtain” are attested.
- **Ming and Qing**: the “Customs” sections of the gazetteers record wedding ceremonies in detail.
- **Today**: hotel weddings and village banquets go side by side; the old customs survive.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Proposal, matching and exchange of cards.
2. Betrothal (the minor gift).
3. Sending the gift (the great engagement, the gift list).
4. Dressing and pressing the bed.
5. Fetching the bride: bridal chair and band; bowing in the hall, sitting in the curtain, roughhousing.
6. Return visit.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Eastern Henan (Kaifeng, Shangqiu)**: close to Shandong and Anhui, with similar observances.
- **Western Henan (Luoyang, Sanmenxia)**: close to Shaanxi, with antique and substantial observances.
- **Southern Henan (Nanyang, Xinyang)**: close to Hubei, with the influence of Chu custom.
- **Northern Henan (Anyang, Xinxiang)**: close to Hebei.
- **The various peoples**: the Hui marriage custom of Henan (Zhengzhou, Kaifeng, Nanyang) forms its own system and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: bridal chair, dragon-and-phoenix candles, veil, wedding steelyard, dowry chests, auspicious quilts.
- **Documents**: the horoscope card, gift list, invitations, marriage letter.
