# Sichuan Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

Sichuan marriage custom is marked by **bridal lament, sitting up the night and grand banquets**, with slight differences between the Chengdu plain and the eastern mountain districts. Its principal features:

1. **“Proposal”, “matching the eight characters”, “betrothal”, “sending the gift”** — the sequence is complete.
2. **“Bridal lament”** — girls sing bridal-lament songs before marriage.
3. **“Sitting up the night”** — kin and friends keep the bride company and sing the night before.
4. **“Reception, bowing in the hall, sitting in the curtain, roughhousing”** — the sequence is complete, and the roughhousing is lively.
5. **“Return visit”** — the third-day homecoming.
6. **“Courtyard banquet”** — the wedding banquet is large, with neighbours all assembling.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Pre-Qin**: the folkways of Ba and Shu meet the song-custom tradition of Chu.
- **Ming and Qing**: the “Huguang filling Sichuan” migration brought the wedding customs of Hubei, Hunan, Jiangxi and Guangdong, forming the “Sichuan-school marriage custom”.
- **Today**: hotel weddings and village courtyard banquets go side by side.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Proposal and matching the eight characters.
2. Betrothal (exchange of cards, sending the gift).
3. Bridal lament and sitting up the night (before the wedding).
4. Reception: bridal chair and band; bowing in the hall, sitting in the curtain, roughhousing.
5. Return visit.
6. Meeting the kin.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **The Chengdu plain (Chengdu, Deyang, Meishan)**: fairly regular observances, with elaborate banquets.
- **Eastern Sichuan (Dazhou, Guang’an)**: close to Chongqing, with similar customs.
- **Southern Sichuan (Yibin, Luzhou, Zigong)**: with the influence of Yunnan and Guizhou.
- **Northern Sichuan (Mianyang, Guangyuan)**: with the influence of Shaanxi and Gansu.
- **The various peoples**: the Qiang, Tibetan, Yi and Miao marriage customs form their own systems and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: bridal chair, dragon-and-phoenix candles, veil, dowry chests.
- **Documents**: the horoscope card, gift list, invitations, marriage letter.
- **Song-books**: manuscripts of bridal-lament songs.
