# Wedding Anniversaries

## 1. Definition

Traditionally there was no institution of the “wedding anniversary”; its counterpart was the **yearly and seasonal homecoming and exchange of visits** after the wedding. The “wedding anniversary” is a **new custom** formed in modern times under Western influence, usually observed by the year.

**Traditional forms**:
- **The return visit and homecoming**: frequent in the early period after the wedding, expressing the etiquette of dealings between affines.
- **The third morning, the full month, the full year**: the nodes connected with the wedding and with the birth of a child.
- **Dealings among old affines**: sustained over decades by the seasons and by birthday celebrations.

**Modern forms**:
- The wedding anniversary (paper, cotton, wood … gold, diamond), with gifts of the corresponding material.
- The **golden wedding** (fifty years) and the **diamond wedding** (sixty years), often celebrated by the children, an important contemporary family custom.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **The Republic**: the Western-style wedding and the idea of the anniversary were introduced, and adopted first among the educated.
- **After the 1950s**: the registration of marriages made the “date of marriage” definite, and the idea of the anniversary spread.
- **From the 1990s**: wedding anniversaries, wedding photographs and anniversary travel became common; celebrations of the golden and diamond weddings are often reported in the media and held in communities.
- **Today**: they coexist with the traditional homecoming and dealings among old affines, forming a post-wedding custom in which old and new run together.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **The anniversary**: the couple celebrate together, exchanging gifts of the corresponding material (paper, cotton, wood, silver, gold, diamond and so on).
2. **Golden and diamond wedding celebrations**: the children hold a banquet, take photographs and give speeches, and relatives and friends offer congratulations.
3. **A family photograph**: the whole family is photographed, reviewing the course of the marriage.
4. **Traditional dealings in parallel**: the return visit, the visiting of affines and the seasonal exchange of gifts are kept.
5. **Keeping the mementos**: the marriage certificate, wedding photographs, anniversary photographs and gift lists are preserved as family documents.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **The cities**: wedding anniversaries and golden-wedding celebrations are general.
- **The countryside**: dealings among old affines and birthday celebrations predominate, anniversaries being secondary.
- **Fujian and Taiwan**: the anniversary of “holding hands” (the couple) and the family photograph are much valued.
- **Ethnic minorities**: marriage anniversaries are generally combined with traditional festivals and family gatherings, with some influence from foreign concepts such as the silver wedding; these must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Mementos**: the marriage certificate, wedding ring, wedding photographs, anniversary album, mementos of the corresponding material.
- **Banquets**: the golden-wedding banquet, the anniversary banquet.
- **Related documents**: invitations, speeches, family albums and anniversary albums.
