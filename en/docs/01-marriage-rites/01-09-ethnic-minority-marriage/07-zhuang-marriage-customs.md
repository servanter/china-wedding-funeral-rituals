# Zhuang Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Zhuang are the most populous ethnic minority, distributed mainly in Guangxi. Marriage practises **exogamy**, with the marked features of **choosing a mate by antiphonal song at the song fair, and “not settling in the husband’s home” in some regions**.

**Core elements**:

1. **The “song fair” (*ge xu*)** — centred on the “Third of the Third” song fair, where young men and women choose mates by antiphonal song.
2. **“Proposal” and “betrothal (fixing the fate)”** — a matchmaker is sent to propose, the eight characters matched (“fixing the fate”), and the bride price sent.
3. **“Blocking-the-road songs” and “sealing the door”** — at the fetching the bride’s family blocks with song, and entry is won only by antiphonal song.
4. **“Bridal lament”** — the bride laments before marriage.
5. **“Not settling in the husband’s home”** (some regions) — the new wife lives long at her mother’s home, moving to the husband’s after bearing children or after some years.
6. **“Return visit”** — the homecoming visit.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Pre-Qin to Tang-Song**: the marriage custom of the forebears (“Luo Yue”, “Western Ou”) is recorded in histories and archaeology.
- **From Tang-Song onward**: Daoism and Han custom entered, blending with the native.
- **Ming and Qing**: the “Customs” sections of the gazetteers (the Guangxi general gazetteer and the prefectural, county and department gazetteers) record the wedding rite and “not settling in the husband’s home”.
- **Today**: the wedding keeps forms such as antiphonal song and door-blocking; the “Third of the Third” has become an important festival.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Meeting at the song fair**: choosing a mate by antiphonal song.
2. **Proposal**: a matchmaker is sent to propose.
3. **Betrothal (fixing the fate)**: matching the eight characters, sending the bride price, fixing the wedding day.
4. **Bridal lament**: the bride laments before marriage.
5. **Fetching the bride**: blocking-the-road antiphonal song, bowing in the hall, feasting guests.
6. **Not settling / return visit**: a temporary stay at the mother’s home or the homecoming.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Central Guangxi (Nanning, Laibin, Liuzhou)**: the song fair and blocking-the-road songs flourish.
- **Western Guangxi (Baise, Hechi)**: living among the Yao, Dong and Bouyei, customs interpenetrate.
- **Northern Guangxi (Guilin)**: close to Hunan, with traces of Hunan custom.
- **The Yunnan Zhuang (Wenshan)**: living among the Miao, Yao and Hani.
- **Relation to other peoples**: Zhuang custom and Han custom influence each other, but the kinship system does not adopt the “five degrees of mourning”, and it must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: the bride price (wine, meat, cloth, silver ornaments), the embroidered ball, the bronze drum, song-fair ware.
- **Dress**: Zhuang brocade, Zhuang dress (indigo-dyed cloth).
- **Documents**: the bride-price list, the eight-character card, the marriage certificate.
