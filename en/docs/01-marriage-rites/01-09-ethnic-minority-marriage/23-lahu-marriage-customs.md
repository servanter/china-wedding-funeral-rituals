# Lahu Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Lahu are distributed in Pu’er, Lincang, Xishuangbanna and Honghe of Yunnan. The marriage custom is marked by **“courting the girls” antiphonal song, “uxorilocal residence” and *lusheng* song and dance**.

**Core elements**:

1. **“Courting the girls” and “antiphonal song”** — young men and women sing antiphonally at night to make friends.
2. **“Proposal” and “betrothal”** — a matchmaker is sent to propose; the bride price is wine, rice, cloth and silver ornaments.
3. **“Uxorilocal residence” (some branches)** — after the wedding the man first lives in the wife’s home for several years, then takes his wife back to his own home.
4. **“*Lusheng* song and dance”** — the wedding is accompanied by *lusheng* dance through the night.
5. **“Calling back the soul”** — at the wedding the “soul” of the new couple is “called back” for blessing.
6. **Christian influence** — some Lahu of Lancang believe in Christianity; the wedding has a church rite.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **From Tang-Song to Ming-Qing**: the marriage custom of the Lahu forebears (“Luohei” and other names) is recorded in histories and local gazetteers.
- **Modern times**: Christianity and Southern Buddhism spread in different regions, diversifying the wedding form.
- **Today**: the traditional custom survives in the Lahu core areas of Lancang and Menglian.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Courting the girls, antiphonal song to make friends.
2. Proposal and betrothal.
3. The wedding: *lusheng* song and dance, calling back the soul for blessing.
4. Uxorilocal residence: the man first enters the wife’s home.
5. Wedding banquet: the village joins in congratulation.
6. Moving to the husband’s home or long-term uxorilocal residence.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **The Lahu Na (Lancang, Menglian)**: living among the Dai and Wa, with Buddhism and Christianity coexisting.
- **The Lahu Si (Lancang, Ximeng)**: the branch customs are more archaic.
- **The Kucong (Jinping, Lüchun and others)**: originally a Lahu branch, with other features.
- **Relation to other peoples**: Lahu marriage custom is not同源 with the Han “Six Rites” and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: the bride price (wine, rice, cloth, silver ornaments), the *lusheng*, bamboo-woven ware.
- **Dress**: Lahu dress (black-based clothing, silver ornaments).
- **Documents**: the bride-price list, the marriage contract.
