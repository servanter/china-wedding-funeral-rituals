# Jino Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Jino are concentrated on the Jino Mountain (Jino Township) of Jinghong, Xishuangbanna, Yunnan. The marriage custom is marked by **"visiting the girls", antiphonal song and the "crying-marriage", with village elders presiding**.

**Core elements**:

1. **"Visiting the girls"** — young men and women gather at night to sing in antiphonal song; courtship is fairly free.
2. **"Coming-of-age rite"** — only after the coming-of-age rite may the youth "visit the girls" and associate.
3. **"Proposing and betrothal"** — the groom's parents commission a matchmaker to propose, sending tea and wine to seal the betrothal.
4. **"Crying-marriage"** — before leaving, the bride sings the crying-marriage song.
5. **"Village elders presiding"** — the wedding is presided over by the "Zhuoba" and "Zhuosheng" (village elders).
6. **"The big drum and Temaoke"** — the "big-drum" cult and the "Temaoke" (Iron-forging Festival) are associated customs.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Qing dynasty**: the Jino (old name "Youle") appear in the *Pu'er fuzhi* and Dai-language documents.
- **Modern period**: the "visiting the girls" custom and the village-elder system are recorded in 1950s ethnic-survey materials.
- **Contemporary**: the Jino Mountain traditional custom and the "Temaoke Festival" are listed for intangible-heritage protection.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Visit the girls and sing in antiphonal song.
2. Propose and send tea and wine to seal the betrothal.
3. Betroth and discuss the bride-price.
4. Fetch the bride, crying-marriage.
5. Village elders preside, feast and antiphonal song.
6. Return visit.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Jino Mountain (Jinghong)**: the Jino core area, where the custom is best preserved.
- **Other townships of Xishuangbanna**: intermingled with the Dai and Hani; customs blend.
- **Relation to other peoples**: Jino marriage custom does not share a common origin with the Han "Six Rites" and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: tea, wine, the big drum, weaving implements.
- **Dress**: Jino striped hemp garments, peaked caps.
- **Documents**: the marriage contract, the gift list.
