# Brown Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Brown are concentrated in Xishuangbanna (Menghai) and Lincang (Shuangjiang, Gengma) of Yunnan. They believe in **Southern Buddhism** and also the traditional spirit faith. The marriage custom is marked by **“going to the village” antiphonal song, “uxorilocal residence” and Buddhist blessing**.

**Core elements**:

1. **“Going to the village”** — young men and women sing antiphonally at night to make friends (some villages have the “bachelors’ house” form of socialising).
2. **“Proposal” and “betrothal”** — a matchmaker is sent to propose; the betrothal gift is tea, wine, rice and wax tapers.
3. **“Uxorilocal residence”** — after the wedding the man first lives in the wife’s home (one to several years), then takes his wife back to his own home.
4. **“Thread-tying” and “water-dripping”** — a monk is invited to chant; white thread is tied to the wrists and water dripped, for blessing.
5. **“The village elder presiding”** — the village head takes part in the marriage negotiation and the rites.
6. **“Return visit”** — returning home to visit the parents.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **From Tang-Song to Ming-Qing**: the marriage customs of the Brown forebears (“Pu”) are recorded in histories and local gazetteers.
- **Modern times**: Southern Buddhism deepened its influence; the monastery took charge of chanting and blessing.
- **Today**: the traditional custom survives in the mountain areas; towns are simpler.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Going to the village, antiphonal song to make friends.
2. Proposal and discussing the betrothal gift.
3. The wedding: the monk chants, thread-tying, water-dripping for blessing.
4. Uxorilocal residence: the man first enters the wife’s home.
5. Wedding banquet: the village joins in congratulation.
6. Moving to the husband’s home / return visit.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Xishuangbanna (Bulang Mountain, Menghai)**: a concentrated core area, with deep Southern-Buddhist influence.
- **Lincang (Shuangjiang, Gengma)**: living among the Dai, Wa and Lahu, customs interpenetrate.
- **Relation to other peoples**: Brown marriage custom is not同源 with the Han “Six Rites” and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: wax tapers, white thread, the betrothal gift (tea, wine, rice), the water-dripping vessel.
- **Dress**: Brown dress (black-and-red brocade, silver ornaments).
- **Documents**: the bride-price list, the marriage contract.
