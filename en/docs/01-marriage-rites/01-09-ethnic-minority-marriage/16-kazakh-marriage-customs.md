# Kazakh Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Kazakhs are distributed in northern Xinjiang and in Gansu and Qinghai. Marriage practises **exogamy beyond the tribe**; the wedding follows **Islamic norms** while keeping its nomadic features (**horse-racing, goat-grabbing, antiphonal song**).

**Core elements**:

1. **“Proposal” and “betrothal”** — the groom’s family sends a matchmaker to propose, and the bride price sent (horses, sheep, cloth, silver ornaments).
2. **“The ahong recites the Nikah”** — at the wedding the ahong is invited to recite the “Nikah” (to witness the marriage), and the couple drink the “Nikah water” (or eat salt-water).
3. **“Antiphonal song” (*aytes*)** — at the wedding young men and women sing antiphonally, lively and joyful.
4. **“Horse-racing, goat-grabbing, chasing the girl”** — competitive sports during the wedding celebration.
5. **“Fetching the bride to the yurt”** — the bride arrives at the husband’s yurt and undergoes the “veil-lifting” (*betashar*) rite.
6. **“Return visit”** — the homecoming after the wedding.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **After the spread of Islam**: the wedding norms combined with the old steppe-nomadic custom.
- **Qing dynasty**: Kazakh tribes moved into Ili, Altay and elsewhere; the wedding custom corresponded to the religious faith.
- **Modern times**: the tribal structure was closely bound to marriage.
- **Today**: town weddings are simplified; the sports and antiphonal song are preserved in pastoral-area celebrations.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Proposal and betrothal.
2. Sending the bride price (the “*qaluqoi*” and others, as the tribe determines).
3. Reciting the Nikah: the ahong witnesses the marriage.
4. Veil-lifting: the bride enters the yurt for the veil-lifting rite.
5. Wedding sports and antiphonal song: horse-racing, goat-grabbing, the *aytes*.
6. Return visit.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Ili, Altay, Tacheng**: Kazakh core areas, with the traditional custom well preserved.
- **Gansu Aksai, Qinghai Haixi**: living among the Hui and Mongol, similar customs.
- **Urban Kazakhs**: weddings simplified, with family feasting continued.
- **Relation to other peoples**: among the Islamic-wedding peoples the details differ; not同源 with the Han “Six Rites”, and it must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: the bride price (horses, sheep, cloth), the yurt, horse gear, the *dombra*.
- **Dress**: Kazakh dress (silver ornaments, the feathered cap).
- **Documents**: the bride-price list, the marriage contract (the marriage certificate).
