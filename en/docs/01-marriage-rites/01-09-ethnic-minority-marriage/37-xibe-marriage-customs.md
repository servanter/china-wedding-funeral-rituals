# Xibe Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Xibe are concentrated in Qapqal of Yili, Xinjiang, and are also distributed in Liaoning, Jilin and Heilongjiang. The custom is deeply influenced by the Manchu and Han, and is marked by **“exogamy beyond the *hara-mokun*”, the fetching of the bride and the “cigarette-offering” rite**.

**Core elements**:

1. **“Exogamy beyond the *hara-mokun*”** — those of the same *hara* (clan) and same *mokun* (family) do not marry; seniority and family standing are observed.
2. **“Proposal” and “fixing the betrothal”** — a matchmaker is sent to propose; wine gifts are sent to fix the engagement, and the bride price delivered (cloth, ornaments, livestock).
3. **The “*dingba*”** — on the fetching day the groom forms a fetching party to go to the bride’s home, and the two sides play and sing antiphonally.
4. **The “cigarette-offering” rite** — on entering the door the bride bows to heaven and earth and to her parents-in-law, and offers cigarettes and lights them for the elders, in token of respect.
5. **The “*beilun* dance”** — at the wedding banquet they dance the *beilun* and sing Manchu tunes.
6. **“Garrison-reclamation and cross-ethnic marriage”** — the Xinjiang Xibe garrisoned the frontier, and mingled increasingly with the Han, Kazakh and Uyghur.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Qing dynasty**: the Xibe were enrolled in the Eight Banners; the custom was influenced by the Manchu. In the Qianlong reign part moved west to garrison Yili, forming the Qapqal concentrated area.
- **Modern times**: the Manchu-style wedding rites were kept; the “cigarette-offering” and “*dingba*” are recorded in local gazetteers and ethnography.
- **Today**: the traditional wedding and the *beilun* dance are revived in cultural activities and intangible-heritage protection.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Proposal: a matchmaker proposes, the marriage is matched.
2. Fixing the betrothal: the groom’s family sends wine gifts and ornaments, formally betrothing.
3. Sending the bride price: cloth, cakes and other gifts before the wedding day.
4. Fetching the bride: the “*dingba*”, the fetching party goes to the bride’s home, playing and singing antiphonally.
5. Bowing in the hall and the cigarette-offering: bowing to heaven and earth and to the parents-in-law, the bride offering cigarettes to the elders.
6. Wedding banquet and return visit: a banquet with the *beilun* dance; returning home on a chosen day after the wedding.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Qapqal (Yili, Xinjiang)**: the Xibe core area, with the most marked Manchu-style custom.
- **Liaoning, Jilin, Heilongjiang**: living among the Han and Manchu, the wedding tends to a Han-Manchu blend.
- **The Yili river valley**: a garrison-reclamation settlement, with much intercourse with the Kazakh and Uyghur.
- **Relation to other peoples**: Xibe marriage custom is not同源 with the Han “Six Rites” and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: the wine gift, ornaments, cloth, the wedding cake, banquetware.
- **Dress**: the Manchu-style robe, the waistcoat, the embroidered shoes (the bride).
- **Documents**: the marriage contract, the gift list (today the marriage certificate).
