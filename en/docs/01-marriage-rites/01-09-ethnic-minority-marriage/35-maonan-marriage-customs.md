# Maonan Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Maonan are concentrated in Hechi of Guangxi (the Huanjiang Maonan Autonomous County and elsewhere). The marriage custom is marked by **“antiphonal song”, “not settling in the husband’s home” and the “flower bamboo hat”**.

**Core elements**:

1. **“Choosing a mate by antiphonal song”** — young men and women sing antiphonally at festivals and song-fairs to make friends.
2. **“Proposal” and “betrothal”** — a matchmaker is sent to propose; the betrothal gift is wine, meat, cloth and silver ornaments.
3. **The “flower bamboo hat”** — the Maonan “*dingka hua*” (the flower bamboo hat) is an important token of love and marriage (given by the groom to the bride).
4. **“Not settling in the husband’s home”** — after the wedding the new wife stays at her mother’s home, moving to the husband’s after bearing children or when the agreed period ends.
5. **The “Fenlong Festival” and the marriage custom** — the Fenlong Festival (the ancestor-worshipping festival) is a common point for proposing and marrying.
6. **“Return visit”** — returning home to visit the parents.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **From Tang-Song to Ming-Qing**: the Maonan forebears (“Maotan”, “Maonan”) are recorded in histories and local gazetteers (the Qingyuan prefectural gazetteer and others).
- **Modern times**: living among the Zhuang and Han, the custom is much Sinicised.
- **Today**: the traditional custom survives in the villages of Huanjiang.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Antiphonal song to make friends.
2. Proposal and betrothal (giving the flower bamboo hat).
3. Fetching the bride: welcoming guests with antiphonal song, bowing in the hall, feasting.
4. Not settling: a temporary stay at the mother’s home after the wedding.
5. Long-term residence in the husband’s home.
6. Return visit.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Huanjiang (Shangnan, Zhongnan, Xianan)**: the Maonan core area, with the custom fairly completely preserved.
- **Other parts of Hechi**: living among the Zhuang and Han, customs interpenetrate.
- **Relation to other peoples**: Maonan marriage custom is not同源 with the Han “Six Rites” and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: the flower bamboo hat (*dingka hua*), the betrothal gift (wine, meat, cloth, silver ornaments).
- **Dress**: Maonan dress (indigo-dyed cloth).
- **Documents**: the bride-price list, the marriage contract.
