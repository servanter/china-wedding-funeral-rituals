# Tatar Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Tatar are distributed across Yining, Urumqi, Tacheng and Altay of Xinjiang. The marriage custom is marked by **the Islamic "Nikah", the lifting of the bridal veil and the "meshrep" song-dance gathering**.

**Core elements**:

1. **"Proposal and betrothal"** — the groom's parents bring gifts to the bride's home to propose; after betrothal the bride-price is sent.
2. **The "Nikah"** — an ahong presides and recites the "Nikah".
3. **"Lifting the veil"** — at the wedding a song is sung and the bride's veil is lifted.
4. **"Scattering sweets"** — at the wedding sweets and food are scattered, signifying sweetness and fulfilment.
5. **The "meshrep"** — a song-and-dance gathering is held at the banquet.
6. **"Valuing education"** — the Tatar esteem education, and weddings tend to be simple.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Qing dynasty**: the Tatar (the "Tatar") migrated from Russian territory into Xinjiang; the custom carries Islamic and Russian influence.
- **Modern period**: settlements formed in Yining and Tacheng; weddings followed Islamic law.
- **Contemporary**: urban Tatar weddings tend to be simplified.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Propose and matchmake.
2. Betroth and send the bride-price.
3. Nikah (presided over by the ahong).
4. Fetch the bride, wedding banquet (meshrep).
5. Lift the veil.
6. Return visit.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Yining, Tacheng**: Tatar core areas, where the custom is best preserved.
- **Urumqi**: urban weddings tend to be simplified.
- **Relation to other peoples**: Tatar marriage custom does not share a common origin with the Han "Six Rites" and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: the frame drum, sweets, pilaf, ornaments.
- **Dress**: embroidered caps, skirts, waistcoats.
- **Documents**: the religious marriage contract, the gift list.
