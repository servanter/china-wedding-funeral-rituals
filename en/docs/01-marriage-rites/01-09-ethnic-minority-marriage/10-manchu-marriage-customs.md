# Manchu Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Manchu are distributed mainly in Liaoning, Jilin, Heilongjiang, Hebei and Beijing. The custom has both **its own features (worshipping the Northern Dipper, archery)** and, because of fusion with the Han after entering the pass, Han rites as well.

**Core elements**:

1. **“Speaking the match” and “setting the betrothal”** — at the “minor betrothal” jewellery is sent; at the “major betrothal” the “*ruyi*” scepter, clothing and gift list are sent.
2. **“Archery”** — at the fetching the groom shoots three arrows at the chair door (or the door); a marker of Manchu custom (to drive off evil).
3. **“Worshipping the Northern Dipper”** — at the wedding the couple salute the Northern Dipper star; a specifically Manchu rite.
4. **“Sitting in the curtain” and “*hejin*”** — entering the bridal chamber to sit in the curtain, sharing the cup, eating the “*zisun* dumplings”.
5. **“Setting the betrothal”, “sending the gift”, “the bride price”** — the procedure is close to the Han but not exactly the “Six Rites”.
6. **“Return visit”** — the third-day homecoming.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Ming dynasty**: the marriage customs of the Jurchen tribes are recorded in histories, with phrases such as “marriage regardless of wealth”.
- **Qing dynasty**: after entering the pass, influenced by Han custom, “bowing to heaven and earth” and “sitting in the curtain” merged with Han usage; the banner people’s wedding is recorded in the *Collected Statutes of the Great Qing*.
- **Modern times**: the banner wedding fused with the Beijing-banner folkways, forming a “Manchu-Han blended” northern custom.
- **Today**: the traditional ceremony is preserved in Manchu areas and wedding displays (archery and worshipping the Dipper are common display items).

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Speaking the match**: a matchmaker is sent to arrange the match, and the horoscopes compared.
2. **Minor betrothal**: jewellery is sent to fix the engagement.
3. **Major betrothal**: the *ruyi* scepter, clothing and gift list are sent, and the wedding day fixed.
4. **Fetching the bride**: bridal chair and band; the groom shoots arrows.
5. **Worshipping the Dipper, bowing to heaven and earth, sitting in the curtain**: the rites, entering the bridal chamber.
6. **Return visit**: the third-day homecoming.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **The Manchu areas of eastern and northern Liaoning**: more of the old custom preserved.
- **The Beijing banners**: fused with Beijing custom, with elaborate observances.
- **Jilin and Heilongjiang**: living among the Han and Korean, the ceremony simplified.
- **Relation to other peoples**: Manchu custom is deeply fused with the Han, yet “archery” and “worshipping the Dipper” remain its own tradition and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: the bridal chair, bow and arrows, the *ruyi* scepter, the *zisun* dumplings, dragon-and-phoenix candles.
- **Dress**: the *qipao*, the horse-hoof cuffs, the banner headdress (the bride’s headpiece).
- **Documents**: the horoscope card, gift list, marriage letter, the genealogy (“the genealogy book”).
