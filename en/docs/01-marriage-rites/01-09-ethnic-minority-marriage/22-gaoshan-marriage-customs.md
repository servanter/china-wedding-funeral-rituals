# Gaoshan Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Gaoshan is the collective name for the Austronesian-language peoples of the Taiwan region (including the Amis, Atayal, Paiwan, Bunun, Tsou, Tao and others); there are also Gaoshan compatriots on the mainland. The customs of the branches differ greatly.

**Common features**:

1. **Age-organisation and mate choice** — young men and women socialise and meet by song and dance through the age organisation (such as the “youth house”).
2. **“Uxorilocal” or “virilocal” residence** — the orientation differs by branch (the Amis and others practise uxorilocal residence, the man entering the wife’s home).
3. **Bride price** — mostly pig, wine, millet, areca nut and cloth.
4. **Song and dance as rite** — the wedding centres on collective song and dance (the “harvest-festival” style of dance).
5. **Ancestral spirits and taboos** — the wedding must report to the ancestral spirits; the mourning-period taboos are elaborate and strictly separate from marriage.
6. **Exogamy** — close-kin marriage is forbidden, with strict rules.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Prehistory to Ming-Qing**: the settlement and burial materials seen in Taiwan archaeology correspond to the branch customs; the Ming-Qing local gazetteers (the Taiwan prefectural gazetteer and others) record the “barbarian” wedding rite.
- **Modern times**: foreign religions (Christianity) entered some branches, changing the wedding form.
- **Today**: cultural revival among the branches has brought attention to and partial restoration of the traditional custom.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Song-and-dance socialising and mate choice.
2. Proposal (through the tribal elder or matchmaker).
3. Discussing the bride price and fixing the wedding day (reporting to the ancestral spirits).
4. Fetching the bride (uxorilocal or virilocal as the branch determines).
5. Wedding song and dance, feasting.
6. Post-wedding residence arrangements, sacrificing to the ancestors.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Amis**: uxorilocal residence, with marked age-organisation and song-dance.
- **Atayal, Seediq**: mountain tribes, with strict marriage taboos.
- **Paiwan, Rukai**: the noble system affects marriage (a tendency to class-endogamy).
- **Tao (Yami)**: an island tribe, with particular wedding customs and taboos.
- **Relation to other peoples**: the Gaoshan branches belong to the Austronesian cultural system, with no inherited relation to the Han wedding, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: the bride price (pig, wine, millet, areca nut, cloth), instruments (the bow-harp, the wooden drum).
- **Dress**: branch dress (shell beads, glass beads).
- **Documents**: the tribal marriage-discussion record (oral), the marriage contract.
