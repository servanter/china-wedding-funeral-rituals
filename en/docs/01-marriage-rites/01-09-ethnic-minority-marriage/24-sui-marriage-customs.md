# Sui Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Sui are concentrated in southern Guizhou (Sandu, Libo, Dushan) and parts of Guangxi and Yunnan. The marriage custom is marked by **“choosing the auspicious by the *Shui* script”, “antiphonal song” and “not settling in the husband’s home”**.

**Core elements**:

1. **“Choosing the auspicious by the *Shui* script”** — the wedding day and the fitness of the match are judged by the Sui written classic, the “*Shui* script” (held by the *Shui*-script master).
2. **“Antiphonal song”** — song accompanies the marriage process.
3. **“Proposal” and “betrothal”** — a matchmaker is sent to propose; the bride price is wine, rice, pig and silver ornaments.
4. **“Not settling in the husband’s home”** — the new wife stays at her mother’s home for a time, moving to the husband’s after bearing children or when the agreed period ends.
5. **The “*Duan* Festival” and the marriage custom** — the *Duan* Festival (the Sui New Year) is a common point for matchmaking and marrying.
6. **“Return visit”** — the homecoming visit.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Tang and Song**: the marriage custom of the Sui forebears (“Liao” and others) is recorded in histories.
- **Ming and Qing**: the local gazetteers (the Dushan department gazetteer, the Sanhe county gazetteer) record the custom; the *Shui*-script documents are transmitted among the people.
- **Modern times**: living among the Han, Bouyei and Miao, the custom blends.
- **Today**: the *Shui* script is on the *Memory of the World* list; the traditional custom survives in Sandu and Libo.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Antiphonal song to make friends.
2. Proposal (asking the *Shui*-script master to match the marriage).
3. Betrothal: sending the bride price and fixing the wedding day (choosing the auspicious by the *Shui* script).
4. Fetching the bride: welcoming guests with song, the wedding banquet.
5. Not settling: a temporary stay at the mother’s home after the wedding.
6. Settling long-term in the husband’s home / return visit.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Sandu, Libo**: the Sui core area, with the most complete *Shui*-script transmission.
- **Dushan, Duyun**: living among the Han and Bouyei, much Sinicisation of the custom.
- **Guangxi (Nandan, Huanjiang)**: adjacent to the Zhuang and Maonan.
- **Relation to other peoples**: Sui marriage custom is not同源 with the Han “Six Rites” and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Classics**: the *Shui* script (manuscripts in the Sui script).
- **Objects**: the bride price (wine, rice, pig, silver ornaments), the horsetail-embroidery dress (a Sui speciality).
- **Documents**: the bride-price list, the marriage contract (the marriage certificate).
