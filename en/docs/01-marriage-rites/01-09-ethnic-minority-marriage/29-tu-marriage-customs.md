# Tu Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Tu are concentrated in Huzhu and Minhe of Qinghai and Tianzhu of Gansu. The wedding is marked by **“bridal lament”, “the *nashijin* (antiphonal song at the fetching)” and song and dance**.

**Core elements**:

1. **“Proposal” and “betrothal”** — a matchmaker is sent to propose; the betrothal gift is sent (wine, tea, cloth, silver ornaments).
2. **The “*nashijin*”** — the fetchers (the “*nashijin*”) go to the bride’s home, and the two sides sing antiphonally and splash water in play; the core rite of the Tu wedding.
3. **“Bridal lament”** — the bride laments before marriage.
4. **“Song and dance”** — at the wedding they sing the “*daola*” (wedding songs) and dance the “*anzhao*”.
5. **“Bowing to heaven and earth and worshipping the ancestors”** — the obeisances are performed and the ancestors reported to (Tibetan Buddhism and folk faith go side by side).
6. **“Return visit”** — returning home to visit the parents.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Yuan and Ming**: the Tu (“the Earth people”) took shape in the Huangshui region, with Tibetan Buddhism and folk faith side by side.
- **Ming and Qing**: the local gazetteers (the Xining prefectural gazetteer, the Nianbo county gazetteer) record the custom.
- **Modern times**: living among the Han, Tibetan and Hui, the customs influenced one another.
- **Today**: “Tu wedding custom” and the “*anzhao* dance” are on the intangible-heritage lists.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Proposal and betrothal.
2. Sending the betrothal gift and fixing the wedding day.
3. Bridal lament (before the wedding).
4. Fetching the bride: the “*nashijin*” antiphonal song and water-splashing.
5. The wedding: bowing to heaven and earth, worshipping the ancestors, the *anzhao* dance, feasting guests.
6. Return visit.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Huzhu, Minhe**: the wedding songs and the *anzhao* dance are the most typical.
- **Datong, Tianzhu**: living among the Tibetan and Han, the ceremony simplified.
- **Relation to other peoples**: Tu custom is deeply influenced by Tibetan Buddhism, not同源 with the Han “Six Rites”, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: the betrothal gift (wine, tea, cloth, silver ornaments), water-splashing ware, *anzhao* dance dress.
- **Dress**: Tu dress (the multicoloured sleeve, embroidered).
- **Documents**: the bride-price list, manuscripts of wedding songs, the marriage contract.
