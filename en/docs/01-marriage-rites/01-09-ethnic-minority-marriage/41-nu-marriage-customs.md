# Nu Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Nu are mainly distributed across the Nujiang Lisu Autonomous Prefecture of Yunnan (Gongshan, Fugong, Lushui) and the west of Diqing and Lijiang. The marriage custom is marked by **clan-exogamous marriage, love-plighting through antiphonal song and the "hearts-together wine"**.

**Core elements**:

1. **Courtship by antiphonal song** — young men and women pluck the "dabiya" (a lute) and sing in response, conveying affection through song.
2. **"Betrothal by wine"** — the proposal and betrothal take wine as the gift; drinking the "hearts-together wine" seals the pledge.
3. **"Clan-exogamous marriage"** — no marriage within the same clan; in the old days there was the custom of "levirate marriage" (a younger brother taking his deceased elder brother's widow).
4. **"Survivals of bride-capture"** — the old custom included a symbolic "bride-capture" at the bride-fetching.
5. **"Bamboo-house hearth"** — the wedding is held by the hearth of the bamboo house, worshipping the hearth and honouring the elders.
6. **"Branch differences"** — the Nusu, Anong, Rouruo, Gongshan Nu and other branches each have their own customs.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Yuan, Ming and Qing**: the Nu (recorded in old texts as "Nu" and "Lu") appear in the *Yiyitong zhi* and the northwest-Yunnan gazetteers.
- **Modern period**: levirate marriage, bride-capture and such are recorded in ethnic-survey materials.
- **Contemporary**: the traditional customs of Gongshan and Fugong are documented under intangible-heritage protection.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Pluck the "dabiya" and sing in courtship.
2. Propose and send wine to pledge the betrothal.
3. Betroth and discuss the bride-price.
4. Fetch the bride (including a symbolic "bride-capture").
5. Worship the hearth and drink the hearts-together wine.
6. Wedding banquet and return visit.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Fugong, Lushui**: branches such as the Nusu live intermingled with the Lisu, and customs blend.
- **Gongshan**: the Gongshan Nu are close to the Derung, and customs are similar.
- **Relation to other peoples**: Nu marriage custom does not share a common origin with the Han "Six Rites" and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: the dabiya (lute), bamboo-tube wine, the "hearts-together wine" vessels.
- **Dress**: Nu hemp cloth, bamboo and silver ornaments.
- **Documents**: the marriage contract, the gift list (in old times secured by notched wood and knotted cord).
