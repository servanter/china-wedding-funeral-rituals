# Jing Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Jing are concentrated in Dongxing of Guangxi (the three islands of Wutou, Shanxin and Wanwei, commonly called the "three Jing islands"). The marriage custom is marked by **mate choice through song at the "Ha" festival, "sand-kicking" and the monochord**.

**Core elements**:

1. **Mate choice at the "Ha" festival** — at the "singing-Ha festival" young men and women sing in antiphonal song to choose mates by conveying affection through song.
2. **"Pledging love by song"** — affection is conveyed through "singing-Ha" folk songs, and the marriage is settled by song.
3. **"Proposal and betrothal"** — a matchmaker proposes; cakes, sugar and cloth are sent to seal the betrothal.
4. **"Worshipping the hall and offering tea"** — at the wedding the "worship-the-hall" rite is performed and tea is offered to acknowledge kin.
5. **The "monochord"** — at the wedding the monochord is played and "Ha" sung for merriment.
6. **"Wooden-clog bride-fetching"** — in the old custom the bride wore wooden clogs for the rite, a Jing speciality.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Late Ming and early Qing**: the Jing forebears migrated from Vietnam's Do Son and elsewhere; the custom carries a Vietnamese flavour.
- **Modern period**: the three-island fishing-village custom is recorded in gazetteers and ethnic-survey materials.
- **Contemporary**: the Dongxing Jing "Ha festival" is listed in the national intangible-cultural-heritage catalogue.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Sing at the Ha festival and choose a mate.
2. Propose and betroth.
3. Send the bride-price and choose the date.
4. Fetch the bride, worship the hall.
5. Banquet (singing-Ha, monochord).
6. Return visit.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Wanwei, Wutou, Shanxin**: Jing core areas, where the custom is best preserved.
- **Towns of Dongxing**: intermingled with the Han; weddings tend to be Sinicised.
- **Relation to other peoples**: Jing marriage custom does not share a common origin with the Han "Six Rites" and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: the monochord, wooden clogs, songbooks (the "Ha" lyrics), cakes, sugar and cloth.
- **Dress**: Jing long garments, conical hats, embroidered shoes.
- **Documents**: the marriage contract, the gift list.
