# Achang Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Achang are concentrated in Dehong of Yunnan (Longchuan, Lianghe, Mangshi) and in Baoshan and Dali. The marriage custom is marked by **“going to the village” antiphonal song, cross-cousin-preference marriage and “sitting in the tent”**.

**Core elements**:

1. **“Going to the village” and antiphonal song** — young men and women gather by night at the song-ground to sing antiphonally and choose mates.
2. **“Cross-cousin-preference marriage”** — the son of the maternal uncle has the first right to marry the daughter of the paternal aunt, old called “repaying the uncle’s debt”.
3. **“Sitting in the tent”** — after the wedding the groom goes to the wife’s home to “sit in the tent” (uxorilocal residence) for a time, then takes his wife back.
4. **“Proposal and sending the betrothal”** — a matchmaker proposes; the betrothal gift includes areca nut, wine and meat and the “Husa knife”.
5. **“Thread-tying blessing”** — under Southern-Buddhist influence, at the wedding an elder ties thread for blessing.
6. **“Areca-nut hospitality”** — at the wedding banquet guests are treated with areca nut and tea.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Ming and Qing**: the marriage customs of the Achang forebears (“Echang”, “Achang”) appear in the *Yunnan gazetteer* and the western-Yunnan local gazetteers.
- **Modern times**: the “going to the village” and “sitting in the tent” customs are recorded in the 1950s south-west ethnic surveys.
- **Today**: the traditional custom is preserved in Husa of Longchuan and Lianghe.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Going to the village, antiphonal song to make friends.
2. Proposal and matchmaking.
3. Fixing the betrothal, sending the betrothal (areca nut, wine, meat, the Husa knife).
4. Fetching the bride, sitting in the tent.
5. Bowing in the hall, thread-tying blessing.
6. Wedding banquet, return visit.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Husa of Longchuan**: an Achang concentrated area, known for the “Husa knife” and Southern-Buddhist wedding custom.
- **Lianghe, Mangshi**: living among the Dai and Jingpo, customs interpenetrate.
- **Relation to other peoples**: Achang marriage custom is not同源 with the Han “Six Rites” and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: the betrothal gift (areca nut, wine, meat, the Husa knife), banquetware.
- **Dress**: Achang dress (mostly black and blue cloth, with silver ornaments).
- **Documents**: the marriage contract, the gift list.
