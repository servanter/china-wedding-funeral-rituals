# Bonan Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Bonan are concentrated in the Jishishan Bonan, Dongxiang and Salar Autonomous County of Linxia, Gansu (the Dahejia area). The marriage custom is marked by **the Islamic "Nikah", scattering walnuts and red dates and "hua'er" antiphonal singing**.

**Core elements**:

1. **The "Nikah"** — an ahong presides and recites the "Nikah", on which the marriage is established.
2. **"Proposal and sending the mahr"** — a matchmaker proposes; the groom's family sends the "mahr" (bride-price).
3. **"Scattering walnuts and red dates"** — at the wedding walnuts and red dates are scattered, signifying many children and good fortune.
4. **"Hua'er antiphonal singing"** — at the wedding "Bonan hua'er" songs are sung for merriment.
5. **"Bonan waist-knife"** — the waist-knife is a characteristic object, often given as a gift.
6. **"Three-day rite and return visit"** — the wedding includes the bride-fetching, the feast and the return visit.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Yuan, Ming and Qing**: the Bonan forebears followed Islamic law in marriage, as seen in the Hezhou gazetteers.
- **Modern period**: concentrated in Dahejia, weddings resemble those of the Dongxiang and Salar.
- **Contemporary**: the traditional Jishishan Bonan wedding is presented in ethnic festivals.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Propose and matchmake.
2. Betroth and send the mahr.
3. Nikah (presided over by the ahong).
4. Fetch the bride, scatter walnuts and red dates.
5. Banquet, hua'er antiphonal singing.
6. Return visit.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Dahejia of Jishishan**: the Bonan core area, where the custom is best preserved.
- **Linxia and Xunhua of Qinghai**: close intercourse with the Hui and Salar; customs are similar.
- **Relation to other peoples**: Bonan marriage custom does not share a common origin with the Han "Six Rites" and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: the Bonan waist-knife, walnuts, red dates, the mahr gift.
- **Dress**: silk robes, veils, embroidered caps.
- **Documents**: the religious marriage contract, the gift list.
