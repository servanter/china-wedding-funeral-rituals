# Derung Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Derung are concentrated in the Dulong River valley of the Gongshan Derung and Nu Autonomous County, Nujiang, Yunnan. The marriage custom is marked by **notched wood as the pledge, ox-slaughtering to entertain guests and the old form of arranged marriage**.

**Core elements**:

1. **"Recording by notched wood"** — the marriage contract and bride-price are secured by notched wood, a token in a society without writing.
2. **"Clan-endogamous and arranged marriage"** — in the old custom marriages were mostly arranged by parents and clan-endogamy predominated (an old custom, to be critically noted).
3. **"Sororal polygyny and levirate marriage"** — there were old customs of "sororal polygyny" (sisters sharing a husband) and "levirate marriage".
4. **"Ox-slaughtering to entertain guests"** — at the wedding an ox is slaughtered and shared, with the whole village celebrating.
5. **"Hearth and Kaqiewa"** — the wedding is held around the hearth; the "Kaqiewa" (New Year) is a node for matchmaking.
6. **"Old facial-tattoo custom"** — women were facially tattooed in the old days as an ethnic mark (an old custom, not to be encouraged).

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Qing dynasty**: the Derung (the "Qiu", "Qu" people) appear in the *Huangqing zhigong tu* and the northwest-Yunnan gazetteers.
- **Modern period**: notched-wood records, ox-slaughtering and such are recorded in ethnic-survey materials.
- **Contemporary**: after the Dulong River highway opened, the traditional custom blends with the outside world.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Propose (parents negotiate the marriage).
2. Betroth, notch wood as the pledge.
3. Send the bride-price (livestock, ironware, bead ornaments).
4. Fetch the bride.
5. Slaughter an ox to entertain guests.
6. Sing and dance around the hearth.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Upper Dulong River (Kongdang, Xianjiudang)**: the Derung core area, where the custom is best preserved.
- **Around the Gongshan county seat**: intermingled with the Nu and Lisu; customs blend.
- **Relation to other peoples**: Derung marriage custom does not share a common origin with the Han "Six Rites" and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: notched wood (the marriage pledge), ironware, bead ornaments, oxen.
- **Dress**: Derung hemp blankets ("yuoduo"), rattan-and-bamboo ornaments.
- **Documents**: notched wood, knotted cord (no writing in old times).
