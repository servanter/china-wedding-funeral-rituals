# Evenki Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Evenki are distributed across Hulunbuir of Inner Mongolia (Evenki Autonomous Banner, the Aoluguya area of Genhe) and Nehe of Heilongjiang. The marriage custom is marked by **clan-exogamous marriage, reindeer and hunting horses as bride-price and bonfire song-and-dance**.

**Core elements**:

1. **Clan-exogamous marriage** — no marriage within the same clan; a matchmaker brokers the match.
2. **"Proposal and bride-price"** — the groom brings wine and a khata to propose; the bride-price is reindeer, horses or game, according to the branch.
3. **"Reindeer branch"** — the Aoluguya reindeer Evenki use reindeer as bride-price and reindeer to fetch the bride.
4. **"Survivals of bride-capture"** — the old custom included a symbolic "bride-capture" at the bride-fetching.
5. **"Bonfire and song-dance"** — the wedding is held around a bonfire, with singing and dancing.
6. **"Shamanic blessing"** — in the old custom a shaman was invited to bless the couple.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Qing dynasty**: the Evenki (recorded as "Solon", "Tungus", "Yakut") appear in the *Heilongjiang waixi* and the gazetteers.
- **Modern period**: the customs of the hunting, pastoral and reindeer areas differ markedly.
- **Contemporary**: the traditional wedding of the Evenki Autonomous Banner and Aoluguya is presented in cultural activities and intangible-heritage protection.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Matchmaker's proposal.
2. Betrothal and discussion of the bride-price (reindeer or horses).
3. Fetch the bride (hunting horse or reindeer).
4. Bonfire rite, shamanic blessing.
5. Banquet song-and-dance.
6. Return visit.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Evenki Autonomous Banner**: pastoral Evenki, with bride-price mainly of horses, cattle and sheep.
- **Aoluguya (Genhe)**: reindeer Evenki, with reindeer as the characteristic bride-price.
- **Nehe of Heilongjiang and elsewhere**: intermingled with the Daur and Han; customs blend.
- **Relation to other peoples**: Evenki marriage custom does not share a common origin with the Han "Six Rites" and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: reindeer, hunting horses, khata, wine, birch-bark vessels.
- **Dress**: Evenki leather robes, reindeer-hide boots, head ornaments.
- **Documents**: the marriage contract, the gift list (in old times mainly an oral agreement).
