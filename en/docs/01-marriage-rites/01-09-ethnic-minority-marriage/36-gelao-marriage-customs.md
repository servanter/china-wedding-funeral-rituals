# Gelao Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Gelao are concentrated in Guizhou (Wuchuan, Daozhen, Zunyi, Anshun and others) and parts of Guangxi and Yunnan. The marriage custom is marked by **“antiphonal song”, the old “tooth-knocking” custom (documented) and the “three *yaotai*” hospitality**.

**Core elements**:

1. **“Choosing a mate by antiphonal song”** — young men and women sing antiphonally at festivals and markets to make friends.
2. **“Proposal” and “betrothal”** — a matchmaker is sent to propose; the betrothal gift is wine, meat, cloth and silver ornaments.
3. **The old “tooth-knocking” custom (documented)** — the ancient “tooth-knocking Gelao” had the custom of knocking out the front teeth at marriage (now abolished), much recorded in the old books.
4. **The “three *yaotai*” hospitality** — guests are treated to the “three *yaotai*” (three courses: the tea course, the wine course, the rice course).
5. **“Bowing in the hall” and “feasting”** — the procedure is close to the Han custom.
6. **“Return visit”** — returning home to visit the parents.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **From pre-Qin to Tang-Song**: the “Liao” marriage custom is recorded in the *Huayang guozhi*.
- **Ming and Qing**: the local gazetteers (the Zunyi prefectural gazetteer, the Anshun prefectural gazetteer) record the Gelao custom and the old “tooth-knocking”.
- **Modern times**: living among the Han and Miao, the custom is much Sinicised.
- **Today**: the traditional custom survives in the villages of north Guizhou.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Antiphonal song to make friends.
2. Proposal and betrothal.
3. Fetching the bride; bowing in the hall and feasting (the three *yaotai*).
4. Feasting kin and clan.
5. Return visit.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **North Guizhou (Wuchuan, Daozhen)**: concentrated areas, with the custom fairly completely preserved.
- **Central Guizhou (Anshun, Pingba)**: living among the Bouyei and Han, customs interpenetrate.
- **Southern Chongqing and southern Sichuan**: deeply Sinicised.
- **Longlin of Guangxi**: living among the Zhuang and Yi.
- **Relation to other peoples**: Gelao marriage custom is not同源 with the Han “Six Rites” and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: the betrothal gift (wine, meat, cloth, silver ornaments), the three-*yaotai* ware.
- **Dress**: Gelao dress (the tube-top garment, the tube skirt).
- **Documents**: the bride-price list, the marriage contract.
