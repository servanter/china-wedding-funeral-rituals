# Dong Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Dong are distributed in the contiguous areas of Guizhou, Hunan and Guangxi. Pre-marital socialising is marked by **“singing and sitting by the moon” (*xing ge zuo yue*)**; the wedding centres on **antiphonal song and road-blocking** as its ritual core.

**Core elements**:

1. **“Singing and sitting by the moon” (going to the village)** — young men and women gather at night to sing antiphonally and make friends.
2. **“Proposal” and “betrothal”** — a matchmaker is sent to propose, and the bride price sent (wine, meat, glutinous rice, cloth).
3. **“Blocking-the-road antiphonal song”** — at the fetching the bride’s family blocks with song (“blocking the road”); the groom’s side enters only by antiphonal song.
4. **“Not settling in the husband’s home”** (some regions) — the new wife stays at her mother’s home for a time after the wedding.
5. **The “uniting feast” (*he long yan*)** — the wedding banquet is eaten at a long table together, a warm atmosphere.
6. **“Return visit”** — the homecoming visit.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Tang and Song**: the marriage custom of the Dong forebears (“Liao”, “Dong”) is recorded in histories.
- **Ming and Qing**: the “Customs” sections of the gazetteers (the Liping prefectural gazetteer and others) record the wedding rite.
- **Modern times**: living among the Han and Miao, the custom blends.
- **Today**: the traditional custom survives in the Dong villages; the “Dong grand song” and “singing and sitting by the moon” have entered intangible-heritage protection.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Singing and sitting by the moon**: making friends by antiphonal song.
2. **Proposal**: a matchmaker is sent to arrange the match.
3. **Betrothal**: the bride price is sent and the wedding day fixed.
4. **Fetching the bride**: blocking-the-road antiphonal song, receiving the bride.
5. **Wedding banquet**: the uniting feast for guests, with song.
6. **Not settling / return visit**: a temporary stay at the mother’s home or the homecoming.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **South-eastern Guizhou (Liping, Congjiang, Rongjiang)**: the Dong core area, with a strong antiphonal-song tradition.
- **South-western Hunan (Tongdao, Jingzhou)**: living among the Miao and Han, customs interpenetrate.
- **Northern Guangxi (Sanjiang, Longsheng)**: adjacent to the Zhuang and Yao.
- **Relation to other peoples**: Dong marriage custom is not同源 with the Han “Six Rites” and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: the bride price (wine, meat, glutinous rice, cloth), the *lusheng*, the pipa lute, the “ox-leg” fiddle.
- **Dress**: Dong cloth, silver ornaments, Dong finery.
- **Documents**: the bride-price list, the “*kuan* words” (Dong customary law, including marriage norms).
