# Bouyei Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Bouyei are distributed in southern and south-western Guizhou and parts of Yunnan and Sichuan. Pre-marital socialising is marked by **“*lang shao*” (courting by antiphonal song)**, and some families practise **“not settling in the husband’s home”** after marriage.

**Core elements**:

1. **“*Lang shao*” (courting)** — young men and women make friends by antiphonal song at festivals and fairs.
2. **“Proposal” and “betrothal”** — a matchmaker is sent to propose, and the bride price sent (wine, meat, cloth, silver ornaments).
3. **“Antiphonal song at the fetching”** — at the fetching, song is sung inside and outside the door, welcoming guests with song.
4. **“Not settling in the husband’s home”** — the new wife stays at her mother’s home for a time, moving to the husband’s after bearing children or when the agreed period ends.
5. **“Bridal lament”** — the bride laments.
6. **“Return visit”** — the homecoming visit.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Tang and Song**: the marriage custom of the Bouyei forebears (“Liao” and others) is recorded in histories.
- **Ming and Qing**: the “Customs” sections of the gazetteers (the Guizhou general gazetteer, the Xingyi prefectural gazetteer) record the wedding rite.
- **Modern times**: living among the Han and Miao, the custom blends.
- **Today**: the traditional custom survives in the villages, the ceremony simplified.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Friendship by *lang shao***: meeting by antiphonal song.
2. **Proposal**: a matchmaker is sent to arrange the match.
3. **Betrothal**: the bride price is sent and the wedding day fixed.
4. **Bridal lament**: the bride laments before marriage.
5. **Fetching the bride**: welcoming guests with antiphonal song, bowing in the hall, feasting.
6. **Not settling / return visit**: a temporary stay at the mother’s home or the homecoming.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Southern Guizhou (the Qiannan prefecture)**: the Bouyei are concentrated; “*lang shao*” and “not settling” are seen.
- **South-western Guizhou (Xingyi, Anlong)**: living among the Miao and Yi, customs interpenetrate.
- **Central Guizhou (Guiyang, Anshun)**: close to the Han, with a high degree of Sinicisation.
- **Yunnan (Qujing, Wenshan)**: customs close to the Zhuang branches.
- **Relation to other peoples**: Bouyei marriage custom is not同源 with the Han “Six Rites” and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: the bride price (wine, meat, cloth, silver ornaments), rice wine, glutinous rice.
- **Dress**: Bouyei dress (indigo-dyed cloth, batik).
- **Documents**: the bride-price list, the marriage contract (today the marriage certificate).
