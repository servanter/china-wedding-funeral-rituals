# Wa Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Wa are distributed in Lincang and Pu’er of Yunnan (Cangyuan, Ximeng and elsewhere). The marriage custom is marked by **“courting the girls” antiphonal song, the vestige of “bride capture” and public village feasting**.

**Core elements**:

1. **“Courting the girls”** — young men and women sing antiphonally at night to make friends (some villages have the “bachelors’ house” form of socialising).
2. **“Proposal” and “betrothal”** — a matchmaker is sent to propose; the bride price is wine, rice, chicken and cattle (as the family’s means allow).
3. **The vestige of “bride capture”** — in some regions the wedding has a symbolic “capture of the bride” (agreed beforehand by both sides).
4. **“Divination by the *moba*”** — divination fixes the wedding day and chooses the auspicious.
5. **Southern-Buddhist influence** — some Wa believe in Southern Buddhism; the wedding also has Buddhist observances.
6. **“Return visit”** — the homecoming visit.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **From Tang-Song to Ming-Qing**: the marriage customs of the Wa forebears are recorded in the *Man shu* and the Ming-Qing local gazetteers.
- **Modern times**: the old customs of some regions were abolished in the mid-20th century; Buddhist influence deepened.
- **Today**: the traditional custom survives in the Wa core areas of Cangyuan and Ximeng.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Courting the girls, antiphonal song to make friends.
2. Proposal, divination to choose the auspicious.
3. Betrothal (discussing the bride price).
4. Fetching the bride: symbolic “capture of the bride”, antiphonal song, feasting guests.
5. The wedding: public village feasting, song and dance.
6. Return visit.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Ximeng, Cangyuan**: the Wa core areas, with more of the traditional custom preserved.
- **Pu’er, other parts of Lincang**: living among the Dai and Lahu, influenced by Southern Buddhism.
- **Relation to other peoples**: Wa marriage custom is not同源 with the Han “Six Rites” and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: the bride price (wine, rice, chicken, cattle), the wooden drum (the great vessel of village sacrifice), sacrificial ware.
- **Dress**: Wa dress (black-and-red brocade).
- **Documents**: the bride-price list, the marriage contract.
