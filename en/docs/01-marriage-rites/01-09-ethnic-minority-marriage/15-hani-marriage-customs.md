# Hani Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Hani are distributed in Honghe, Pu’er, Yuxi and Xishuangbanna of Yunnan. Marriage follows the principle of **“lineage division within the village” and exogamy beyond the group**; the wedding is marked by **the vestige of “bride capture” and antiphonal song**.

**Core elements**:

1. **“Courting the girls” and “antiphonal song”** — young men and women make friends by antiphonal song (some branches have the “girls’ house” form of socialising).
2. **“Proposal” and “betrothal”** — a matchmaker is sent to propose, and the bride price sent (rice, wine, silver ornaments, cloth).
3. **The vestige of “bride capture”** — in some branches the wedding has a symbolic “capture of the bride” (agreed beforehand by both sides, in playful cooperation).
4. **“Sacrificing at the village gate” and “calling back the soul”** — the wedding is tied to the village sacrifice and the “soul-calling” rite.
5. **The “long-street banquet” for guests** (some branches) — a form of public village feasting.
6. **“Return visit”** — the homecoming visit.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Tang and Song**: the marriage custom of the Hani forebears (“He Man” and others) is recorded in histories.
- **Ming and Qing**: the local gazetteers (the Lin’an prefectural gazetteer and others) record the custom; the village rites of the terraced-field rice society were stably transmitted.
- **Modern times**: influenced by the Dai (Southern Buddhism) and Yi custom.
- **Today**: the traditional custom is preserved in the villages of Honghe and Pu’er.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Making friends by antiphonal song.
2. Proposal and matching the marriage (divination).
3. Betrothal: sending the bride price and fixing the wedding day.
4. Fetching the bride: symbolic “capturing the bride”, antiphonal song, feasting guests.
5. The wedding: meeting the elders, sacrificing at the village gate, calling back the soul.
6. Return visit.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Honghe (Yuanyang, Honghe, Lüchun)**: the Hani core area, with the custom fairly completely preserved.
- **Pu’er, Yuxi (Mojiang, Yuanjiang)**: living among the Yi and Dai.
- **Xishuangbanna, Lancang**: influenced by the Dai Southern Buddhism.
- **Relation to other peoples**: Hani marriage custom is not同源 with the Han “Six Rites” and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: the bride price (rice, wine, silver ornaments, cloth), bamboo-woven ware, terraced-field farm tools.
- **Dress**: Hani dress (headdress, silver ornaments).
- **Documents**: the bride-price list, oral songs, the marriage contract.
