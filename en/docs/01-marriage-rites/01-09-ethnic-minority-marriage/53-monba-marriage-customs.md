# Monba Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Monba are distributed across Medog, Cona, Nyingchi and the Shannan area of Tibet. The marriage custom is marked by **Tibetan-Buddhist rites, "uxorilocal marriage" and "maternal-uncle authority"**.

**Core elements**:

1. **"Uxorilocal marriage"** — it is fairly common for the groom to move into the bride's family (uxorilocal residence).
2. **"Proposal and betrothal"** — a matchmaker proposes; a khata is offered and wine sent to seal the betrothal.
3. **"Maternal-uncle authority"** — the maternal uncle holds an honoured place at the wedding and must be given an "uncle's gift".
4. **"Offering the khata and toasting"** — at the wedding a khata is offered and barley wine toasted.
5. **"Scripture-chanting blessing"** — under the Nyingma school of Tibetan Buddhism, a lama is invited to chant scriptures at the wedding.
6. **"Singing and dancing"** — at the wedding songs are sung and folk dances danced, celebrating through the night.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Qing dynasty**: the Monba custom appears in the *Xizang zhi* and Tibetan-language documents.
- **Modern period**: uxorilocal marriage and maternal-uncle authority are recorded in ethnic-survey materials.
- **Contemporary**: after the Medog highway opened, the Monba traditional custom blends with that of the Tibetan.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Propose and matchmake.
2. Betroth (offer the khata, send wine).
3. Discuss the bride-price and choose the date.
4. Fetch the bride (or move in uxorilocally).
5. Scripture-chanting, khata-offering, toasting at the wedding.
6. Singing, dancing, return visit.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Medog**: a Monba core area, where the custom is best preserved.
- **Cona, Nyingchi**: intermingled with the Tibetan and Lhoba; customs blend.
- **Relation to other peoples**: Monba marriage custom does not share a common origin with the Han "Six Rites" and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: the khata, barley wine, butter tea, Buddhist-offering vessels.
- **Dress**: Monba pulu long robes, the "Balxia" hat.
- **Documents**: the marriage contract, the gift list (Tibetan-language documents).
