# Jingpo Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Jingpo are concentrated in Dehong of Yunnan (Mangshi, Yingjiang, Longchuan and others). Marriage practises **“cross-cousin marriage” together with exogamy**, and the wedding is marked by **antiphonal song, the vestige of “bride capture” and collective song and dance**.

**Core elements**:

1. **“Going to the village” and antiphonal song** — young men and women sing antiphonally at night to make friends.
2. **“Proposal” and “betrothal”** — a matchmaker is sent to propose; the bride price centres on cattle, pigs, wine and cloth.
3. **The vestige of “bride capture”** — in some regions the wedding has a symbolic “capture of the bride” (agreed beforehand by both sides).
4. **“Divination by the *dumsa*”** — a *dumsa* priest is invited to divine and fix the auspicious day.
5. **“The *munao* song and dance”** — the wedding is tied to collective dances such as the “*munao zongge*”.
6. **“Return visit”** — returning home to visit the parents.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **From Tang-Song to Ming-Qing**: the marriage customs of the Jingpo forebears (“Xunchuan Man” and others) are recorded in the *Man shu* and other histories.
- **Modern times**: influenced by the Dai and Han, the custom blended; the *munao zongge* is on the intangible-heritage list.
- **Today**: the traditional custom survives in the mountainous areas.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Going to the village, antiphonal song to make friends.
2. Proposal, divination to choose the auspicious.
3. Betrothal (discussing the bride price).
4. Fetching the bride: symbolic “capture of the bride”, antiphonal song.
5. The wedding: collective song and dance, feasting guests.
6. Return visit.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **The Dehong mountains (Longchuan, Yingjiang)**: the traditional custom is better preserved.
- **The flatlands (Mangshi, Ruili)**: living among the Dai, customs interpenetrate.
- **Relation to other peoples**: Jingpo marriage custom is not同源 with the Han “Six Rites” and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: the bride price (cattle, pigs, wine, cloth), the long knife, the *tongpa* (woven satchel), instruments (the frog-drum, the *mang* gong).
- **Dress**: Jingpo dress (silver studs, black-and-red brocade).
- **Documents**: the bride-price list, the marriage contract.
