# Hezhen Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Hezhen are distributed across Tongjiang, Raohe, Fuyuan and Jiamusi of Heilongjiang. The marriage custom is marked by **fetching the bride in a painted boat, serving raw fish to guests and the "Yimakan" blessing**.

**Core elements**:

1. **"Proposing"** — a matchmaker proposes, and the two families meet and view.
2. **"Lesser gift and greater gift"** — the betrothal is performed in two stages, called the "lesser gift" and the "greater gift".
3. **"Fetching the bride in a painted boat"** — the bride is fetched in a painted boat (birch-bark boat), a feature of a fishing-hunting people.
4. **"Serving raw fish to guests"** — at the banquet "slaughtered raw fish" (sliced raw fish) is the finest dish.
5. **"Yimakan" epic singing** — at the wedding "Yimakan" epic singing is performed for blessing.
6. **"Fish-skin bridal dress"** — in old times the bridal dress was sewn from fish-skin and deer-skin.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Qing dynasty**: the Hezhen (the "Heijin", "Hezhen") appear in the *Huangqing zhigong tu* and the gazetteers.
- **Modern period**: the fishing-hunting custom is recorded in ethnic-survey materials.
- **Contemporary**: the traditional custom of the Hezhen townships of Jiejinkou (Tongjiang) and Sipai (Raohe) is transmitted under intangible-heritage protection.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Propose.
2. Lesser gift, greater gift.
3. Choose the date, prepare the wedding.
4. Fetch the bride in the painted boat.
5. Banquet (raw-fish dish).
6. Yimakan blessing, return visit.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Jiejinkou of Tongjiang**: a Hezhen core area, where the custom is best preserved.
- **Sipai of Raohe**: intermingled with the Han and Manchu; customs blend.
- **Relation to other peoples**: Hezhen marriage custom does not share a common origin with the Han "Six Rites" and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: the birch-bark boat (painted boat), fishing nets, fish-skin, deer-skin.
- **Dress**: fish-skin garments, deer-skin garments (bridal dress), roe-deer-skin caps.
- **Documents**: the marriage contract, the gift list.
