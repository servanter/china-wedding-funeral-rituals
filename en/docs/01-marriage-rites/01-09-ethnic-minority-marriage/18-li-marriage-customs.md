# Li Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Li are the indigenous people of Hainan island, divided into dialect-branches such as the “Ha”, “Qi”, “Run”, “Meifu” and “Sai”. The marriage custom is marked by **choosing a mate by “*fangliao*” (night visiting and antiphonal song), “not settling in the husband’s home” and public feasting at the wedding banquet**.

**Core elements**:

1. **“*Fangliao*” (that is, “night visiting”)** — young men and women sing antiphonally at night to make friends (some branches have the “courting hut” form of socialising).
2. **“Proposal” and “betrothal”** — the groom’s family sends a matchmaker to propose, and the bride price sent (wine, pig, rice, areca nut).
3. **“Not settling in the husband’s home”** — the new wife lives at her mother’s home, staying long in the husband’s after bearing children or after some years (some branches).
4. **“Areca nut as the gift”** — at the wedding the areca nut is an important token of good faith.
5. **The influence of the “joint-plough system”** (a historical form in some Li areas) — marriage is tied to the “joint-plough” organisation.
6. **“Return visit”** — the homecoming visit.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Pre-Qin to Tang-Song**: the marriage custom of the Li forebears (descendants of “Luo Yue”) is recorded in histories and archaeology.
- **Ming and Qing**: the “Customs” sections of the local gazetteers (the Qiongzhou prefectural gazetteer and the county gazetteers) record the wedding under “Li customs”.
- **Modern times**: living among the Han and Miao, some customs were Sinicised.
- **Today**: the custom survives in the Li villages; “*fangliao*” and others enter ethnographic records.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. *Fangliao* antiphonal song to make friends.
2. Proposal (sending areca nut and wine).
3. Betrothal: discussing the bride price and fixing the wedding day.
4. Fetching the bride: receiving the bride, feasting guests, eating together.
5. Not settling: a temporary stay at the mother’s home after the wedding.
6. Settling long-term in the husband’s home / return visit.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **The Five-finger Mountains, Qiongzhong (the Qi and Sai dialect areas)**: more of the traditional custom preserved.
- **Baisha, Changjiang (the Run dialect area)**: customs close to the Han areas.
- **Ledong, Sanya (the Ha dialect area)**: the most populous, with marked intra-branch differences.
- **The Meifu dialect area (Dongfang, Changjiang)**: the custom has other features.
- **Relation to other peoples**: Li marriage custom is not同源 with the Han “Six Rites” and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: areca nut, the bride price (wine, pig, rice), the Li brocade, silver ornaments.
- **Dress**: the Li skirt, Li-brocade dress.
- **Documents**: the bride-price list, the marriage contract.
