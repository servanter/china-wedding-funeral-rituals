# Salar Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Salar are concentrated in Xunhua Salar Autonomous County of Qinghai and in Hualong and Jishishan of Gansu. They believe in Islam. The wedding follows **the religious norms** with its own ethnic features.

**Core elements**:

1. **“Proposal” and “betrothal”** — the groom sends a matchmaker to propose; “engagement tea” (tea, sugar, cloth) is sent as the betrothal gift, and the bride price negotiated.
2. **“Reciting the Nikah”** — at the wedding the ahong is invited to recite the “Nikah” to witness the marriage.
3. **The wedding banquet** — a feast for guests (wine is forbidden).
4. **“Singing the *hua’er*”** — the Salar folk song, the “*hua’er*”, is sung on weddings and other occasions.
5. **“Acknowledging the kin”** — the bride meets her parents-in-law and elders.
6. **“Return visit”** — returning home after the wedding.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Yuan dynasty**: the Salar forebears migrated east from Central Asia to Xunhua; Islam was central to community life.
- **Ming and Qing**: the mosque and the menhuan *gongbei* (the Sufi saint’s tomb) took charge of weddings and commemorations.
- **Today**: the rites continue; the mosque community takes part in weddings.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Proposal.
2. Betrothal (sending the engagement tea, negotiating the bride price).
3. Nikah: the ahong witnesses the marriage.
4. Wedding banquet: a feast for guests.
5. Acknowledging the kin: the bride meets her parents-in-law and elders.
6. Return visit.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Xunhua of Qinghai**: a concentrated core area, with regular observances.
- **Hualong, Jishishan of Gansu**: living among the Hui, Dongxiang and Bonan, similar customs.
- **Relation to other peoples**: among the Islamic-wedding peoples the details differ; not同源 with the Han “Six Rites”, and it must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: the engagement tea, the bride price, banquetware.
- **Religious objects**: the *Quran*, the mosque, the *gongbei*.
- **Documents**: the bride-price list, the marriage contract (the marriage certificate).
