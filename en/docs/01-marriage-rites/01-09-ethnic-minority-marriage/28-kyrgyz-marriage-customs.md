# Kyrgyz Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Kyrgyz are distributed in the Kizilsu Kyrgyz Autonomous Prefecture of Xinjiang and in Yili and Aksu. They believe in Islam, and marriage practises **exogamy beyond the tribe**; the wedding follows the religious norms while keeping its nomadic features.

**Core elements**:

1. **“Proposal” and “betrothal”** — the groom sends a matchmaker to propose, and the bride price is sent (horses, sheep, cloth, silver ornaments).
2. **“Reciting the Nikah”** — at the wedding the ahong is invited to recite the “Nikah” to witness the marriage.
3. **“Antiphonal song” and sport** — at the wedding young men and women sing antiphonally, with horse-racing and goat-grabbing.
4. **“Lifting the veil”** — the bride undergoes the veil-lifting rite on entering her husband’s home (like the Kazakh custom).
5. **The wedding banquet** — a feast for guests (milking tea, hand-grabbed mutton, koumiss, as the faith allows).
6. **“Return visit”** — returning home after the wedding.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **After the spread of Islam**: the marriage norms combined with the old nomadic custom.
- **Qing dynasty**: Kyrgyz tribes were distributed north and south of the Tianshan; the wedding custom corresponded to the religious faith.
- **Today**: town weddings are simplified; the traditional sport and antiphonal song are preserved in the pastoral areas.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Proposal and betrothal.
2. Sending the bride price.
3. Nikah: the ahong witnesses the marriage.
4. Lifting the veil: the bride enters her husband’s home for the rite.
5. Wedding celebration: antiphonal song, sport, feasting.
6. Return visit.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Kizilsu (Artux, Wuqia)**: a concentrated community, with regular observances.
- **Yili, Aksu**: adjacent to the Uyghur and Kazakh in custom.
- **Relation to other peoples**: among the Islamic-wedding peoples the details differ; not同源 with the Han “Six Rites”, and it must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: the bride price (horses, sheep, cloth), the yurt, horse gear, the *komuz* (a plucked string instrument).
- **Dress**: Kyrgyz dress (silver ornaments, the white felt cap).
- **Documents**: the bride-price list, the marriage contract (the marriage certificate).
