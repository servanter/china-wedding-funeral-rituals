# Mulao Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Mulao are concentrated in Hechi of Guangxi (the Mulao Autonomous County of Luocheng and elsewhere). The marriage custom is marked by **“walking the slope” (antiphonal song), “not settling in the husband’s home” and the “*yifan*” ancestor-worshipping festival**.

**Core elements**:

1. **“Walking the slope”** — young men and women sing antiphonally to choose mates on the slopes and at festivals.
2. **“Proposal” and “betrothal”** — a matchmaker is sent to propose; the betrothal gift is wine, meat, cloth and silver ornaments.
3. **“Not settling in the husband’s home”** — after the wedding the new wife stays at her mother’s home, moving to the husband’s after bearing children or when the agreed period ends.
4. **The “*yifan* Festival” and the marriage custom** — the *yifan* Festival (the ancestor-worshipping festival) is a common point for proposing and marrying.
5. **“Influenced by the Han and Zhuang”** — they practise antiphonal song and bowing in the hall, with traces of Han and Zhuang custom.
6. **“Return visit”** — returning home to visit the parents.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **From Tang-Song to Ming-Qing**: the marriage customs of the Mulao forebears (“Liao”, “Ling” and others) are recorded in histories and local gazetteers (the Qingyuan prefectural gazetteer and others).
- **Modern times**: living among the Zhuang and Han, the custom is much Sinicised.
- **Today**: the traditional custom survives in the villages of Luocheng.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Walking the slope, antiphonal song to make friends.
2. Proposal and betrothal.
3. Fetching the bride: welcoming guests with antiphonal song, bowing in the hall, feasting.
4. Not settling: a temporary stay at the mother’s home after the wedding.
5. Long-term residence in the husband’s home.
6. Return visit.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Luocheng**: the Mulao core area, with the custom fairly completely preserved.
- **Yizhou, Liucheng**: living among the Zhuang and Han, customs interpenetrate.
- **Relation to other peoples**: Mulao marriage custom is not同源 with the Han “Six Rites” and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: the betrothal gift (wine, meat, cloth, silver ornaments), song-fair ware.
- **Dress**: Mulao dress (indigo-dyed cloth).
- **Documents**: the bride-price list, the marriage contract.
