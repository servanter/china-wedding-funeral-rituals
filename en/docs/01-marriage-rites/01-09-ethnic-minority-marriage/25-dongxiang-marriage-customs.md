# Dongxiang Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Dongxiang are concentrated in Gansu Linxia (the Dongxiang Autonomous County and elsewhere) and parts of Xinjiang. They believe in Islam, and the wedding follows **the religious norms** with its own ethnic features.

**Core elements**:

1. **“Proposal” and “betrothal (sending the engagement tea)”** — the groom sends a matchmaker to propose; “engagement tea” (tea, sugar, cloth) is sent as the betrothal gift.
2. **The bride price (*mahr*)** — the betrothal gift is negotiated (silver, cloth, ornaments), called the “*mahr*”.
3. **“Reciting the Nikah”** — at the wedding the ahong is invited to recite the “Nikah” to witness the marriage.
4. **The wedding banquet** — a feast for guests (wine is forbidden).
5. **“Acknowledging the kin”** — the bride meets her parents-in-law and elders.
6. **“Return visit”** — returning home after the wedding.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Yuan and Ming**: the Dongxiang took shape in the Hezhou area of Gansu; Islam (with the menhuan Sufi orders) was central to community life.
- **Ming and Qing**: the mosque took charge of weddings and marriage documents.
- **Today**: the rites continue; town and country alike have the mosque take part in weddings.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Proposal (a matchmaker is sent).
2. Betrothal: sending the engagement tea and negotiating the bride price.
3. Nikah: the ahong witnesses the marriage.
4. Wedding banquet: a feast for guests.
5. Acknowledging the kin: the bride meets her parents-in-law and elders.
6. Return visit.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **The Dongxiang Autonomous County**: a concentrated community, with regular observances.
- **Other parts of Linxia and Lanzhou**: living among the Hui, Bonan and Salar, similar customs.
- **The Xinjiang Dongxiang**: adjacent to the Uyghur and Kazakh in custom.
- **Relation to other peoples**: among the Islamic-wedding peoples the details differ; not同源 with the Han “Six Rites”, and it must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: the engagement tea (tea, sugar, cloth), the bride price, banquetware.
- **Religious objects**: the *Quran*, the mosque, the *gongbei* (the Sufi saint’s tomb).
- **Documents**: the bride-price list, the marriage contract (the marriage certificate).
