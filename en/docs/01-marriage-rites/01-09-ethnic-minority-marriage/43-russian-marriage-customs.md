# Russian Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Russian are distributed across Yili, Tacheng, Altay and Urumqi of Xinjiang, and in the Ergun River area (Enhe, Shiwei) of Inner Mongolia. The marriage custom is marked by **the Orthodox church wedding, the "bread and salt" welcome and the song-dance banquet**.

**Core elements**:

1. **The Orthodox church wedding** — held in church, presided over by a priest; the couple wear wedding crowns and exchange rings.
2. **"Matchmaking" and "meeting"** — a "svakha" (matchmaker) brings the match together; the two sets of parents meet and view.
3. **"Bread and salt"** — welcoming the couple with bread dipped in salt is the highest Russian honour.
4. **Separate betrothal and wedding** — the betrothal ("svatanye") and the wedding are held on two separate occasions.
5. **"Accordion and tap-dance"** — the banquet is accompanied by the accordion, with Russian folk dancing.
6. **Festivals linked to weddings** — the "Paskha" (Easter) and such festivals are linked with the marriage custom.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Qing dynasty**: the Russian entered from Siberia and Russian territory; the custom came with Orthodox Christianity.
- **Modern period**: settlements formed in Yili and the Ergun area; church weddings and Russian banquets proceeded side by side.
- **Contemporary**: the traditional wedding is revived in ethnic-township festivals and cultural activities.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Matchmaking and meeting.
2. Betrothal (svatanye).
3. Church wedding (wearing wedding crowns, exchanging rings).
4. Welcome into the new home with the "bread and salt" rite.
5. Banquet song-and-dance (accordion, tap-dance).
6. Return visit.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Yili, Tacheng of Xinjiang**: Russian core areas, where the Orthodox wedding is best preserved.
- **Ergun of Inner Mongolia (Enhe, Shiwei)**: villages of Sino-Russian descendants, where custom blends Han and Russian.
- **Relation to other peoples**: Russian marriage custom does not share a common origin with the Han "Six Rites" and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: bread and salt, wedding crowns, rings, accordion, painted eggs.
- **Dress**: Russian long skirts, headscarves (bride), embroidered shirts.
- **Documents**: the marriage contract, the church-wedding registration.
