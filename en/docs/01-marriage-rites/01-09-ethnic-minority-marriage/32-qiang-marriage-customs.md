# Qiang Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Qiang are concentrated in Aba of Sichuan (Maoxian, Wenchuan, Lixian, Heishui and others). The marriage custom is marked by **the “*shibi* officiating”, the “flower night” antiphonal song and the “*za* wine**”.

**Core elements**:

1. **“Proposal” and “betrothal”** — a matchmaker is sent to propose; the betrothal gift is wine, meat, cloth and silver ornaments.
2. **The “flower night”** — the night before the wedding the bride’s family holds a “flower night”, with kin and friends singing antiphonally and dancing the *salang*.
3. **“The *shibi* officiating”** — a Qiang priest, the “*shibi*”, is invited to bless, and report to the ancestors and the white-stone god.
4. **“The *za* wine for guests”** — guests are treated with *za* wine (brewed from highland barley).
5. **“Equal weight on mourning and wedding”** — the Qiang “flower night” and *salang* belong to the same song-and-dance tradition as the “jump-funeral” of the mourning custom.
6. **“Return visit”** — returning home to visit the parents.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Pre-Qin**: Qiang marriage custom is recorded in the *Xunzi* and the *Lüshi chunqiu*.
- **Ming and Qing**: the local gazetteers (the Wenchuan county gazetteer, the Maozhou gazetteer) record the custom.
- **Modern times**: influenced by Han culture, the wedding also has Han observances.
- **Today**: the traditional custom is combined with festivals such as the “Qiang New Year”; after the 2008 Wenchuan earthquake, reconstruction and cultural protection went forward together.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Proposal and betrothal.
2. Flower night: antiphonal song and the *salang* before the wedding.
3. Fetching the bride: the *shibi* blesses, worships the ancestors.
4. The wedding: bowing in the hall, treating guests with *za* wine, song and dance.
5. Sacrificing to the white-stone god.
6. Return visit.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Maoxian, Wenchuan**: Qiang concentrated areas, with the *shibi* system and the song-dance tradition better preserved.
- **Lixian, Heishui**: living among the Tibetan and Han, the wedding has Tibetan and Han elements.
- **Relation to other peoples**: Qiang marriage custom is not同源 with the Han “Six Rites” and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: the *za* wine, the betrothal gift, the *shibi* ritual implements, the white stone (an object of worship).
- **Dress**: Qiang dress (the embroidered apron, the head-wrap).
- **Documents**: the bride-price list, the marriage contract.
