# She Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The She are distributed in the mountainous areas of Fujian, Zhejiang, Jiangxi, Guangdong and Anhui. The marriage custom is marked by **“antiphonal song”, “being a cousin-sister” (*zuo biao jie*) and the “phoenix dress”**.

**Core elements**:

1. **“Antiphonal song”** — song accompanies the whole marriage process (the “song ground”).
2. **“Being a cousin-sister”** — before the wedding the bride goes to her maternal uncle’s and paternal aunt’s homes to “be a cousin-sister” and sing antiphonally with her cross-cousins; a characteristic custom.
3. **“Proposal” and “betrothal”** — a matchmaker is sent to propose; the bride price centres on wine, meat, cakes and silver ornaments.
4. **The “phoenix dress”** — the bride wears the “phoenix dress” (phoenix crown, fringed blouse, coloured skirt), the mark of She wedding dress.
5. **“The man bows but the woman does not”** — at the bowing in the hall the She have the custom that “the man bows but the woman does not” (traditions differ; an ethnic feature).
6. **“Return visit”** — the homecoming visit.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Tang and Song**: the She forebears (“She people”) are recorded in histories, tied to migration and mountain settlement.
- **Ming and Qing**: the local gazetteers (the county gazetteers of Fujian and Zhejiang) record the custom.
- **Modern times**: long cohabitation with the Han, much Sinicisation of the custom, but the wedding songs and the “phoenix dress” keep the She character.
- **Today**: “She marriage custom” and “She folk songs” are on the intangible-heritage lists.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Making friends by antiphonal song.
2. Proposal and betrothal.
3. “Being a cousin-sister”: singing antiphonally with cousins before the wedding.
4. Fetching the bride: welcoming guests with song, bowing in the hall (the man bows but the woman does not).
5. Wedding banquet: antiphonal singing at the song ground.
6. Return visit.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Eastern Fujian (Ningde, Fu’an)**: the She core area, with the most typical wedding songs and phoenix dress.
- **Southern Zhejiang (Jingning, Lishui)**: living among the Han, the ceremony simplified.
- **Western Fujian, eastern Guangdong**: customs interpenetrate with the Hakka.
- **Relation to other peoples**: She custom is deeply fused with the Han, but antiphonal song and the phoenix dress are its own tradition and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: the bride price (wine, meat, cakes, silver ornaments), the phoenix crown, the fringed blouse, the coloured skirt.
- **Dress**: the She phoenix dress.
- **Documents**: the bride-price list, manuscripts of wedding songs, the marriage contract.
