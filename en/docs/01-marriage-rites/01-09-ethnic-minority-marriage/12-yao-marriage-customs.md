# Yao Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Yao have many branches (the Pan Yao, Bunu Yao, White-trouser Yao, Chashan Yao and others), distributed in Guangxi, Hunan, Guangdong, Yunnan and Guizhou. The marriage custom is marked by **choosing a mate by antiphonal song, blocking the door with wine, and “not settling in the husband’s home” in some branches**.

**Core elements**:

1. **“Choosing a mate by antiphonal song”** — young men and women make friends by song at festivals and in the song-hall.
2. **“Proposal” and “betrothal”** — a matchmaker is sent to propose; the bride price centres on silver ornaments, wine and meat.
3. **“Blocking-the-road song” and “door-blocking wine”** — at the fetching a door-blocking rite is set; one enters only by antiphonal song and drinking.
4. **“Not settling in the husband’s home”** (some branches) — the new wife stays at her mother’s home for a time after the wedding.
5. **The “*duojie*” coming-of-age rite** — among Yao men the “*duojie*” (initiation) is tied to marriage eligibility (some branches).
6. **“Uxorilocal residence is common”** — in some branches the live-in son-in-law keeps his surname and has status equal to a natural son.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Tang and Song**: names such as “Mo Yao” appear in histories; the marriage custom of the Yao forebears is recorded in local gazetteers.
- **Ming and Qing**: the local gazetteers and documents such as the *Guoshan bang* reflect the marriage custom and the group’s rules.
- **Modern times**: the customs of the branches are stably different.
- **Today**: the traditional custom survives in the villages, some items on the intangible-heritage list.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Choosing a mate by song**: meeting in the song-hall by antiphonal song.
2. **Proposal**: a matchmaker is sent to arrange the match.
3. **Betrothal**: the bride price is sent and the wedding day fixed.
4. **Fetching the bride**: blocking-the-road song, door-blocking wine.
5. **The wedding**: meeting the elders, feasting guests.
6. **Not settling / uxorilocal residence / return visit**: as the branch and family agree.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Guangxi Jinxiu (the Dayao Mountain)**: the Chashan, Pan and Ao Yao branches each have their own customs.
- **Guangxi Nandan (the White-trouser Yao)**: the wedding has other features.
- **Hunan (Jiangyong, Jianghua)**: living among the Han, the custom is much Sinicised.
- **Yunnan and Guizhou**: living among the Miao, Zhuang and Bouyei.
- **Relation to other peoples**: Yao marriage custom is not同源 with the Han “Six Rites” and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: silver ornaments, wine, meat, song-hall ware.
- **Dress**: Yao finery (the Pan Yao “Pan Wang” motifs, the White-trouser Yao’s white trousers).
- **Documents**: group documents such as the *Guoshan bang*, the bride-price list, the marriage contract.
