# Naxi Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Naxi are concentrated in Lijiang of Yunnan and in Diqing and Liangshan of Sichuan. The marriage custom blends **the Dongba faith (the Dongba officiates and chants scripture) with the Han ritual (a vestige of the Six Rites)**; the Mosuo people of the Lugu Lake area have their own “visiting system”.

**Core elements**:

1. **“Proposal” and “betrothal”** — a matchmaker is sent to propose; the betrothal gift is sent (tea, wine, rice, silver ornaments).
2. **“The Dongba chanting scripture”** — a Dongba priest is invited to chant for blessing and report to the ancestors.
3. **“Fetching the bride” and “bowing in the hall”** — fetched by bridal chair; bowing to heaven and earth and to the ancestors.
4. **The “heaven-worshipping” connection** — the Naxi “heaven-worshipping” rite is tied to the ideas of marriage and ancestor worship.
5. **The Mosuo “visiting system” (*azhu* / *axia*)** — historically the Lugu Lake area practised the “visiting” form of “men do not marry in, women do not marry out”, not belonging to the Han marriage system.
6. **“Return visit”** — returning home to visit the parents.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Tang and Song**: the Naxi forebears (“Moso”) are recorded in histories; the Dongba faith took shape between the Tang and Song.
- **Ming and Qing**: under the Mu tusi, Dongba culture flourished; after the “reform of the native chieftains into officials” in the Qing, Han cultural influence deepened, and the marriage system drew close to the Han rite.
- **Today**: the custom survives in Dongba-culture protection; the Mosuo visiting system has become an ethnographic focus.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Proposal and matching the marriage.
2. Betrothal: sending the betrothal gift and fixing the wedding day.
3. The Dongba chants scripture for blessing.
4. Fetching the bride: fetching by bridal chair, bowing in the hall, feasting guests.
5. Worshipping heaven and the ancestors: reporting to the ancestors.
6. Return visit.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **The Lijiang flatlands**: marked Han-rite features, with the Dongba rite running side by side.
- **Diqing (Sanba, Shangri-La)**: more of the Dongba culture preserved.
- **Sichuan (Yanyuan, Muli)**: living among the Yi and Tibetan, customs interpenetrate.
- **The Mosuo by Lugu Lake**: the visiting system under a matrilineal-family background.
- **Relation to other peoples**: Naxi custom fuses with the Han “Six Rites” but has a different basis, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Religious objects**: Dongba ritual implements, Dongba scriptures, the *Soul-road Painting* (used at funerals, but the wedding also has Dongba blessing).
- **Objects**: the betrothal gift (tea, wine, rice, silver ornaments), the bridal chair.
- **Documents**: the horoscope card, the betrothal-gift list, the marriage contract.
