# Tajik Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Tajik are concentrated in Tashkurgan Tajik Autonomous County of Xinjiang and are also distributed in Shache, Zepu and Yecheng. The marriage custom is marked by **the Islamic “Nikah”, blessing with sprinkled flour and the eagle dance with goat-grabbing**.

**Core elements**:

1. **The “Nikah”** — an ahong presides and recites the “Nikah” according to Islamic law, confirming the marriage.
2. **Blessing with “sprinkled flour”** — at the wedding flour is sprinkled on the couple, an ancient blessing survival.
3. **“Relics of fire-worshipping”** — on entering the husband’s home the bride circles and crosses fire, keeping traces of Zoroastrianism (the fire faith).
4. **The “three-day wedding”** — the wedding lasts three days, including the “Nikah” and the “lifting of the veil”.
5. **The “eagle flute and eagle dance”** — at the wedding they play the eagle flute and dance the eagle dance, with the sport of “goat-grabbing” and horse-racing.
6. **The “*kulita* cap and veil”** — the bride wears the embroidered “*kulita*” cap, her veil lifted on the third day.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **From the Tang dynasty**: the Tajik forebears (the “Jibin”, “Tashkurghan” and others) sat astride the Silk Road; Islam entered with the Road.
- **Ming and Qing**: the marriage custom of the Sarikol (today’s Tashkurghan) area is recorded in the *Xiyu tuzhi*.
- **Today**: the traditional wedding and the eagle dance of the Pamir Tajiks are valued in intangible-heritage protection.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Proposal and matchmaking.
2. Betrothal, sending the betrothal (cloth, ornaments, livestock).
3. Nikah (presided over by the ahong).
4. Fetching the bride, sprinkling flour, circling the fire.
5. Wedding banquet, eagle dance, goat-grabbing.
6. Lifting the veil on the third day, return visit.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Tashkurghan**: the Tajik core area, with the most marked custom.
- **Shache, Zepu, Yecheng**: the plain Tajiks, with much intercourse with the Uyghur.
- **Relation to other peoples**: Tajik marriage custom is not同源 with the Han “Six Rites” and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: the eagle flute, the frame drum, flour, ornaments, horse gear.
- **Dress**: the “*kulita*” cap, the embroidered head-scarf, the red skirt.
- **Documents**: the religious marriage contract, the gift list.
