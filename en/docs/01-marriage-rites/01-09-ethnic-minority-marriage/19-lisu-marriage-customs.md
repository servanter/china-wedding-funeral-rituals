# Lisu Marriage Customs

## 1. Definition

The Lisu are distributed in Nujiang, Lijiang and Diqing of Yunnan and in Liangshan of Sichuan. Pre-marital socialising is marked by **“courting the girls” and “antiphonal song”**; the wedding rites by **“archery” and “antiphonal song”**.

**Core elements**:

1. **“Choosing a mate by antiphonal song”** — young men and women make friends by song when gathering at festivals and in the villages.
2. **“Proposal” and “betrothal”** — a matchmaker is sent to propose; the bride price centres on wine, cloth and livestock (historically “cattle as bride price”).
3. **“Archery at the fetching”** — when the fetchers come to the bride’s home, her kin “welcome them with arrows” (symbolically).
4. **“Not settling in the husband’s home”** (some regions) — the new wife stays at her mother’s home for a time after the wedding.
5. **Christian influence** — some Lisu of Nujiang believe in Christianity; the wedding has a church rite (prayer, singing hymns).
6. **“Return visit”** — the homecoming visit.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Tang and Song**: names such as “Li-su” appear in histories.
- **Ming and Qing**: the local gazetteers (the Yongchang prefectural gazetteer, the Lijiang prefectural gazetteer) record the custom.
- **Modern times**: Christianity spread in Nujiang, forming two parallel wedding forms.
- **Today**: the traditional custom survives in the villages of Nujiang and Lijiang.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. Making friends by antiphonal song.
2. Proposal and betrothal (discussing the bride price).
3. Fetching the bride: welcoming with arrows, antiphonal song.
4. The wedding: feasting guests, singing and dancing.
5. Not settling (some regions).
6. Return visit.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Nujiang (Lushui, Fugong, Gongshan)**: marked Christian influence, with a church form of wedding.
- **Lijiang, Diqing**: living among the Naxi and Tibetan, customs interpenetrate.
- **Sichuan (Liangshan, Panzhihua)**: living among the Yi, similar customs.
- **Relation to other peoples**: Lisu marriage custom is not同源 with the Han “Six Rites” and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: the bride price (wine, cloth, livestock), the crossbow, the mouth-harp.
- **Dress**: Lisu dress (hemp cloth, silver ornaments).
- **Documents**: the bride-price list, the marriage contract; a church wedding has a prayer-rite text.
