# Elegies, Mourning Couplets and Mourning Banners

## 1. Definition

Three core documents arise during lying-in-state:

- **Elegy (prayer)**: the lament read at the offering, by the family or a writer.
- **Mourning couplet**: the condoler's antithetical couplet, hung at the hall's sides.
- **Mourning banner**: the condoler's whole cloth (or paper) with a eulogy, hung inside or out.

They differ in **author** (elegy by family, couplet/banner by guest), **place**, and **form**.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Elegy**: ancient. The *Wenxuan* collects "eulogy", "lament", "elegy" (Yan Yanzhi's "Master Tao", Lu Ji's "On Emperor Wu"); Han Yu's "To Twelfth Lang", Ouyang Xiu's "To Shi Manyin" are famous. Sincere feeling, fixed form.
- **Couplet**: from "elegy" + "antithesis". Elegy in Han; couplets flourished in Ming-Qing; the mourning couplet followed. Tone-balance and response required — the most literary.
- **Banner**: from ancient "silk writing", "gift of shrouds". Ming-Qing whole silk with a name; Republican paper; modern wreath and ribbon.
- **Tablet, banner**: "X forever", "riding the crane west" above the hall.
- Formats in the document-template section.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Write the elegy**: time, offerer, life/virtues, grief, "may you enjoy".
2. **Fix couplet/banner**: by guest/unit, proper wording and address.
3. **Place**:
   - Elegy: read by the offerer at the spirit, burned after.
   - Couplet: hall sides (upper right, lower left, by custom).
   - Banner: hall wall or yard; modern wreath ribbon.
   - Banner-tablet: above the hall, horizontal.
4. **Read and burn**: the elegy read, burned with paper ("burn the prayer").
5. **Register and thank**: in the gift-book; thank-you cards after.
6. **Remove**: burned at the grave, or kept.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **North**: silk banners "in pairs"; white (or yellow) paper couplets.
- **Jiang-Zhe**: literary couplets, allusion; white/blue/black cloth banners.
- **Min-Guang**: silk banners "X forever"; Teochew strict tone.
- **Taiwan**: intact couplet/banner system with a name table.
- **Ethnic minorities**: mostly no couplet/banner. Muslims none, but condolence money, scripture, escort; Tibetans *khata*, lamps, monks. **As a Chinese-literary product, not for other peoples.**

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Elegy (prayer)**: paper, burned after reading.
- **Couplet**: white/yellow paper, black, sides.
- **Banner**: silk or paper, named.
- **Banner-tablet**: horizontal, above.
- **Wreath, basket**: modern.
- **Gift-book, thank-you card**.
