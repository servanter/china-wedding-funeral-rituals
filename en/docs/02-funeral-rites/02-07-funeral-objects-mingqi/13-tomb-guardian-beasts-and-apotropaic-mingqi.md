# Tomb-Guardian Beasts and Apotropaic Mingqi

## 1. Definition

Tomb-guardian beasts and apotropaic mingqi are **mingqi placed in the grave to drive away evil, expel spectres and guard the dead soul and the living** — of the "yansheng" (subduing) class. Their common conceptual basis: the grave is dark, a gathering of ghosts and spectres, so it must be subdued by fierce beasts, spirits and town-objects.

**Main classes:**

| Term | Form | Age |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Tomb-guardian beast** | Crouching fierce beast (human-faced beast-bodied, beast-faced beast-bodied), one or two horns, winged | Warring States to Sui-Tang (especially Northern Wei–Tang) |
| **Bixie, Tianlu** | Winged divine beast, lion-like with horns | Han to Northern and Southern Dynasties |
| **Tomb-guardian figurine** | Warrior, heavenly-king, strongman (treading a little ghost) figurines | Northern Wei to Tang-Song |
| **Fangxiang (trail-opener)** | At the procession the "Fangxiang" leads; later becomes the paper "trail-opener" | Pre-Qin to modern |
| **Other subduing objects** | Stone-can-dare (shigandang), peach charm, mirror, five-colour stone, paper talisman | Long used |

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Pre-Qin**: the *Zhouli* ("Xia Guan") has the office of "**Fangxiang**": "in charge of covering with bear skin, four golden eyes, black coat and red skirt, holding the spear and raising the shield, leading the hundred servants in the seasonal *nuo* (exorcism), to search the chamber and drive the pestilence"; at the procession the Fangxiang leads — the earliest ritual form of the exorcist idea.
- **Warring States, Qin-Han**: Chu and Han tombs yield "tomb-guardian beast", "bixie", "tianlu" stone-carving and pottery; the chamber murals paint divine beasts.
- **Wei-Jin, Northern and Southern Dynasties**: the tomb-guardian beast and figurine appear in sets; the typical Northern Wei configuration is "a pair of tomb-guardian beasts + a pair of warrior figurines".
- **Sui-Tang**: the tomb-guardian beast (lion, tiger shape, long horns) and the heavenly-king figurine (treading a little ghost) become fixed — one of the Tang-tomb grade marks.
- **After the Song**: real tomb-guardian beasts lessen, replaced by the paper "trail-opener", the town-beast text in the land deed, talismans.
- **Modern**: some areas still have the paper trail-opener at the procession; contemporary tombs use stone lion and shigandang as town-objects.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Fangxiang leads**: at the procession (pre-Qin) a man dressed as Fangxiang leads; later replaced by the paper trail-opener.
2. **Place the town-object**: at burial place the tomb-guardian beast and figurine at the grave door, passage or before the coffin.
3. **Write the town-beast text**: in the grave or the land deed write the "**town-beast text**" — telling the underground gods and ghosts "do not invade, do not disturb", with phrases like "as the law commands" (strongly Daoist in colour).
4. **Seal the grave**: after placing the town-objects, seal the grave door.
5. **Contemporary continuation**: set stone lions, shigandang before the tomb, or place a small town-object by the ashes casket.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Central plains**: Northern Wei and Tang tombs yield concentrated tomb-guardian beasts and figurines, a complete sequence.
- **Jiangnan**: Six-Dynasties tombs have stone bixie (e.g. Nanjing, Danyang Southern-Dynasty tomb stone-carving).
- **North China**: the modern custom keeps the paper trail-opener.
- **Ethnic minorities**: some peoples use religious instruments, amulets, animal skulls to exorcise (e.g. the Tibetan "chemar", wind-horse; some peoples hang beast bones on the grave); their belief system differs from the Han subduing, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Tomb-guardian beast, bixie, tianlu**: stone, pottery, porcelain, winged and horned.
- **Tomb-guardian figurine**: warrior, heavenly-king, strongman figurines.
- **Town-beast text / relief-sorrow text**: carved stone, vermilion or ink writing in the grave, mostly with "urgent as the law commands", "heaven-earth envoy" and the like.
- **Paper town-objects**: trail-opener paper, talisman, peach charm.
