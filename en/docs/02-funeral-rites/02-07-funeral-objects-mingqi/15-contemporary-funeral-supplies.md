# Contemporary Funeral Supplies

## 1. Definition

Contemporary funeral supplies means the **objects used in funerals since the mid-20th century**; their composition has shifted from the traditional "coffin — mingqi" system to the "**funeral home — cremation — cemetery**" system, with traditional, Western and electronic supplies coexisting.

**Main classes:**

| Class | Representative | Relation to tradition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Encoffining gear** | Ashes casket, grave clothes, sanitary coffin (cremation coffin) | The ashes casket replaces the coffin's containing function |
| **Display** | Photo frame, black armband, white flower, mourning dress (mourning sleeve, mourning belt) | Black armband and white flower replace traditional mourning dress |
| **Mourning gifts** | Wreath, flower basket, elegiac couplet, hanging | Continues the modern elegiac gift |
| **Sacrifice goods** | Paper effigy, paper money, spirit coin, incense and candle, electronic offering | Paper effigy and spirit coin continue the burning custom |
| **Service goods** | Hearse, refrigeration coffin (ice coffin), body bag, rite goods | Industrialised, specialised products |
| **Electronic / network** | Online sweeping, electronic incense-candle, QR-code stele | Wholly new forms |

## 2. Historical Origins

- **From the 1950s**: funeral reform, advocating cremation and simple funerals; the cemetery and funeral-home system established.
- **1985** *Provisional Funeral Management Rules*, **1997** *Funeral Management Regulations*: set the cremation-zone and earth-burial-zone division and the funeral-supplies management rules.
- **Since the 1990s**: the funeral-service system matured; ashes caskets, ice coffins, fresh-flower wreaths spread; meanwhile paper effigy and spirit coin persist in villages.
- **Since the 2000s**: urban measures banning paper-money burning and spirit-coin sale; "electronic offerings", "online sweeping", "ecological burial" (tree, sea, flower-bed burial) arose.
- **Contemporary coexistence**: cities use black armband, white flower, fresh wreaths, ashes casket; villages and some lineage areas still see mourning dress, paper effigy, burning paper money.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Encoffin**: wash the body, dress in grave clothes, place in the sanitary (cremation) coffin or refrigeration coffin.
2. **Set the hall**: hang the portrait, set wreaths and couplets; kin wear black armband and white flower.
3. **Farewell ceremony**: the funeral home holds a farewell (silence, eulogy, flowers, last view).
4. **Cremate**: cremate the body, take the ashes into the casket (ashes casket).
5. **Place or bury**: keep the ashes in the ashes hall, or into the cemetery, or ecological burial.
6. **Sacrifice**: at Qingming, Zhongyuan, the death day sweep the cemetery, or sweep online.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Cities**: the funeral-home — cemetery system is complete; supplies standardised; mostly fresh flowers and black armband.
- **Villages**: traditional supplies (paper effigy, spirit coin, mourning dress, mourning fan) and funeral services coexist; "rear nourishment, thin burial" and old custom intertwine.
- **Min-Tai (Fujian-Taiwan)**: the "careful end, pursue the beginning" custom is strong; cemetery, hall sacrifice and paper effigy, gold paper coexist.
- **Ethnic minorities**: each people's funeral mode (sky, water, tree, fire, earth burial) differs vastly; their supplies (e.g. the Tibetan *tsa-tsa*, the Dai offering) are not the same system as the Han modern funeral supplies, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Encoffining gear**: ashes casket (wood, stone, jade, porcelain), sanitary coffin, grave clothes, body bag.
- **Display goods**: photo frame, black armband, white flower, mourning sleeve, mourning belt, mourning ribbon.
- **Sacrifice goods**: paper effigy, paper money, spirit coin, incense and candle, electronic incense-candle.
- **Documents**: death certificate, cremation certificate, obituary, eulogy, cemetery-plot certificate; online-sweeping page.
- **Management basis**: the *Funeral Management Regulations* and local funeral-management rules (including funeral-supplies production and sale rules).
