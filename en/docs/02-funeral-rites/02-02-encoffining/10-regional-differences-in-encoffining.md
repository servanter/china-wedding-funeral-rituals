# Regional Differences in Encoffining

## 1. Definition

This entry compares encoffining regionally across four dimensions: **time, rite, objects, taboos** (full records in the "Han regional funeral variants" entries; this is a synoptic contrast).

## 2. Historical Origins

- The classics give one model (gentleman: minor then major dressing the next day), but practice varies greatly by climate (south must encoffin fast), economy (coffin thickness), clan (waiting for kin) and religion (Buddho-Daoist depth).
- Ming-Qing gazetteers are the key comparative source. Hu Pu'an's *China-wide Customs Record* gathers many Republican-era field records, the basis for comparison.
- Modern cremation and town-country divides widen the regional gap.

## 3. Regional Comparison

**Time**

| Region | Encoffining time |
| --- | --- |
| North China | mostly the third day, "not past noon" |
| Jiangnan | one to three days; summer same or next day |
| Min-Tai | within 12–24 hours, "watch the hour" |
| Guangdong | one to three days, "not see daylight" |
| Hakka | about three days, for secondary burial |

**Rite**

- North: *kaiguang* before, descendant-nails by sons in turn.
- Jiangnan: minor/major on separate days, clear layers.
- Min-Tai: by the earth officer, Daoist chanting; a "soul-silk" after.
- Guangdong: linked with "buying water", "reporting the ground".
- Hakka: first burial simple, valuing later "bone-picking".

**Objects**

- Grain-bag, dog-stick: both north and south, different names/form.
- Min-Guang: more "road-pass" papers; "gold-bucket urn" (secondary-burial areas).
- Jiangnan: "gold under, silver on", mirror, scissors.
- North: "grain-bin", ingots in the coffin.

**Taboos**

- Shared: tears on the corpse, cats/dogs near it, clashing zodiac present, pregnant women seeing it.
- Differing: south fears "rain on the coffin", north fears "seeing stars" (night encoffining).

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- The above "regions" are Han areas, including Cantonese, Teochew, Hakka, Huizhou groups.
- **Ethnically**: encoffining does not exist for some peoples (Tibetan sky burial, Muslim swift burial), and differs for others (Miao log-coffin, Yao rock-coffin, Dong boat-coffin). To be recorded apart, not as "variant differences".
