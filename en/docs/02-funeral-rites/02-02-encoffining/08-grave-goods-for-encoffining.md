# Grave Goods for Encoffining

## 1. Definition

The items for encoffining are the various folk objects placed in or by the coffin at laying-out. They are not the classic "mingqi" but the materialisation of folk belief: each answers a need of the dead on the road to the underworld.

This entry records their form, use and underlying idea.

## 2. Historical Origins

- The classics set "mingqi" (made to be unusable) and "use-vessels" (the dead's own), per the *Liji* ("Tan'gong"): "mingqi, for the spirit".
- "Dog-beating stick", "grain-bag" and the like are not in the classics; they arose with folk underworld belief: the soul meets fierce dogs, hunger, thirst, bridges and rivers, so must be provided for.
- Ming-Qing gazetteers record them abundantly, named by place ("dog-beating stick", "dog-beating cake", "grain-bin", "bridge cake", "road-pass").
- Their common trait is a **practical logic solving concrete troubles**, in interesting contrast to the classic symbolic "mingqi not for use".
- Earth-burial areas keep them; cremation areas mostly dropped them.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Prepare**: the items, mostly by daughters and daughters-in-law.
2. **Grain-bag**: a red cloth bag of the five grains (wheat, millet, rice, bean, sorghum) in the hand or coffin corner — "food in the underworld".
3. **Dog-stick and dog-cake**: a stick (or willow) with cakes, in the hand or sleeve — "throw cakes, beat dogs".
4. **Bridge-cake / bridge-plank**: cakes and a symbolic plank — "cross the Naihe bridge with food and a board".
5. **Road-pass**: some areas a "road-pass" (paper with name and native place) as the "underworld pass".
6. **Mirror, scissors**: small, in the coffin — "against demons", "against evil".
7. **Beloved things**: books, pen, glasses, pipe, comb, jewelry by status.
8. **Check and record**: an elder verifies; some areas note in the account book.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **North China**: dog-stick, grain-bag, dog-cake most common, in pairs.
- **Jiangnan**: also bridge-cake, plank, mirror, scissors.
- **Min-Guang**: road-pass, "underworld guide", with written format.
- **Hakka (secondary burial)**: first burial simpler; a different set at bone-picking.
- **Ethnic minorities**: different logic — Yi tools and silver (to "keep working in the ancestor land"), Miao silver (wealth and group-mark), Tibetan sky burial and Hui swift burial none. **Each people's "after-death needs" differ, so do the items.**

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Grain-bag**: red cloth bag of five grains.
- **Dog-stick, dog-cake**: stick and cakes.
- **Bridge-cake, plank**: paper or dough.
- **Road-pass**: paper with the name.
- **Mirror, scissors, jade plugs**: against evil and to stop orifices.
- **Beloved things**: by status.
- **Account book, grave-goods list**: some families keep.
