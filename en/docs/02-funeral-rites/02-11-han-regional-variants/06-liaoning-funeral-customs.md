# Liaoning Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

Liaoning's Han mostly descend from "**braving the East Pass**" immigrants (mainly Shandong and Hebei), so their funeral customs inherit the **Shandong-Hebei type** and took shape in the Northeast environment; the clearest mark is "**bao miao · song jiangshui**". Main features:

1. **"Bao miao" and "song jiangshui"** — after death report at the Earth God temple; three times a day "send pulp-water" (send water and rice to the temple front), the typical mark of Northeast funeral custom.
2. **"Burn the great paper"** — the night before the procession or on a set day burn large amounts of paper effigies and money.
3. **"Smash the basin"** — the eldest son smashes the mourning basin, taking the mourning-master power.
4. **Three days lying-in** — usually three, or five, seven, by season and means.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Qing**: the ban slowly loosened; Shandong-Hebei immigrants poured in, bringing native customs.
- **Republic**: peak of "braving the East Pass"; the Northeast Han society took shape, customs formed.
- **Contemporary**: cremation spread; funeral homes centralise mourning; villages keep "bao miao", "song jiangshui" traces.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **First death**: change clothes, lay out, set the spirit; the filial son goes to the Earth God temple to "bao miao".
2. **Send pulp-water**: three times a day (or twice morning and evening) send water-rice to the temple front.
3. **Encoffin**: great encoffining, nail the lid.
4. **Keep vigil, receive**: the filial son kneels at the spirit, kin condole.
5. **Burn the great paper**: burn paper carriages and the bundle.
6. **Procession**: smash the basin, raise the coffin, send, bury.
7. **After burial**: round the mound, "fifth seven", "hundred days", "anniversary".

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Southern Liaoning (Dalian, Yingkou)**: near Shandong, customs like Jiaodong.
- **Western Liaoning (Jinzhou, Chaoyang)**: near Hebei, with "song panchan".
- **Northern and eastern Liaoning mountains**: village customs, simpler.
- **Other ethnicities**: Liaoning's Manchu, Mongol, Korean, Hui funerals each have systems, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: coffin, mourning dress, mourning basin, soul-guiding banner, paper effigies, pulp-water bucket.
- **Documents**: fuwen, funeral notice, gift list.
- **Local feature**: the Earth God temple (mostly gone, replaced by crossroads, temple site).
