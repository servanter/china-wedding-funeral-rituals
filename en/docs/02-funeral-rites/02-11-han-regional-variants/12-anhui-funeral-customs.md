# Anhui Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

Anhui funeral customs differ sharply north and south: **south-Anhui Huizhou** has a developed clan system, solemn and ordered funeral; **north Anhui** near Henan-Shandong, plain customs. Main features:

1. **Huizhou "hall sacrifice" and "dotting the lord"** — the hall and clan lead the funeral; writing and dotting the lord is solemn.
2. **"Doing the sevens" and "doing merit"** — south Anhui does the sevens strictly, with an altar; north Anhui simpler.
3. **"Carrying the coffin through the village"** — some Huizhou areas circle the village main road at the procession, to bid farewell to the whole village.
4. **"Road sacrifice" and "blocking the coffin"** — kin set a road sacrifice to block the coffin in grief.
5. **Clan-rule constraint** — Huizhou genealogies and house-rules expressly fix the funeral rites (degree, period, gifts).

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Ming-Qing**: Huizhou merchants peaked; Huizhou funeral stressed "fitting the rite", "ordered"; clan and hall bore the organising function.
- **North Anhui**: near the central plains; customs near Henan, Shandong.
- **Modern**: the merchants declined, funeral scale shrank; north Anhui's frequent wars made customs simpler.
- **Contemporary**: cremation promoted; funeral-home mourning; Huizhou old custom is recorded in clan villages and genealogies.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **First death**: cleanse, change, set the spirit, report death.
2. **Encoffin**: minor and great encoffining.
3. **Do the sevens**: an offering every seven days; "fifth seven" the weight.
4. **Open condolence and dot the lord**: set the mourning hall to receive; invite the great guest to dot the lord.
5. **Procession**: raise the coffin, road sacrifice, "carry the coffin through the village" (Huizhou), bury.
6. **After burial**: round the mound, "hundred days", "anniversary"; sweep at Qingming, midwinter at hall and grave.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Huizhou (Huangshan, Jixi, Shexian)**: most complete clan funeral; hall leads, genealogy archives.
- **South Anhui (Xuancheng, Chizhou)**: both Huizhou and Jiangnan colour.
- **Central Anhui (Hefei, Lu'an)**: north-south transition, moderate rites.
- **North Anhui (Fuyang, Bozhou, Suzhou)**: near Henan-Shandong; "bao miao", "song panchan" seen.
- **Other ethnicities**: Anhui's Hui funeral (Bozhou, Bengbu) forms its own system, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: coffin, mourning dress, paper effigies, tin-foil, mourning basin.
- **Documents**: obituary, thanks-note, gift list, genealogy house-rules, hall-sacrifice documents.
- **Clan goods**: hall, clan rules, charity fields (some bear funeral aid).
