# Jiangxi Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

Jiangxi funeral customs are marked by "**clan hall and Buddhist-Daoist rites equally stressed**"; central, south and northeast Jiangxi each have a face. Main features:

1. **"Doing the sevens" and "doing merit"** — do the sevens strictly, with monks and Daoists for "doing merit", "releasing the hungry ghosts".
2. **"Crying song" and "filial song"** — sing filial songs, crying songs while keeping vigil; central and west Jiangxi especially flourish.
3. **Hall leads the funeral** — in clan-developed areas the hall and clan chief lead; clan rules constrain the rites.
4. **"Burn paper effigies" and "bundle"** — before and after the procession burn paper effigies and the "bundle" (paper-money envelope writing the addressee).
5. **Secondary burial (some areas)** — south-Jiangxi Hakka areas have bone-picking re-burial.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Song-Ming**: Jiangxi was a Neo-Confucian stronghold (Zhu Xi, Lu Jiuyuan); the *Family Rituals* deeply influenced; clan system developed.
- **Ming-Qing**: gazetteers' "customs" record the rites in detail; south-Jiangxi Hakka secondary burial became custom.
- **Modern**: under war, funerals simplified.
- **Contemporary**: cremation promoted; funeral-home mourning; villages keep "doing the sevens", "fifth seven".

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **First death**: cleanse, change, set the spirit, report death.
2. **Encoffin**: minor and great encoffining.
3. **Keep vigil, sing filial songs**: kin keep the night, sing filial songs in grief.
4. **Do the sevens / do merit**: an offering every seven days, invite monks and Daoists to chant.
5. **Procession**: raise the coffin, road sacrifice, send, bury.
6. **After burial**: round the mound, "hundred days", "anniversary"; sweep at Qingming, Zhongyuan; south Jiangxi "picks bones" to re-bury.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Central Jiangxi (Nanchang, Fuzhou)**: moderate rites; filial songs and sevens together.
- **Northeast Jiangxi (Shangrao, Jingdezhen)**: near Huizhou and Zhejiang, stronger clan colour.
- **South Jiangxi (Ganzhou)**: Hakka area, secondary burial and clan custom clear.
- **West Jiangxi (Yichun, Pingxiang)**: near Hunan, with Hunan custom influence.
- **Other ethnicities**: Jiangxi's She funeral forms its own system, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: coffin, mourning dress, paper effigies, tin-foil, bundle, filial-song book.
- **Documents**: obituary, thanks-note, gift list, genealogy family-rite manuscript, bundle-envelope text.
- **Clan goods**: hall, clan rules.
