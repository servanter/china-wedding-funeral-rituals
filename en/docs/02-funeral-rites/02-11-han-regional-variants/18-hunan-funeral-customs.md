# Hunan Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

Hunan funeral customs are known for "**solemn rites, flourishing funeral songs**"; northeast and west Hunan each have a face. Main features:

1. **"Do the altar"** — invite Daoists for deliverance rites; the Changsha, Xiangtan area especially flourishes.
2. **"Sing the night song"** — on the vigil sing the night song (filial song); famous in Changsha, Yiyang etc.
3. **"Do the sevens" and "bow penance"** — do the sevens strictly, with monks and nuns bowing penance.
4. **"Burn the spirit house"** — burn the paper spirit house, paper people and horses.
5. **"Up the mountain"** — burial commonly called "up the mountain"; west-Hunan mountains have the "circle the coffin" custom.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Pre-Qin**: the Chu-land shamanism and soul-summoning tradition (*Chu Ci* "Summoning the Soul") deeply influenced.
- **Song-Ming**: the *Family Rituals* and Daoist rite ran parallel; gazetteers record "night song", "altar".
- **Qing**: gazetteers' "customs" record the rites in detail.
- **Contemporary**: cremation promoted; Changsha, Yiyang still keep the night song (some entered the intangible-heritage list).

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **First death**: cleanse, change, set the spirit, report death.
2. **Encoffin**: minor and great encoffining.
3. **Sing the night song**: keep the night, sing the night song to the drum.
4. **Do the altar**: invite Daoists for rites (three to five days).
5. **Procession**: raise the coffin, send, up the mountain.
6. **After burial**: round the mound, "fifth seven", "hundred days", "anniversary"; sweep at Qingming, Zhongyuan.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Central-north Hunan (Changsha, Yiyang, Xiangtan)**: night song, altar most flourish.
- **South Hunan (Hengyang, Chenzhou)**: near Guangdong, Jiangxi; with Hakka colour.
- **West Hunan (Xiangxi prefecture, Huaihua)**: Tujia, Miao, Dong gathered; Han custom influenced by "jump the mourning", "circle the coffin".
- **East Hunan (Zhuzhou, Liling)**: near Jiangxi, like customs.
- **Other ethnicities**: Tujia, Miao, Dong, Yao funerals form their own systems, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: coffin, mourning dress, spirit house (paper), paper money, ritual instruments, mourning drum.
- **Documents**: obituary, thanks-note, gift list.
- **Song books**: night-song, filial-song manuscripts.
