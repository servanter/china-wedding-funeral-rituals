# Inner Mongolia Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

Inner Mongolia's Han mostly descend from **Qing "Walk-West-Pass" immigrants** (mainly Jin and Shaanxi), so their funeral customs carry an obvious **Jin-Shaanxi base** (bao miao, song panchan, smash the basin, do the sevens), yet grow simpler in the grassland livelihood and Han-Mongol mixed living. Main features:

1. **Jin-Shaanxi base** — "bao miao", "song panchan", "smash the basin", "dot the lord" like Shanxi and Shaanxi.
2. **Practical and simple** — vast land and sparse people, nomadic livelihood, short mourning, simple rites.
3. **Han-Mongol blend** — some Han families' rites influenced by the Mongol "offering", "fire sacrifice" ideas.
4. **Winter "breaking frozen earth"** — in cold areas, earth burial needs burning firewood to thaw and dig the cave.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Qing**: opening the land beyond the pass, Jin-Shaanxi immigrants "walked west of the pass", forming Han settlements around Guihua (now Hohhot), Baotou and the Hetao.
- **Republic**: immigrants grew; village customs differed by native prefecture (in one village Jin-south and Shanbei customs coexisted).
- **Contemporary**: cremation and funeral-home system spread; villages keep "fifth seven", "anniversary" offerings.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **First death**: put on grave clothes, lay out the body, set the spirit.
2. **Report death (bao miao)**: the filial son goes to the Earth God temple or crossroads to report and burn paper.
3. **Encoffin**: great encoffining, nail the lid.
4. **Keep vigil, open condolence**: the filial son keeps vigil, receives.
5. **Procession**: smash the basin, raise the coffin, send.
6. **Bury and after**: round the mound, "fifth seven", "hundred days", "anniversary".

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Hetao and Guisui (Hohhot, Baotou)**: Jin-Shaanxi immigrants concentrated, Jin custom most clear.
- **East (Chifeng, Tongliao)**: near Liaoning, Jilin, strong Northeast-type colour.
- **West (Alashan, Ordos)**: neighbours Ningxia, Shaanxi, with Hui-Han mixed influence.
- **Other ethnicities**: the Mongol traditional funeral (exposure, cremation, stone-mound grave) and the Hui funeral each form their own system, not the Han custom, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: coffin, mourning dress, mourning basin, paper effigies, paper money.
- **Documents**: fuwen, funeral notice, gift list.
- **Local feature**: the firewood and digging tools for winter "breaking frozen earth".
