# Guangxi Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

Guangxi's Han mainly distribute in "**east Guangxi, southeast Guangxi (Yulin, Wuzhou, Guigang) and north Guangxi (Guilin, Liuzhou)**"; their funeral customs carry the triple influence of "**Cantonese, Hakka and Hunan-dialect areas**". Main features:

1. **"Do the sevens" and "hit the zhai"** — do the sevens strictly, with monks and Daoists for the zhai, releasing the hungry ghosts.
2. **"Secondary burial"** — southeast Guangxi Hakka and Cantonese-immigrant descendants do bone-picking re-burial.
3. **"Sing the filial song"** — sing the filial song on the vigil; central and north Guangxi see it.
4. **Seasonal sweeping at "Third of March" etc.** — runs parallel with the Zhuang, Yao and other peoples' seasonal customs.
5. **Multi-ethnic mixed influence** — Han custom and Zhuang, Yao, Dong customs penetrate each other.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Since Tang-Song**: central-plains and Guangdong, Hunan immigrants entered Guangxi in succession, forming Han settlements.
- **Ming-Qing**: east Guangxi clan system developed; gazetteers record the rites in detail; north Guangxi near Hunan, like customs.
- **Contemporary**: cremation promoted; funeral-home mourning; villages keep doing the sevens, secondary-burial traces.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **First death**: cleanse, change, set the spirit, report death.
2. **Encoffin**: minor and great encoffining.
3. **Keep vigil, sing the filial song**: kin keep the night.
4. **Do the sevens / hit the zhai**: an offering every seven days; invite monks and Daoists for rites.
5. **Procession**: raise the coffin, send, bury.
6. **After burial**: round the mound, "hundred days", "anniversary"; southeast Guangxi does bone-picking re-burial; sweep at Qingming, Third of March.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Southeast Guangxi (Yulin, Wuzhou, Guigang)**: near Cantonese; secondary burial, doing the sevens key.
- **North Guangxi (Guilin, Liuzhou)**: near Hunan; with Hunan custom.
- **South Guangxi (Nanning, Qinzhou, Beihai)**: both Cantonese and native colour.
- **West Guangxi (Baise, Hechi)**: fewer Han; mostly mixed with Zhuang, Yao.
- **Other ethnicities**: Zhuang, Yao, Dong, Miao, Mulao, Maonan funerals each form systems, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: coffin, gold pagoda (for bone-picking), mourning dress, paper effigies, paper money, ritual instruments.
- **Documents**: obituary, thanks-note, gift list, genealogy family-rite manuscript.
