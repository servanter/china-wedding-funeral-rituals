# Zhejiang Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

Zhejiang funeral customs are marked by "**solemn Buddhist-Daoist rites, vivid clan colour**"; the Zhejiang-east coast and Zhejiang-west mountains each have a face. Main features:

1. **"Doing merit"** — the Wenzhou, Taizhou area invites Daoists to "do merit" (deliverance rites), grand in scale, the mark of south-Zhejiang custom.
2. **"Doing the sevens" and "bowing penance"** — do the sevens strictly, mostly invite monks and Daoists to bow penance, release the hungry ghosts.
3. **"Road-head sacrifice"** — set a road sacrifice on the way, kin answer.
4. **Clan and hall** — Zhejiang-east clans developed; the funeral is run by the hall and clan chief, clan rules constrain the rites.
5. **"Returning to the mountain"** — burial is commonly called "returning to the mountain", reflecting mountain burial.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Song-Ming**: Zhejiang-east Neo-Confucianism and clan system developed; the funeral followed the *Family Rituals*; many genealogy and family-rite manuscripts survive.
- **Qing**: gazetteers' "customs" record the rites in detail; "doing the sevens", "doing merit" common.
- **Modern**: Ningbo, Wenzhou merchant groups influenced; funerals stressed show; coast towns turned new.
- **Contemporary**: cremation spread; "doing the sevens", "doing merit" still remain in south-Zhejiang villages.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **First death**: cleanse, change, set the spirit; "report death".
2. **Encoffin**: minor and great encoffining.
3. **Do the sevens**: an offering every seven days; "fifth seven" solemn.
4. **Do merit / bow penance**: invite Daoists or monks for deliverance rites.
5. **Procession**: road-head sacrifice, raise the coffin, send, return to the mountain.
6. **After burial**: round the mound, "hundred days", "anniversary"; sweep at Qingming, midwinter.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **South Zhejiang (Wenzhou, Taizhou)**: "doing merit" most flourishing, complex rites.
- **East Zhejiang (Ningbo, Shaoxing)**: strong clan colour, hall leads the funeral.
- **North Zhejiang (Hangzhou, Jiaxing, Huzhou)**: near south-Jiangsu customs; "doing the sevens" refined.
- **West Zhejiang (Quzhou, Lishui)**: mountain customs, mostly by village convention.
- **Other ethnicities**: Zhejiang's She funeral forms its own system (e.g. "doing merit", "merit songs"), and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: coffin, mourning dress, paper effigies, tin-foil, ritual instruments (hand-bell, wooden fish), merit-altar furnishings.
- **Documents**: obituary, elegiac statement, thanks-note, gift list, genealogy family-rite manuscript.
- **Clan goods**: hall, clan rules, funeral book.
