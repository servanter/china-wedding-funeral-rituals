# Jiangsu Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

Jiangsu funeral customs show **sharp north-south difference**: south Jiangsu (Suzhou, Wuxi, Changzhou) has complex rites and thriving Buddhist-Daoist rites; north Jiangsu (Xuzhou, Huai'an, Yancheng, Lianyungang) near Shandong-Henan-Anhui, plain customs. Main features:

1. **"Doing the sevens" and rites** — south Jiangsu does the sevens strictly, with monks and Daoists; north Jiangsu simplifies.
2. **"Xuan Juan"** — the Suzhou, Changshu area takes "xuan juan" (preaching the precious scrolls) as a funeral-specific form.
3. **"Open condolence" and "procession"** — set the mourning hall to receive; the procession sends under a "coffin canopy".
4. **"Tofu feast"** — the funeral feast is mostly vegetarian (like Shanghai).
5. **Water-town feature** — some water-town areas once had "boat burial", "water-route procession".

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Ming-Qing**: Suzhou, Yangzhou were economic-cultural centres; funeral customs refined, gazetteers record in detail.
- **Clan and charity estate**: south Jiangsu charity estates (Fan-family etc.) bore clan funeral relief; the estate rules set the funeral rites.
- **Modern**: under Shanghai's influence, south Jiangsu customs turned new.
- **Contemporary**: cremation spread; funeral-home mourning; villages keep "fifth seven", "hundred days", "anniversary" offerings.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **First death**: cleanse, change, set the spirit; "send the impermanence" (some areas).
2. **Report death**: obituary to kin, paste white paper at the door.
3. **Encoffin**: minor and great encoffining.
4. **Do the sevens**: an offering every seven days, chant.
5. **Open condolence**: receive, rite-master cant (south Jiangsu).
6. **Procession**: raise the coffin, send, bury.
7. **After burial**: round the mound, "fifth seven", "hundred days", "anniversary"; sweep at Qingming, midwinter.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **South Jiangsu (Suzhou, Wuxi, Changzhou)**: most complex rites; "xuan juan", "doing the sevens" refined.
- **Nanjing**: both north and south winds; "smash the basin" seen.
- **Central Jiangsu (Yangzhou, Taizhou, Nantong)**: both north-south, near Jiangnan but simpler.
- **North Jiangsu (Xuzhou, Huai'an, Yancheng)**: near Shandong-Henan, stresses "bao miao", "song panchan".
- **Other ethnicities**: Jiangsu's Hui (Nanjing, Yangzhou) funeral forms its own system, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: coffin, mourning dress, paper effigies, tin-foil, xuan-juan instruments.
- **Documents**: obituary, elegiac statement, thanks-note, gift list, charity-estate rules.
- **Trade goods**: charity estate, coffin shop, paper-effigy shop.
