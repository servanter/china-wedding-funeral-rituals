# Shandong Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

Shandong is the "**land of Confucius and Mencius**"; its funeral customs bear the deepest ritual tradition, known for "**stressing rite, stressing filial, stressing the earth**", and with "braving the East Pass" immigrants brought the whole rite to the Northeast. Main features:

1. **"Bao miao · send soup (send pulp-water)"** — the filial son goes to the Earth God temple to report, sends water-rice several times a day; the common mark of Shandong-Hebei and Northeast customs.
2. **Strong "peace in the earth" idea** — earth burial, the grave mound, rounding the mound are greatly valued.
3. **"Smash the basin, hold the banner"** — the eldest son smashes the mourning basin, holds the soul-guiding banner, marking mourning-master and succession.
4. **"Round the mound" and "fifth seven"** — three days after burial round the mound; "fifth seven", "hundred days", "anniversary" set the offering.
5. **"Send panchan"** — the night before the procession burn paper wealth, "sending panchan" for the dead soul.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Since pre-Qin**: the Qi-Lu land had flourishing ritual learning; the *Yili* and *Liji*'s shi-funeral rites cast a deep shadow on the folk.
- **Ming-Qing**: gazetteers' "customs" record "bao miao", "song panchan" in detail.
- **Modern**: immigrants brought Shandong custom to the Northeast (Liaoning, Jilin, Heilongjiang).
- **Contemporary**: cremation promoted, grave mounds levelled; villages still keep "rounding the mound", "fifth seven".

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **First death**: cleanse, change, lay the body in the main room ("lie the death").
2. **Report death and bao miao**: obituary to kin; go to the Earth God temple to report.
3. **Encoffin**: minor and great encoffining, nail the lid.
4. **Keep vigil, receive**: the filial son kneels at the spirit, kin condole.
5. **Send panchan**: burn paper carriages and wealth.
6. **Procession**: smash the basin, raise the coffin, send, bury.
7. **After burial**: round the mound on the third day; "fifth seven", "hundred days", "anniversary"; send winter clothes at the Cold-Clothes Festival.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Central Shandong (Jinan, Tai'an)**: ordered rites; bao miao, song panchan complete.
- **Jiaodong (Yantai, Qingdao)**: coast custom, with fisher features (sea-sacrifice traces).
- **Southwest Shandong (Jining, Heze)**: near Henan, stresses "doing the sevens".
- **Northwest Shandong (Dezhou, Liaocheng)**: near Hebei, like customs.
- **Other ethnicities**: Shandong's Hui funeral (Jinan, Linqing etc.) forms its own system, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: coffin, mourning dress (white robe and cap, hemp rope at waist), mourning basin, soul-guiding banner, paper effigies, "panchan" paper work.
- **Documents**: fuwen, funeral notice, gift list.
- **Local goods**: Earth God temple (mostly abolished, replaced by crossroads, temple site), grave mound and stone stele.
