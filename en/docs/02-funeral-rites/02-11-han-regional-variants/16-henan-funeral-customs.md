# Henan Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

Henan lies in the central plains; its funeral customs are seen as a type that "**preserves more ancient rite**", with "**keep vigil, bao miao, smash the basin, do the sevens**" as the backbone. Main features:

1. **"Keep vigil" and "sit the night"** — the filial son keeps vigil; kin sit the night with the spirit.
2. **"Bao miao"** — go to the Earth God temple (or City God temple) to report death.
3. **"Smash the basin, hold the banner"** — the eldest son smashes the mourning basin, holds the banner; the key of raising the coffin.
4. **"Do the sevens" strictly** — first seven to seven sevens, with "fifth seven" the weight.
5. **"Send winter clothes"** — on the first day of the tenth lunar month (Cold-Clothes Festival) go to the grave to send winter clothes; a central-plains custom.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Since pre-Qin**: Henan is the core region of the *Yili* and *Liji* rites; folk funeral custom casts a deep ritual shadow.
- **Northern Song**: the Bianjing (Kaifeng) custom recorded in the *Dongjing Menghua Lu*; "doing the sevens", "paper horses" can be traced.
- **Ming-Qing**: gazetteers' "customs" record the rites in detail.
- **Contemporary**: cremation promoted; funeral-home mourning; villages still keep bao miao, doing the sevens.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **First death**: cleanse, change, lay the body in the main room, set the spirit.
2. **Report death and bao miao**: obituary to kin; the filial son goes to the temple to report, burn paper.
3. **Encoffin**: minor and great encoffining, nail the lid.
4. **Keep vigil, sit the night**: the filial son keeps vigil; kin keep the night.
5. **Do the sevens**: an offering every seven days, fifth seven solemn.
6. **Procession**: smash the basin, raise the coffin, send, bury.
7. **After burial**: round the mound, "fifth seven", "hundred days", "anniversary"; sweep at Cold-Clothes, Qingming, Zhongyuan.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **East Henan (Kaifeng, Shangqiu)**: near Shandong, Anhui; bao miao, song panchan seen.
- **West Henan (Luoyang, Sanmenxia)**: near Shaanxi; stresses "doing the sevens" and the altar.
- **South Henan (Nanyang, Xinyang)**: near Hubei; with Chu custom influence (e.g. sit the night, sing filial songs).
- **North Henan (Anyang, Xinxiang)**: near Hebei, like customs.
- **Other ethnicities**: Henan's Hui funeral (Zhengzhou, Kaifeng, Nanyang) forms its own system, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: coffin, mourning dress, mourning basin, soul-guiding banner, paper effigies, tin-foil, winter clothes.
- **Documents**: fuwen, funeral notice, gift list.
- **Seasonal goods**: winter clothes, paper money, bundle.
