# Qinghai Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

Qinghai's Han are concentrated in "**the Huangshui region (Xining, Haidong)**", mostly early-Ming Jiangnan, Jianghuai immigrant descendants; their funeral customs preserve the Ming native-place remnant, yet are influenced by the Tibetan, Hui, Tu and Salar. Main features:

1. **"Do the sevens" and "do the altar"** — do the sevens strictly, with Daoists for rites.
2. **"Sing the filial song" and "chant scripture"** — sing the filial song on the vigil, with Buddhist chanting (Tibetan-Buddhist influence).
3. **"Earth burial main"** — the Huangshui valley mostly earth burial; graves by the mountain.
4. **"Shehuo and funeral run parallel"** — the Huangshui "shehuo" folk custom and the funeral both belong to village public activities.
5. **Multi-ethnic mixed living** — long mixed with Hui, Tibetan, Tu, Salar; customs penetrate.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Ming**: the Hongwu-era immigrants filling the frontier (Jiangnan, Jianghuai troops entering Qinghai) brought native funeral custom and the "shehuo" tradition.
- **Ming-Qing**: gazetteers (Xining prefecture gazetteer, county gazetteers) "customs" record the rites.
- **Modern**: hard transport kept old customs more.
- **Contemporary**: cremation gradually promoted; villages keep doing the sevens, anniversary.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **First death**: cleanse, change, set the spirit, report death.
2. **Encoffin**: minor and great encoffining.
3. **Keep vigil, sing the filial song**: kin keep the night, sing.
4. **Do the sevens / do the altar**: an offering every seven days; invite Daoists or monks to chant.
5. **Procession**: raise the coffin, send, bury.
6. **After burial**: round the mound, "hundred days", "anniversary"; sweep at Qingming, Zhongyuan.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Xining, Haidong (Huangshui)**: Han concentrated; Ming-immigrant custom clear.
- **Huangzhong, Datong**: neighbours Tibetan villages; with Tibetan influence.
- **Haixi, Hainan**: fewer Han; mostly mixed with Mongol, Tibetan.
- **Other ethnicities**: Tibetan (sky burial, stupa burial etc.), Hui (quick burial), Tu, Salar funerals each form systems, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: coffin, mourning dress, paper effigies, paper money, ritual instruments, scripture rolls.
- **Documents**: obituary, thanks-note, gift list, genealogy.
