# Sichuan Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

Sichuan funeral customs are marked by "**sit the night and sing the filial song, lavish rites, grand funeral feast**"; the west-Sichuan plain and east-Sichuan mountains differ a little. Main features:

1. **"Sit the night (beat the mourning fire)"** — on the vigil night kin gather, drum and sing the filial song, reciting to aid grief.
2. **"Do the altar"** — invite Daoists for deliverance rites, three, five, seven days.
3. **"Do the sevens" and "return of the sha"** — do the sevens strictly; old had the "return of the sha" (the dead soul returns home) taboo.
4. **"Funeral feast (ba-ba banquet)"** — the funeral sets seats; kin and villagers gather, sizable.
5. **"Up the mountain" and "look at the land"** — burial called "up the mountain"; mostly invite a geomancer to choose the land.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Pre-Qin**: Ba-Shu shamanism and soul-summoning tradition.
- **Ming-Qing**: the "Huguang filling Sichuan" immigrants brought Hubei, Hunan, Jiangxi, Guangdong customs, merging with native, forming the "Sichuan-school funeral custom".
- **Qing**: gazetteers' "customs" record the filial song, altar, funeral-feast in detail.
- **Contemporary**: cremation promoted; funeral-home mourning; villages still sit the night, do the sevens.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **First death**: cleanse, change, set the spirit, report death.
2. **Encoffin**: minor and great encoffining.
3. **Sit the night, sing the filial song**: kin keep the night, drum and sing.
4. **Do the altar / do the sevens**: invite Daoists for rites; an offering every seven days.
5. **Procession**: raise the coffin, send, up the mountain.
6. **After burial**: round the mound, "fifth seven", "hundred days", "anniversary"; sweep at Qingming, Zhongyuan.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **West-Sichuan plain (Chengdu, Deyang, Meishan)**: ordered rites; altar and feast both stressed.
- **East Sichuan (Dazhou, Guang'an)**: near Chongqing, like customs.
- **South Sichuan (Yibin, Luzhou, Zigong)**: with Yunnan-Guizhou influence.
- **North Sichuan (Mianyang, Guangyuan)**: with Shaanxi-Gansu influence.
- **Other ethnicities**: Qiang, Tibetan, Yi, Miao funerals each form systems, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: coffin, mourning dress, mourning drum, paper effigies (spirit house), paper money, ritual instruments.
- **Documents**: obituary, thanks-note, gift list.
- **Song books**: filial-song manuscripts.
