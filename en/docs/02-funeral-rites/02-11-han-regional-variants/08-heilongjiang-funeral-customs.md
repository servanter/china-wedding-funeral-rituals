# Heilongjiang Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

Heilongjiang's Han are mainly **late-Qing and early-Republic immigrants** (Shandong, Hebei, Liaoning), the funeral custom the **Northeast type**, with features from the severe cold. Main features:

1. **Bao miao · song jiangshui** — the core rite like Liaoning and Jilin.
2. **Winter "breaking frozen earth"** — digging the frozen cave needs burning firewood to thaw, hence the "roasting the ground" custom; also taboo on choosing winter burial.
3. **Short lying-in** — for cold and living conditions, mostly three days to procession.
4. **"Burn the great paper" and "song panchan"** — burn paper effigies and money.
5. **Reclamation and forest features** — state farms and forest farms' workers' funerals tend simple and collective.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Late-Qing land-opening**: immigrants poured in (braving the East Pass), bringing native Shandong-Hebei customs.
- **Republic**: commerce-town rise along the Chinese Eastern Railway; town mourning grew simple.
- **Contemporary**: cremation fully promoted; farms and forests mainly mourn at funeral homes; old custom remains in remote villages.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **First death**: change clothes, lay out, bao miao.
2. **Send pulp-water**: morning and evening water-rice to the temple front.
3. **Encoffin**: great encoffining, nail the lid.
4. **Keep vigil, receive**: the filial son kneels at the spirit.
5. **Burn the great paper**: burn paper effigies.
6. **Procession**: smash the basin, raise the coffin, send; in winter must first "roast the ground" to dig.
7. **After burial**: round the mound, "fifth seven", "hundred days", "anniversary".

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Harbin, Qiqihar**: town customs modernised, simple.
- **Songnen plain reclamation area**: farm workers' funerals collective, fewer old customs.
- **Greater and Lesser Khingan forests**: forest custom, simpler.
- **Other ethnicities**: Manchu, Korean, Mongol, Daur, Oroqen, Hezhen funerals each have systems, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: coffin (or cremation coffin), mourning dress, mourning basin, paper effigies, pulp-water bucket.
- **Local feature**: the firewood and tools for winter digging ("roasting the ground").
- **Documents**: fuwen, funeral notice, gift list.
