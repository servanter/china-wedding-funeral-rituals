# Hainan Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

Hainan's Han are mainly "**Minnan immigrant descendants**" (speak Hainanese, a Min dialect), so their funeral customs "**resemble Minnan**" yet have tropical-island features. Main features:

1. **"Do the decade / do the sevens"** — take the decade or seven as the sacrifice term; with monks and Daoists for the zhai.
2. **"Crying the funeral"** — kin grieve with the crying song; women's crying is especially prominent.
3. **"Secondary burial (bone-picking)"** — after earth burial years, pick the bones into the gold urn re-bury; same system as Fujian-Taiwan.
4. **"Parade the village" procession** — the procession circles the village road, to bid farewell to neighbours.
5. **Clan hall** — north Hainan (Haikou, Wenchang) has a developed clan and hall system.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Yuan-Ming-Qing**: Minnan people crossed to Hainan in succession, bringing Minnan custom; north Hainan Han settlements took shape.
- **Ming-Qing**: gazetteers (Qiongzhou prefecture gazetteer, county gazetteers) "customs" record the rites in detail.
- **Modern**: overseas Chinese (Hainanese in Nanyang) influence; funerals had "remittance-funded funeral" phenomena.
- **Contemporary**: cremation gradually promoted; villages still do the decade, bone-picking.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **First death**: cleanse, change, set the spirit, report death.
2. **Encoffin**: great encoffining, grave goods in the coffin.
3. **Do the decade / do the sevens**: an offering every decade (or seven days); invite monks and Daoists to chant.
4. **Procession**: raise the coffin, parade the village, send, bury.
5. **After burial**: round the mound, bone-picking re-burial; "hundred days", "anniversary"; sweep at Qingming.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **North Hainan (Haikou, Wenchang, Chengmai)**: strongest Minnan colour; clan halls developed.
- **East Hainan (Qionghai, Wanning)**: like customs.
- **South Hainan (Sanya, Lingshui)**: with native and Li influence traces.
- **West Hainan (Danzhou, Dongfang)**: dialect and custom more complex; the "Danzhou-speech" lineage.
- **Other ethnicities**: the Li, Miao (Hainan Miao) funerals form their own systems, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: coffin, gold urn (for bone-picking), mourning dress, paper effigies, paper money, ritual instruments.
- **Documents**: obituary, thanks-note, gift list, genealogy.
- **Clan goods**: hall, ancestral grave.
