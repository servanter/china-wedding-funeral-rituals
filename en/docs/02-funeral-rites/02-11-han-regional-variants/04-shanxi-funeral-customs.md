# Shanxi Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

Shanxi (Jin) funeral customs are known for "**ancient and deep rites, complex ceremony**"; the "great funeral" of rich Shanxi-merchant households especially stresses show. Main features:

1. **Long lying-in** — rich families stay the coffin up to "seven sevens" (49 days) or longer; the "coffin period" is solemn.
2. **Solemn "dotting the lord"** — invite a notable to write and dot the lord; the red-brush dotting is a grand rite of central Shanxi.
3. **"Sending lamps" and "burning paper"** — before and after the procession send lamps and burn paper along set routes, a night procession like a column.
4. **Wind music and opera** — hire wind-music troupes; rich families build a stage and sing ("funeral opera", "sacrifice opera").

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Ming-Qing**: Shanxi merchants ruled; central Shanxi (Taigu, Qixian, Pingyao) rich merchants showed family power by thick burial, forming the luxurious "central-Shanxi funeral custom".
- **Ritual basis**: the *Family Rituals* and local ritual books (covenants, genealogy house-rules).
- **Modern**: the merchants declined, the luxury faded; after the war simplified.
- **Contemporary**: cremation promoted; central and southern Shanxi folk still keep "fifth seven", "anniversary" offerings, rites trimmed.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **First death**: move the body, bathe, put on grave clothes, set the spirit.
2. **Report death**: obituary to kin and friends, paste white paper at the door.
3. **Encoffin**: minor and great encoffining, grave goods in the coffin.
4. **Lying-in and open condolence**: set the mourning shed, receive; rich families invite a rite-master to cant.
5. **Dot the lord**: invite the great guest to write and dot the lord.
6. **Procession**: smash the basin, raise the poles, send; "send lamps" to lead.
7. **After burial**: round the mound, "fifth seven", "hundred days", "anniversary"; at Cold-Food and Qingming go to the grave and add earth.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Central Shanxi (Taiyuan, Jinzhong)**: once the most luxurious; "dotting the lord", "lying-in" refined.
- **Southern Shanxi (Linfen, Yuncheng)**: ancient and deep rites, near Guanzhong customs.
- **Northern Shanxi (Datong, Xinzhou)**: near Inner Mongolia and Hebei, simpler.
- **Southeastern Shanxi (Changzhi, Jincheng)**: mountain areas, mostly by village custom, with "society-sacrifice" traces.
- **Other ethnicities**: Shanxi's Hui funeral forms its own system, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: coffin (stressing cypress, single plank), mourning dress, coffin cover, wind-music instruments, paper effigies.
- **Documents**: fuwen, funeral notice, gift list, genealogy family-rite manuscript, lord-dotting document.
- **Others**: spirit money, tin-foil, paper-effigy courtyards.
