# Shanghai Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

Shanghai funeral customs are a layering of **local (Songjiang, Jiading, Pudong) custom** and **immigrant (Ningbo, Subei, Guangdong etc.) custom**, and in the modern age were shaped by the urban funeral system, forming "**the Shanghai-school funeral custom**". Main features:

1. **"Great encoffining" and "doing the sevens"** — great encoffining is the key rite; "doing the sevens" is complete (first seven to seven sevens), with the "fifth seven" stressed.
2. **"Tofu feast"** — the funeral feast's main dish is tofu, hence "eating the tofu feast".
3. **Monk-Daoist rites and "Xuan Juan"** — invite monks and Daoists to chant; suburbs have the custom of singing "xuan juan" (preaching the precious scrolls).
4. **Modern funeral** — the guild-hall, funeral home and cemetery system first took shape here (e.g. the International Cemetery).
5. **"Lowering the grave" and "anniversary"** — after the ashes are placed in the cemetery, sweep at Qingming, midwinter, the death day.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Ming-Qing**: the customs of Shanghai county and Songjiang prefecture recorded in gazetteers; guild-halls (e.g. the Ningbo guild-hall) ran fellow-provincial funerals.
- **After the modern opening**: the concession funeral system, cemeteries and cremation were gradually introduced; newspaper obituaries and memorial meetings became common.
- **Contemporary**: the cremation and funeral-home system leads the country; customs trend to simple holding; "doing the sevens" is mostly simplified at the funeral home or home.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **First death**: cleanse, change, set the spirit.
2. **Report death**: obituary (old used white cards).
3. **Great encoffining**: into the coffin (or sanitary coffin), kin view.
4. **Do the sevens**: first seven, fifth seven, seven sevens set the offering and chant.
5. **Procession**: old carried the coffin to the grave; now a hearse to the funeral home.
6. **Cremate and lower**: place or lower the ashes casket in the cemetery.
7. **After burial**: sweep at Qingming, midwinter, death day; "hundred days", "anniversary" set the offering.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **City**: Shanghai-school custom, simple rites, funeral-home mourning main.
- **Pudong, Songjiang, Jiading**: local custom better kept; "doing the sevens", "xuan juan" seen.
- **Suburbs (Qingpu, Jinshan)**: mostly by village custom, with Suzhou, Jiaxing influence.
- **Other ethnicities**: Shanghai's Hui (Islamic funeral) and other peoples' funerals form their own systems, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: coffin (old), ashes casket, mourning dress (or black armband, white flower), paper effigies, tin-foil.
- **Documents**: obituary, elegiac statement, thanks-note, gift list.
- **Trade goods**: guild-hall charity estate, funeral home (modern new institution).
