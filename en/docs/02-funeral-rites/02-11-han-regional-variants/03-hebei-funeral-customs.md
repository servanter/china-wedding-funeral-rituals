# Hebei Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

Hebei (Ji) funeral customs belong to the **North-China type**, with "**three days of lying in state, peace in the earth**" as the basic frame; the rites are plain and the taboos dense. Main features:

1. **"Bao Miao" and "Song Panchan"** — after death go to the Earth God temple to "bao miao" (or "report death"), and the night before the procession burn "panchan" (paper wealth).
2. **"Smash the basin, hold the banner"** — the eldest son smashes the mourning basin and holds the soul-guiding banner, a symbol of succession and mourning-master status.
3. **Mourning dress by weight** — white robe and cap, hemp rope at the waist; kin get mourning cloth by near/far.
4. **"Round the mound"** — three days after burial round the mound, the first important node after lowering.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Ming**: Hebei was Zhili; the funeral notes mostly followed the *Ming Code* and the *Family Rituals*.
- **Qing**: Zhili customs recorded in the "customs" sections of prefecture/state/county gazetteers; the paper-effigy and bearing-house trades flourished.
- **Modern**: immigrants "braving the East Pass" took this type of custom to the Northeast (see Liaoning, Jilin, Heilongjiang).
- **Contemporary**: cremation promoted; plain areas ban earth burial, rites simplified to body-farewell and ashes-placing.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **First death**: put on grave clothes, lay out the body, set the spirit.
2. **Bao Miao**: the filial son goes to the Earth God temple (or crossroads) to report and burn paper.
3. **Encoffin**: great encoffining into the coffin, nail the lid.
4. **Keep vigil and open condolence**: the filial son keeps vigil, kneels in the shed to receive.
5. **Song Panchan**: the night before the procession burn paper carriages and wealth.
6. **Procession**: smash the basin, raise the coffin, send, bury.
7. **After burial**: round the mound, "fifth seven", "hundred days", "anniversary"; send winter clothes at the Cold-Clothes Festival.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Central Ji (Baoding, Shijiazhuang)**: more complex rites; "bao miao", "song panchan" complete.
- **Southern Ji (Handan, Xingtai)**: near Henan, stresses "doing the sevens".
- **Eastern Ji (Tangshan, Qinhuangdao)**: near the Northeast, has "sending pulp-water" etc.
- **Bashang (Zhangjiakou, Chengde)**: near Inner Mongolia, simpler, with Han-Mongol blending traces.
- **Other ethnicities**: Hebei's Hui, Manchu, Mongol funeral customs form their own systems, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: coffin, mourning dress, mourning basin, soul-guiding banner, paper effigies (panchan), paper money.
- **Documents**: fuwen, funeral notice, gift list, mourning-cloth distribution sheet.
- **Seasonal sacrifice**: the grave-sweeping documents of Qingming, Zhongyuan, Cold-Clothes.
