# Fujian Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

Fujian funeral customs are one of the regions where **the Han traditional funeral is best preserved**; clan, hall, monk-Daoist rites and the special "**doing the decade**" (zuo xun), "**picking the bones**" (shi gu) coexist. Main features:

1. **"Doing the decade"** — takes the "decade" (ten days) as the sacrifice unit (first decade to seventh decade); Minnan calls it "doing the decade", like the north's "doing the sevens" but its own system; the "**sixth decade**" is mostly offered by the daughter.
2. **"Master doing merit"** — invite Daoists (masters) for deliverance rites, grand.
3. **"Crying at the road-head"** — the daughter, hearing of death and returning home, cries a welcome at the road-head; unique to Minnan.
4. **"Bone-picking burial"** — a few years after first earth burial "pick the bones" (collect the bones), put in a gold urn and re-bury elsewhere; the common secondary burial of Fujian-Taiwan.
5. **"Paper house" and "burning treasury money"** — paper houses are fine; burn "treasury money" (the nether treasury).
6. **Hall and "entering the lord"** — after removing the dress, the spirit-lord enters the hall to be worshipped.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Tang-Song**: central-plains immigrants entered Fujian, bringing the ritual tradition; Minnan, Fuzhou lineages formed after the Song.
- **Ming-Qing**: the clan system highly developed; genealogies, family-rite manuscripts, hall system complete; customs recorded in gazetteers and genealogies.
- **Modern**: Minnan people crossed to Taiwan, taking "doing the decade", "picking bones" to Taiwan (see Taiwan).
- **Contemporary**: cremation promoted, but "doing the decade", "picking bones" remain in Minnan villages, Kinmen and Taiwan.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **First death**: cleanse, change, move the bed (move the body to the main hall), "crying at the road-head".
2. **Report death**: obituary to kin (white card).
3. **Encoffin**: great encoffining, grave goods in the coffin.
4. **Do the decade / do merit**: an offering every ten days; invite the master to do merit.
5. **Procession**: raise the coffin, road sacrifice, send; mourning kin hold the mourning staff.
6. **First burial and bone-picking**: earth burial, after years pick the bones, put in the gold urn.
7. **After burial**: after removing the dress "join the furnace", the spirit-lord "enters the hall"; sweep at Qingming, death day.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Minnan (Quanzhou, Xiamen, Zhangzhou)**: "doing the decade", "picking bones", "paper house" most typical; clan halls developed.
- **Fuzhou (east Fujian)**: elegant rites, the "doing ten decades" custom; the "Aojiu Festival" ties to filial care.
- **Putian-Xianyou**: unique customs, with "doing the sevens", "thanking grace".
- **North and west Fujian (Hakka)**: west-Fujian Hakka do secondary burial, strong clan colour.
- **Other ethnicities**: Fujian's She funeral forms its own system (e.g. "doing merit", "merit songs"), and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: coffin, gold urn (for bone-picking), mourning dress (hemp shirt, ramie shirt, white shirt), mourning staff, paper house, treasury money, spirit money.
- **Documents**: obituary (white card), thanks-note, gift list, genealogy family-rite manuscript, lord-entering document.
- **Clan goods**: hall, spirit-lord, clan rules.
