# Chongqing Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

Chongqing funeral customs belong to the "**Ba-Yu type**", marked by "**sit the night and sing the filial song, solemn rites**"; the mountain environment also shaped rites like "**carrying the coffin up the slope**". Main features:

1. **"Sit the night" and "beat the mourning fire"** — on the vigil night kin sit, drum and sing the filial song; Sichuan-Chongqing call it "sit the night".
2. **"Sing the filial song"** — sing for weeping, singing the dead's life and filial duty.
3. **"Do the altar"** — invite Daoists for deliverance rites, three to five days.
4. **"Do the sevens"** — first seven to seven sevens, with "fifth seven" the weight.
5. **Mountain-city feature** — the procession "climbs slopes and steps"; the carrying route mostly follows mountain paths; "looking at the land" is mostly on slopes.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Pre-Qin**: the Ba-land shamanism and the *Chu Ci* "Summoning the Soul" soul-summoning tradition influenced each other.
- **Song-Ming**: the *Family Rituals* and Daoist rite ran parallel; gazetteers record the filial song, altar.
- **Qing**: the "Huguang filling Sichuan" immigrants brought Hubei, Hunan, Jiangxi customs, merging with native.
- **Contemporary**: cremation promoted; funeral-home mourning; villages still sit the night, sing the filial song.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **First death**: cleanse, change, set the spirit, report death.
2. **Encoffin**: minor and great encoffining.
3. **Sit the night, sing the filial song**: kin keep the night, drum and sing.
4. **Do the altar / do the sevens**: invite Daoists for rites; an offering every seven days.
5. **Procession**: raise the coffin, send, up the mountain.
6. **After burial**: round the mound, "fifth seven", "hundred days", "anniversary"; sweep at Qingming, Zhongyuan.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Main city and near suburbs**: town customs simpler; funeral-home mourning main.
- **West Chongqing (Jiangjin, Yongchuan)**: near south Sichuan, like customs.
- **Northeast Chongqing (Wanzhou, Fengjie)**: near Hubei; with Chu custom influence.
- **Southeast Chongqing (Qianjiang, Xiushan)**: Tujia, Miao gathered; Han custom influenced by "jump the mourning", "circle the coffin".
- **Other ethnicities**: Tujia, Miao funerals form their own systems, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: coffin, mourning dress, mourning drum, paper effigies (spirit house), paper money, ritual instruments.
- **Documents**: obituary, thanks-note, gift list.
- **Song books**: filial-song manuscripts.
