# Guizhou Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

Guizhou's Han are mostly "**early-Ming garrison immigrants (tunpu people) and Ming-Qing immigrant descendants**"; the closed geography made them "**preserve more ancient rite**", and blend with local ethnic customs. Main features:

1. **"Tunpu ancient custom"** — the Anshun tunpu people (Ming-dynasty Jiangnan-immigrant descendants) have archaic funeral custom, rites near the Ming Jiangnan remnant.
2. **"Do the sevens" and "do the altar"** — do the sevens strictly, with Daoists for rites.
3. **"Circle the coffin"** — before and after the procession circle the coffin singing; mutually influenced by local ethnic custom.
4. **"Sing the filial song"** — sing the filial song on the vigil.
5. **"Block the coffin, road sacrifice"** — kin set a road sacrifice to block the coffin in grief.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Early Ming**: Zhu Yuanzhang's "transfer north, conquer south" stationed Jiangnan troops in Guizhou, forming tunpu settlements and unique folk custom.
- **Ming-Qing**: immigrants grew; gazetteers (Guizhou general gazetteer, prefecture/county gazetteers) record the rites.
- **Modern**: closed transport kept old customs more.
- **Contemporary**: cremation makes towns simpler; tunpu villages still keep old custom.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **First death**: cleanse, change, set the spirit, report death.
2. **Encoffin**: minor and great encoffining.
3. **Keep vigil, sing the filial song / circle the coffin**: kin keep the night, circle the coffin singing.
4. **Do the sevens / do the altar**: an offering every seven days; invite Daoists for rites.
5. **Procession**: raise the coffin, road sacrifice, send, bury.
6. **After burial**: round the mound, "hundred days", "anniversary"; sweep at Qingming, Zhongyuan.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Central Guizhou (Anshun, Guiyang)**: tunpu custom concentrated, ancient rite better kept.
- **North Guizhou (Zunyi)**: near Chongqing, Sichuan; with Sichuan-Guizhou colour.
- **East Guizhou (Tongren)**: near Hunan, like customs.
- **South and southwest Guizhou**: Han mixed with Bouyei, Miao, Dong; customs penetrate.
- **Other ethnicities**: Miao, Bouyei, Dong, Shui, Yi funerals each form systems, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: coffin, mourning dress, paper effigies, paper money, ritual instruments, mourning drum.
- **Documents**: obituary, thanks-note, gift list, genealogy.
- **Tunpu goods**: dixi masks (some funeral rites tie to dixi, by village).
