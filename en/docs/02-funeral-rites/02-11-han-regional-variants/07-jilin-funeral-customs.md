# Jilin Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

Jilin's Han are mainly "**braving the East Pass**" immigrants' descendants; the funeral custom is the **Northeast type**, basically the same as Liaoning and Heilongjiang, with "bao miao · song jiangshui" as the common mark. Main features:

1. **Bao miao · song jiangshui** — go to the Earth God temple to report, send pulp-water several times a day.
2. **Short lying-in** — short lying-in, taboo on long stay (winter may be a little longer).
3. **"Burn the great paper" and paper effigies** — burn paper people, horses, houses, cabinets.
4. **"Round the mound" and "fifth seven"** — three days after burial round the mound; "fifth seven", "hundred days", "anniversary" are the sacrifice terms.
5. **Korean-Han mixed influence** — some Han families' diet and rites influenced by Korean custom.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Qing**: Jilin was a forbidden land; after the mid-period Shandong-Hebei immigrants gradually entered; late-Qing land-opening, Han population surged.
- **Republic**: farming and forestry development, villages took shape, customs converged with Liaoning and Heilongjiang.
- **Contemporary**: cremation spread; funeral homes centralise mourning; villages still keep bao miao, song jiangshui.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **First death**: change clothes, lay out, bao miao.
2. **Send pulp-water**: morning and evening pulp-water at the temple front (or crossroads).
3. **Encoffin**: great encoffining, nail the lid.
4. **Keep vigil, receive**: the filial son kneels at the spirit, kin condole.
5. **Burn the great paper**: burn paper effigies.
6. **Procession and bury**: smash the basin, raise the coffin, send.
7. **After burial**: round the mound, "fifth seven", "hundred days", "anniversary".

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Central (Changchun, Jilin)**: town customs modernised early, simpler.
- **East (Yanbian, Tonghua)**: Korean-Han mixed; Han custom partly influenced by Korean.
- **West (Baicheng, Songyuan)**: near Inner Mongolia, with Han-Mongol blending traces.
- **Other ethnicities**: Korean, Manchu, Mongol, Hui funerals each form their own system, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: coffin, mourning dress, mourning basin, soul-guiding banner, paper effigies, pulp-water bucket.
- **Documents**: fuwen, funeral notice, gift list.
