# Tianjin Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

Tianjin funeral customs are known for "**sending a great funeral**" — in old times rich merchants and salt merchants gathered, funerals vied in display; the "**great funeral**" (huge insignia, paper effigies, monks and Daoists, bearer teams) is the marked label of Tianjin-weis folk custom. Main features:

1. **"Great funeral" insignia** — paper "civil officers" and "military officers", banners and umbrellas, gongs and drums all complete.
2. **"Jie San" and "Song Lu"** — on the third day Jie San, in the evening "Song Lu" (send paper effigies to the road-crossing to burn).
3. **"Blocking the coffin sacrifice"** — on the way, kin and friends set a road sacrifice to intercept; the filial son answers the rite.
4. **Monk-Daoist rites** — "holding the jiao", "releasing the hungry ghosts" prevailed, fitting the mart-town culture's love of martial and grand style.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Ming-Qing**: Tianjin was a grain-transport hub and salt centre; merchants gathered, funerals vied in luxury, forming "the wei-school funeral custom".
- **Late Qing–Republic**: concessions and Chinese town coexisted; Chinese and Western funeral customs met; newspaper obituaries and public memorials appeared.
- **Contemporary**: cremation spread, funeral homes centralise mourning; the "great funeral" custom vanished, some paper-effigy and road-sacrifice traces remain in suburbs and counties.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **First death**: move the body, bathe, put on grave clothes, set the spirit.
2. **Report death**: obituary, paste the door report.
3. **Encoffin**: great encoffining, grave goods in the coffin.
4. **Jie San, Song Lu**: on the third day Jie San, at night burn paper carriages and horses.
5. **Open the condolence**: set the shed to receive, monks and Daoists chant.
6. **Procession**: raise the poles, "blocking the coffin sacrifice", send to the grave.
7. **After burial**: round the mound, "fifth seven", "hundred days", "anniversary".

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Six city districts and old town**: old customs refined; paper effigies and insignia most complex.
- **Coast (Tanggu, Hangu)**: fishers and salt workers' custom, with traces of sea sacrifice.
- **Jizhou, Baodi and other counties**: near the capital countryside, simpler.
- **Other ethnicities**: Tianjin's Hui funeral (quick burial, wash with clean water, wrap in white cloth) differs from the Han, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: paper-effigy insignia, banners and umbrellas, coffin canopy, bearing gear, mourning basin, mourning dress.
- **Documents**: fuwen, funeral notice, gift list, elegiac couplets and hangings.
- **Trade goods**: bearing-house, shed-shop, mingyi-shop old products.
