# Hubei Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

Hubei funeral customs blend "**central-plains ancient rite and Chu-land shamanism**"; the clearest feature is "**sit the night and sing the funeral song**" ("jump the mourning", "sit the mourning"). Main features:

1. **"Sit the night" and "sing the filial song"** — on the vigil night kin sit, drum and sing the filial song; east and central Hubei flourish.
2. **"Beat the mourning drum"** — sing to the drum, singing the dead's life and filial tales.
3. **"Do the sevens" and "do the altar"** — do the sevens strictly, with Daoists for the altar.
4. **"Procession up the mountain"** — burial is called "up the mountain", reflecting hill-and-mountain burial.
5. **"Spirit house" paper effigy** — burn the paper spirit house, paper people and horses.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Pre-Qin**: the Chu-land shamanism and the *Chu Ci* "Summoning the Soul" soul-calling tradition deeply influenced.
- **Tang-Song**: the funeral-song and drum-music custom appears in literature; the *Jingchu Suishi Ji* records Jing-Chu customs.
- **Ming-Qing**: gazetteers' "customs" record "sit the night", "sing the filial song" in detail.
- **Contemporary**: cremation promoted; west and east Hubei villages still sing the filial song, beat the mourning drum.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **First death**: cleanse, change, set the spirit, report death.
2. **Encoffin**: minor and great encoffining.
3. **Sit the night, sing the filial song**: kin keep the night, drum and sing.
4. **Do the sevens / do the altar**: an offering every seven days; invite Daoists for rites.
5. **Procession**: raise the coffin, send, up the mountain (bury).
6. **After burial**: round the mound, "fifth seven", "hundred days", "anniversary"; sweep at Qingming, Zhongyuan.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **East Hubei (Huanggang, Huangshi)**: singing the filial song, sitting the night most flourish.
- **Central Hubei (Wuhan, Xiaogan, Jingzhou)**: both ancient rite and Chu custom.
- **Northwest Hubei (Xiangyang, Shiyan)**: near Shaanxi, Henan; stresses doing the sevens.
- **Southwest Hubei (Enshi, Yichang)**: Tujia etc. gathered; Han custom partly influenced by "jump the mourning".
- **Other ethnicities**: Tujia, Miao funerals form their own systems (e.g. the "Saying Er He" jumping mourning), and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Objects**: coffin, mourning dress, mourning drum, paper effigies (spirit house), paper money, ritual instruments.
- **Documents**: obituary, thanks-note, gift list.
- **Song books**: filial-song, funeral-song manuscripts.
