# Hour and Direction of the Procession

## 1. Definition

The **hour** and **direction** of the procession (sending forth the coffin) must both be chosen; both belong to the core of funeral auspicious dating.

**Hour**:
- Mostly choose "**chen hour**" (7–9 a.m.) or "yin hour", "mao hour" to raise the coffin, taking "rise and go early", "not travel by night".
- Taboo on "zhongshang hour", "empty-and-dead hour"; some areas taboo the afternoon procession ("not see off the setting sun").

**Direction**:
- The procession route taboo on "**turning back**", taboo on repeating the coming path.
- Taboo on the "**sha direction**" (zhongshang sha, year sha) — the coffin leaving the door must avoid the current evil direction.
- Once the coffin leaves the door, taboo on "**hitting the door frame, bumping the door post**".
- "**Carry the coffin without setting it down**" (it must not touch ground midway; touching is ill); "**do not turn the head**" (the mourners must not look back).

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Pre-Qin**: the procession rite appears in the *Yili* ("Ji Xi Li"); the choice of day first used "divining the day", "divining the dwelling".
- **Tang-Song**: the yinyang hour-choosing teaching spread; the choice of procession hour and direction grew fine.
- **Qing**: the *Xieji Bianfang Shu* systematically integrated the various spirits and directional do's and don'ts.
- **Since the near age**: villages still strictly keep the hour and direction; cities follow transport and funeral-home arrangements, the taboos fading.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Choose the hour**: the master reckons the hour, issues the sheet.
2. **Raise the coffin**: hit the door, smash the basin, the coffin leaves the house.
3. **Fix the route**: avoid the evil direction and taboo stretches, do not turn back.
4. **On the way**: carry the coffin without setting down, scatter paper money (buying the road), do not turn the head.
5. **Enter the pit**: by the fixed hour set the coffin, seal the grave.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **North China**: stresses "do not turn the head", "coffin not to ground", scattering paper money along the way.
- **Min-Tai (Fujian-Taiwan)**: the procession watches the "three-sha direction", taboo on opening the door to the "sha direction".
- **Jiangnan**: mostly choose an early-morning procession, the route surveyed beforehand.
- **Ethnic minorities**: some peoples fix the procession direction by divination (e.g. the Yi *bimo* casts wood lots, the Tibetan choose the hour by star-calculation), its basis not the same source as the Han spirit-sha system, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Auspicious-date basis**: the *Xieji Bianfang Shu* almanac, calendar.
- **Documents**: the day-sheet, the procession-route sheet.
- **Objects**: bearing gear, paper money, mourning basin, soul banner.
