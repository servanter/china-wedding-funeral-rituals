# Sha-Clash and Zodiac Taboos

## 1. Definition

One core reckoning in funeral date-choosing is the **stem-branch and zodiac clash-sha**: whichever zodiac clashes with the dead's day-pillar, the burial day, or the burial direction must **avoid** the corresponding rite.

| Item | Meaning | Response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Clash (zodiac clash)** | The twelve zodiacs clash (e.g. zi–wu, chou–wei) | The clashing one avoids encoffining, procession, burial |
| **Offending Tai Sui** | A zodiac clashing, punishing or harming the year's Tai Sui | Wear a "Tai Sui talisman" or avoid |
| **Three-Sha direction** | The direction of the year/month's "three sha" | Procession, breaking earth avoid it |
| **Zhongshang sha direction** | The direction the zhongshang day rules | The coffin leaving the door avoids it |
| **Year break, month break** | Clashing with the year/month branch | Taboo on burial, breaking earth |
| **Empty-and-dead** | The stem-branch "xunkong" | Taboo on burial |

**Avoidance**: the clashing one "**turns the body and does not see**" at the set moment, or steps outside the door; some areas have the clashing one "**tie red cloth**" (red dissolves); at the procession a "clash-dissolving talisman" is pasted on the mourning dress or coffin.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Han**: stem-branch date-choosing and the "Tai Sui" idea already appear in the *Day Book* and the *Lun Heng*.
- **Tang-Song**: the yinyang five-elements auspicious-date learning flourished; the zodiac clash-sha reckoning matured.
- **Qing**: the *Xieji Bianfang Shu* systematically arranged the spirits and directional do's and don'ts.
- **Since the near age**: villages strictly reckon by the almanac; cities mostly do not bother.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Report the zodiac**: the bereaved report each one's zodiac to the master.
2. **Reckon**: the master reckons the clashing zodiacs and hours.
3. **Avoidance arrangement**: the clashing ones avoid encoffining, procession, burial, or tie red cloth.
4. **Choose the direction**: procession, breaking earth avoid the three-sha, year-break directions.
5. **Dissolve the sha**: if needed, perform the "clash-dissolving" rite.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **North China**: taboo on the "zodiac-clashing" one approaching at encoffining.
- **Min-Tai (Fujian-Taiwan)**: the procession watches the three-sha direction; mourning kin of clashing zodiac step aside.
- **Jiangnan**: "clash-dissolving talisman", "tying red" are common remedies.
- **Ethnic minorities**: each people's divination and avoidance rules differ (e.g. by the people's own calendar, divination fixes the taboo), not the same system as the Han zodiac clash-sha, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Auspicious-date basis**: almanacs, calendars, the *Xieji Bianfang Shu*.
- **Objects**: talisman paper, red cloth, compass.
- **Documents**: the date-choosing sheet (listing clashing zodiacs and avoidance matters).
