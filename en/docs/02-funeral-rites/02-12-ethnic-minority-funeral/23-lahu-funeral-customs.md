# Lahu Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

The Lahu mainly distribute in Yunnan's Pu'er, Lincang, Xishuangbanna, Honghe. The burial is "**earth burial**" main; the village has a public graveyard.

**Core elements**:
- **"Mopa" (priest) presides** — the "mopa" (or "bimo") chants scripture, sends the soul, guiding the dead soul back to the ancestral home.
- **Funeral song and lusheng** — keep the night singing the funeral song; blow the lusheng to accompany the mourning.
- **"Call the soul" and "send the soul"** — send the soul for the dead, and "call the soul" for the living for peace.
- **Christian influence** — some Lahu (especially Lancang) believe in Christianity, holding church funerals.
- **Mourning taboos** — village taboos on farming and song-dance; after burial a purification rite.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Tang-Song to Ming-Qing** : the Lahu ancestors ("Luohei" etc.) burial appears in histories and gazetteers.
- **Modern** : Christianity and Theravada Buddhism spread in different areas; the funeral diverged.
- **Contemporary** : traditional and religious rites run parallel; village customs simplified.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Dying** : family keep watch; fire a gun to report death.
2. **Cleanse and dress** : cleanse; wear new clothes.
3. **Encoffin and keep the spirit** : into the coffin, set the spirit; the mopa chants, sing the funeral song.
4. **Send the soul** : chant the soul-sending scripture, guiding the dead soul home.
5. **Procession and bury** : choose a day to send; bury in the village public graveyard.
6. **After-burial sacrifice** : round the mound, "call the soul" to purify; sweep at festivals and the Torch Festival.

## 4. Regional and Subgroup Variants

- **Lahu Na (Lancang, Menglian)** : neighbour Dai, Wa; Buddhist and Christian influence coexist.
- **Lahu Xi (Lancang, Ximeng)** : branch customs more archaic.
- **Kucong (Jinping, Luchun etc.)** : originally a Lahu branch; customs with other traits.
- **Relation to other peoples** : the Lahu have no five-degree system; mourning is limited by taboos and term, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Instruments** : lusheng, elephant-foot drum (areas near Dai custom).
- **Religious objects** : mopa instruments, sacrificial vessels; cross, hymnal (Christian families).
- **Funeral goods** : coffin, sacrifice animal, wine, offerings.
