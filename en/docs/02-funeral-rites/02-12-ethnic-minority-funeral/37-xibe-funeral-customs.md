# Xibe Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

The Xibe are mainly concentrated in Liaoning (Shenyang, Tieling) and the Qapqal Xibe Autonomous County of Ili, Xinjiang. Earth burial is the main mode, and the funeral customs are shaped by **Shamanic belief, Manchu–Han ritual systems and Confucian mourning-garment views**.

**Core elements**:
- **Shaman sends the soul** — in old times a shaman was invited to send the soul; later lamas or Buddhist/Taoist clergy were usually invited to chant.
- **Mourning garments and the three-year mourning** — under Manchu–Han influence, there is a mourning-garment system and a mourning period.
- **"Burning paper" and "observing the seventh days"** — in recent times Han-style paper-burning, seventh-days and anniversary sacrifices are practised.
- **The old "blacken-the-face" custom** — during mourning some areas blackened the face with soot (signifying grief).
- **The westward-migration branch** — the Xinjiang Xibe, garrisoned there, preserve their language and customs better.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Late Ming–early Qing**: the Xibe lived in the Northeast, under the Khorchin and Manchu.
- **Qianlong reign of the Qing**: part of the Xibe were ordered westward to garrison Ili, forming the Qapqal settlement.
- **Modern**: contact with Manchu, Han and Uyghur; the funeral customs became plural.
- **Contemporary**: cremation is promoted; the traditional rites still survive in the settlement areas.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Dying**: family keep watch; inform the clan elders.
2. **Cleanse and dress**: cleanse the body, dress in new clothes.
3. **Encoffin and keep the spirit**: place in the coffin, set up the spirit; invite the shaman (or lama, Buddhist/Taoist clergy) to chant.
4. **Condolence and wake**: the filial son keeps the wake; relatives and friends offer condolences.
5. **Funeral and burial**: choose a day to send the burial, earth burial.
6. **After-burial sacrifice**: burn paper, observe the seventh days, the "hundredth day" and the "anniversary"; sweep tombs at Qingming and the New Year festival.

## 4. Regional and Subgroup Variants

- **Liaoning (Shenyang, Tieling)**: intermingled with Manchu and Han; customs are deeply sinicised.
- **Qapqal, Xinjiang**: the settlement area, preserving its language and some old customs better.
- **Relation to other peoples**: Xibe funeral customs are plural and blended, but Shamanic belief and the group memory of the westward migration are distinctive content that must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Religious objects**: shaman drum, shaman robe (old times); lama scriptures, ritual implements.
- **Funeral supplies**: coffin, mourning garments, paper money, offerings.
- **Documents**: obituary, clan genealogy (Xibe-language and Manchu-language family registers).
