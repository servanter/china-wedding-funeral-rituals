# Gelao Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

The Gelao are mainly concentrated in Guizhou (Wuchuan, Daozhen, Zunyi, Anshun, etc.) and parts of Guangxi and Yunnan. Earth burial is the main mode; historically they practised **rock burial (cave burial, hanging-coffin burial)**, with remains found in Guizhou, southern Sichuan and elsewhere.

**Core elements**:
- **Presided over by the "Zhangtan master" (duangong)** — the duangong (zhangtan master) performs the rites to deliver the dead.
- **Rock-burial tradition** — historically the Gelao practised cave burial; rock-coffin remains still exist in parts of Guizhou and Chongqing today.
- **The old "tooth-knocking" custom** — the ancient "tooth-knocking Gelao" knocked out the front teeth to bury with the dead (now abolished), much recorded in the literature.
- **Singing the funeral song and "jumping the wake"** — sing funeral songs and jump the wake during the vigil.
- **Influenced by Han culture** — observe the "seventh days" and "perform the Taoist rite".

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Pre-Qin to Tang–Song**: the "Liao people's" rock-burial customs are recorded in works such as the *Huayang Guo Zhi*.
- **Ming–Qing**: gazetteers (Zunyi Prefecture gazetteer, Anshun Prefecture gazetteer, etc.) record "Gelao" funeral customs and rock-coffin remains.
- **Modern**: intermingled with Han and Miao; the funeral customs are highly sinicised.
- **Contemporary**: rock-coffin remains have become objects of archaeological study; rural areas retain the traditional rites.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Dying**: family keep watch; fire crackers to announce the death.
2. **Cleanse and dress**: cleanse the body, dress in new clothes.
3. **Encoffin and keep the spirit**: place in the coffin, set up the spirit; the duangong performs rites.
4. **Perform the Taoist rite**: invite the duangong to perform rites such as "breaking the prison"; relatives and friends offer condolences and sing funeral songs.
5. **Funeral and burial**: choose a day to send the burial, earth burial.
6. **After-burial sacrifice**: round the grave, observe the seventh days and the "anniversary"; sweep tombs at Qingming and the New Year festival.

## 4. Regional and Subgroup Variants

- **Northern Guizhou (Wuchuan, Daozhen)**: settlement area, with a complete duangong system.
- **Central Guizhou (Anshun, Pingba)**: intermingled with Bouyei and Han; customs merge.
- **Southern Chongqing, southern Sichuan**: more rock-coffin remains.
- **Longlin, Guangxi**: intermingled with Zhuang and Yi; differing customs.
- **Relation to other peoples**: the Gelao have no five-degree mourning system; mourning is bounded by taboos and the mourning period, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Religious objects**: duangong ritual implements, scriptures, masks.
- **Funeral supplies**: coffin, paper effigies, paper money, sacrificial livestock, wine.
- **Remains**: rock coffins, stone coffins (archaeological remains).
