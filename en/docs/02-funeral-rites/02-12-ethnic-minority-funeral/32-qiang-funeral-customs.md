# Qiang Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

The Qiang gather in Sichuan's Aba (Mao, Wenchuan, Li, Heishui etc.). Traditionally "**cremation**" is the native burial mode; in modern times they also "**earth-bury**".

**Core elements**:
- **"Shibi" presides** — the Qiang priest "shibi" hosts the funeral, chants scripture to send the soul, chooses the day and ground.
- **Cremation** — after burning collect the ashes, jar-bury or scatter on mountains and rivers; the cremation flat is mostly near the village.
- **"Jump the mourning" and funeral song** — people circle to jump the mourning, sing the funeral song.
- **White-stone worship** — the Qiang white-stone worship ties to funeral sacrifice (e.g. set white stone on the grave).
- **"Qiang New Year" ancestor worship** — the Qiang New Year (the first day of the tenth lunar month) ancestor worship ties to the funeral ancestor-worship idea.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Pre-Qin** : the Qiang burial (cremation) appears in the *Xunzi* and *Lüshi Chunqiu*, an early document basis of the Qiang cremation tradition.
- **Ming-Qing** : gazetteers (Wenchuan county gazetteer, Maozhou gazetteer) record the funeral.
- **Modern** : under Han influence, more earth burial.
- **Contemporary** : after the 2008 Wenchuan earthquake, rebuilding and funeral protection went together.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Dying** : family keep watch; fire a gun to report death.
2. **Cleanse and dress** : cleanse; wear new clothes.
3. **Keep the spirit and chant** : lay the body, set the spirit; the shibi chants to send the soul.
4. **Jump the mourning and condole** : people jump the mourning, sing the funeral song; kin condole.
5. **Cremation (or earth burial)** : on an auspicious day cremate at the cremation flat, or earth-bury and build the tomb.
6. **After-burial sacrifice** : collect the bones to rest, set white stone on the grave; at the Qiang New Year and Qingming sweep.

## 4. Regional and Subgroup Variants

- **Mao, Wenchuan** : Qiang core area; the shibi system and cremation tradition better kept.
- **Li, Heishui** : neighbour Tibetan, Han; more earth burial.
- **Relation to other peoples** : the Qiang cremation tradition is not the same source as the Han earth-burial rite; mourning also does not take the five degrees, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Religious objects** : shibi instruments (staff, sheep-skin drum, ritual crown).
- **Funeral goods** : firewood (cremation), ashes jar, white stone, offerings, wine.
- **Documents** : oral scriptures, genealogy.
