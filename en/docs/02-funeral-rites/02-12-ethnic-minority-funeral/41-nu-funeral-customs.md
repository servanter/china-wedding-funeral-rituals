# Nu Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

The Nu are mainly concentrated in Nujiang, Yunnan (Lushui, Fugong, Gongshan) and Diqing (Weixi). Earth burial is the main mode, with cremation practised in some areas.

**Core elements**:
- **Priest ("Nima", "Yugu") presides** — the ethnic priest chants to send the soul, guiding the deceased back to the ancestral home.
- **"Jumping the wake" and funeral songs** — sing funeral songs through the night and form a circle to jump the wake.
- **Burial of production tools** — men are buried with bow and crossbow, knife; women with weaving tools and ornaments (some areas).
- **Christian influence** — some Nu (and the neighbouring Lisu) believe in Christianity and hold church funerals.
- **Mourning taboos** — the village forbids farming, song and dance; purification rites are performed after burial.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Tang–Song to Ming–Qing**: the Nu forebears ("Nu yi", etc.) and their burial customs appear in histories and local gazetteers (the Lijiang Prefecture gazetteer, etc.).
- **Modern**: Christianity spread in Nujiang, forming two parallel funeral customs.
- **Contemporary**: the traditional rites are preserved in the mountains; towns have grown simpler.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Dying**: family keep watch; fire a gun to announce the death.
2. **Cleanse and dress**: cleanse the body, dress in new clothes.
3. **Encoffin and keep the spirit**: place in the coffin (or wrap in a bamboo/wood mat), set up the spirit; the priest chants.
4. **Wake-jumping and condolence**: relatives and friends offer condolences, sing funeral songs and jump the wake.
5. **Funeral and burial**: choose a day to send the burial, earth burial (or cremation).
6. **After-burial sacrifice**: round the grave and conclude the soul-sending; sweep tombs at the New Year festival and Qingming.

## 4. Regional and Subgroup Variants

- **Gongshan (Nu branches)**: adjacent to the Derung and Lisu; customs interpenetrate.
- **Fugong, Lushui**: Christian influence is obvious, with church-form funerals.
- **Relation to other peoples**: the Nu have no five-degree mourning system; mourning is bounded by taboos and the mourning period, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Religious objects**: priest ritual implements, sacrificial vessels; cross, hymnal (Christian families).
- **Burial goods**: bow and crossbow, knife, weaving tools, ornaments.
- **Funeral supplies**: wooden coffin, bamboo mat, wine, sacrificial livestock.
