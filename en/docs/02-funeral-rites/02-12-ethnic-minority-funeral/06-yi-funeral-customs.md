# Yi Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

The Yi are mainly in Sichuan Liangshan, Yunnan, Guizhou, Guangxi; traditionally "**cremation is noble**" (especially typical of Liangshan Yi), some areas under Han influence earth-bury.

**Core elements**:
- **Bimo presides** — the priest "bimo" hosts the funeral, chants the *Guide Scripture* (*Soul-Sending Scripture*), sending the dead soul back to the homeland along the ancestral migration route.
- **Burn the body, collect the bones to bury** — after burning, collect the bones, scatter in the forest, or put in a pottery jar or wooden box to bury.
- **"Nimu", "sumi"** — religious persons of different levels each have duties in the funeral.
- **"Beat the ox" to host guests in mourning** — affines and clan members bring sheep and oxen to condole; the family treats with "tuotuo meat", sizable.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Han-Tang**: the *Man Shu* etc. record the southwest peoples' "cremation", matching Yi ancestors.
- **Yuan-Ming-Qing**: under the tusi system, nobles cremated with thick rites; gazetteers' "Yi custom" record the funeral.
- **Modern**: Liangshan Yi "clan" system joined the funeral; the rite is also a display of clan relations.
- **Contemporary**: Liangshan promotes cremation; traditional cremation and new funeral coexist; Yi scriptures like the *Guide Scripture* are sorted.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Dying**: family keep watch; notify the clan and affines.
2. **Cleanse and dress**: cleanse; wear new clothes (or wrap in the felt cape).
3. **Keep the spirit and chant**: the bimo chants the *Guide Scripture*, day and night; kin condole, sing the funeral song.
4. **Beat the ox to host**: slaughter oxen and sheep, treat the condolers with "tuotuo meat".
5. **Cremation**: choose an auspicious day to burn at the fixed village cremation ground ("cremation flat").
6. **Collect bones and place**: gather bones, scatter in the forest or jar-bury; old Liangshan custom scattered ashes in clean forest.
7. **After-burial sacrifice**: sacrifice at the anniversary or "ancestor festival"; the "nimu" sends the soul.

## 4. Regional and Subgroup Variants

- **Sichuan Liangshan**: cremation most typical; high clan participation.
- **Yunnan (Chuxiong, Honghe, Dali)**: cremation and earth burial coexist; mutual influence with Bai, Hani.
- **Guizhou (Bijie, Liupanshui)**: near Han; rites with Han traces.
- **Guangxi, Yunnan border**: mixed with Zhuang, Miao; customs penetrate.
- **Relation to other peoples**: the Yi "clan mourning" is not the same source as the Han five degrees, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Religious objects**: bimo instruments (spirit-fan, ritual hat, scriptures).
- **Classics**: Yi-language *Guide Scripture*, *Soul-Sending Scripture* manuscripts.
- **Funeral goods**: felt cape, wine, sheep and ox (sacrifice), cremation firewood.
- **Places**: cremation flat, ancestor-spirit cave (some branches enshrine the ancestor-spirit tube).
