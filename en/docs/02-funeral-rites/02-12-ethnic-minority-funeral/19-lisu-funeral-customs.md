# Lisu Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

The Lisu mainly distribute in Yunnan's Nujiang, Lijiang, Diqing and Sichuan's Liangshan. The burial is "**earth burial**" main; some areas "**cremate**".

**Core elements**:
- **"Nipa" presides** — the shaman "nipa" (or "bipa") chants scripture, sends the soul, along the ancestral migration route sending the dead soul home.
- **"Jump the mourning" and funeral song** — sing the funeral song all night, circle to jump the mourning.
- **Bury production tools** — men bury the crossbow and knife, women the weaving tool (some areas).
- **Christian influence** — some Nujiang Lisu believe in Christianity, holding church funerals (hymns, prayer).

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Tang-Song** : names like "Li Su" appear in histories.
- **Ming-Qing** : gazetteers (Yongchang prefecture gazetteer, Lijiang prefecture gazetteer) record the funeral.
- **Modern** : Christianity spread in Nujiang, forming two parallel funeral customs.
- **Contemporary** : cremation gradually promoted; village traditional rites remain.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Dying** : family keep watch; fire a gun to report death.
2. **Cleanse and dress** : cleanse; wear new clothes.
3. **Encoffin and keep the spirit** : into the coffin (or wooden coffin, mat), set the spirit, sing the funeral song.
4. **Send the soul** : the nipa chants the soul-sending scripture, guiding by the ancestral migration route.
5. **Procession and bury** : choose a day to send; earth burial (or cremation).
6. **After-burial sacrifice** : round the mound, sacrifice ancestors; sweep at festivals and Qingming.

## 4. Regional and Subgroup Variants

- **Nujiang (Lushui, Fugong, Gongshan)** : clear Christian influence; church-form funerals exist.
- **Lijiang, Diqing** : neighbour Naxi, Tibetan; customs penetrate.
- **Sichuan (Liangshan, Panzhihua)** : neighbour Yi; customs alike.
- **Relation to other peoples** : the Lisu have no five-degree system; mourning is limited by taboos and term, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Religious objects** : nipa instruments, sacrificial vessels; cross, hymnal (Christian families).
- **Burial goods** : crossbow, knife, weaving tool, silver ornaments.
- **Funeral goods** : wooden coffin, mat, wine, sacrifice animal.
