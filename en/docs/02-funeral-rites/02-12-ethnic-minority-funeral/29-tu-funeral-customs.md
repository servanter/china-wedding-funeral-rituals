# Tu Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

The Tu gather in Qinghai's Huzhu, Minhe, Datong and Gansu's Tianzhu. The traditional burial is "**cremation**" main (especially Huzhu, Minhe), in modern times partly turned to "**earth burial**".

**Core elements**:
- **Tibetan-Buddhist influence** — invite lamas to chant for deliverance; the funeral bears Tibetan-Buddhist colour.
- **"Benbenzi" and other folk faith** — the Tu folk religion ("benbenzi", "fala") joins the funeral in some areas.
- **Cremation** — after burning, collect the ashes, jar-bury or scatter on mountains and rivers.
- **Mourning taboos** — village taboos on song-dance; kin have a mourning term and dress marks (e.g. wear clothes inside-out, tie a white belt).
- **"Wutu"** — the Tu "jump the wutu" exorcism (year-end) shares its exorcism idea with funeral taboos.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Yuan-Ming** : the Tu ("Tu ren") formed in the Huangshui region; Tibetan Buddhism and folk faith ran parallel.
- **Ming-Qing** : gazetteers (Xining prefecture gazetteer, Nianbo county gazetteer) record the funeral.
- **Modern** : living with Han, Tibetan, Hui; customs mutually influenced.
- **Contemporary** : cremation and earth burial run parallel; religious rites simplified.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Dying** : family keep watch; invite lamas to chant.
2. **Cleanse and dress** : cleanse; wear new clothes.
3. **Keep the spirit and chant** : lay the body, set the spirit; lamas chant for deliverance.
4. **Cremation or earth burial** : choose a day to send; the cremator prepares firewood to burn; the earth-burier coffins and buries.
5. **After-burial sacrifice** : collect the bones to rest, chant for transfer; sweep at festivals and Qingming.

## 4. Regional and Subgroup Variants

- **Huzhu, Minhe** : clear cremation tradition; solemn lama chanting.
- **Datong, Tianzhu** : neighbour Tibetan, Han; more earth burial.
- **Relation to other peoples** : the Tu custom is deeply under Tibetan-Buddhist influence; not the same source as the Han five degrees, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Religious objects** : lama instruments, scriptures, butter-lamp.
- **Funeral goods** : coffin or firewood (cremation), ethnic dress, offerings.
- **Documents** : deliverance scriptures, genealogy.
