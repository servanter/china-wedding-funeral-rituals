# Naxi Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

The Naxi gather in Yunnan's Lijiang and Diqing, Sichuan's Liangshan. They traditionally "**cremate**" (Lijiang Naxi), and in modern times mostly turned to "**earth burial**"; the Mosuo by Lugu Lake have their own burial trait.

**Core elements**:
- **"Dongba" presides** — the Dongba priest hosts the funeral, chants the *Soul-Sending Scripture* and the *Spirit-Road Picture* scriptures, pointing the dead soul's way home.
- **"Spirit-Road Picture"** — the Naxi Dongba-religion scroll used at funerals, depicting the dead soul's road to heaven, hung in the mourning hall.
- **"Orere" funeral song and dance** — sing the funeral song and dance all night.
- **"Heaven sacrifice" and "ancestor worship"** — the Naxi "heaven sacrifice" grand rite ties to the ancestor-worship idea.
- **Cremation to earth burial** — since the Qing under Han influence, Lijiang turned to earth burial and built tombs.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Tang-Song** : the Naxi ancestors ("Mosuo") appear in histories; the Dongba religion formed between Tang and Song.
- **Ming-Qing** : under the Mu tusi, Dongba culture flourished; after the Qing "reform replacing the tusi with officials", Han influence deepened and the funeral changed.
- **Modern** : Dongba rites and Han-style funerals ran parallel.
- **Contemporary** : Dongba classics listed in the *Memory of the World Register*; funeral studies deepened.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Dying** : family keep watch; fire a gun or blow a horn to report death.
2. **Cleanse and dress** : cleanse; wear new clothes (some in ethnic dress).
3. **Keep the spirit and offer** : set the mourning hall, hang the *Spirit-Road Picture*; the Dongba chants.
4. **Dongba rite** : "send the soul", "open the funeral" liturgy; sing the funeral song, dance the mourning dance.
5. **Cremation or earth burial** : Lijiang modern mostly earth burial; other areas cremate.
6. **After-burial sacrifice** : hold the "ancestor worship" rite; sweep at Qingming, New-Year, the heaven-sacrifice season.

## 4. Regional and Subgroup Variants

- **Lijiang flatland** : earth burial main; Dongba rites and Han-style funerals parallel.
- **Diqing (Sanba, Shangri-La)** : Dongba culture better kept.
- **Sichuan (Yanyuan, Muli)** : neighbour Yi, Tibetan; customs penetrate.
- **Mosuo by Lugu Lake** : cremate; with matrilineal-family funeral traits.
- **Relation to other peoples** : the Naxi have no five-degree system; mourning is limited by taboos and term, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Religious objects** : Dongba instruments (staff, spirit-board, five-banner crown), *Spirit-Road Picture*, Dongba scriptures.
- **Funeral goods** : ethnic dress, offerings, wine.
- **Documents** : Dongba scriptures (pictograph manuscripts), obituary, genealogy.
