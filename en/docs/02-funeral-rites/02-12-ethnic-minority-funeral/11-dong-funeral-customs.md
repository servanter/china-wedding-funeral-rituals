# Dong Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

The Dong distribute in the border areas of Guizhou, Hunan, Guangxi. The burial is "**earth burial**" main; some areas keep "**keeping the coffin awaiting burial**" (the coffin stays outside the village or on a slope, until an auspicious year/day to bury).

**Core elements**:
- **"Song-master" sings the funeral song** — the song-master leads the funeral song (the Dong grand song is also used at the funeral), singing instead of crying.
- **"Sacrifice to Sa" and village-god faith** — "Sa Sui" (the Sa goddess) is the Dong's highest god; the funeral ties to the village-god sacrifice.
- **"Tread the song-hall" and "block the road"** — at sending the coffin and welcoming guests there is the "block the road" antiphonal song.
- **Han influence** — some areas do the "sevens", "do the altar".

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Tang-Song**: the Dong ancestors ("Liao", "Dong") burial appears in histories.
- **Ming-Qing**: gazetteers (Guizhou general gazetteer, Liping prefecture gazetteer) "customs" record the rites; the Dong drum-tower, village-gate and other public buildings tie to the funeral.
- **Modern**: mixed with Han, Miao; customs blended.
- **Contemporary**: cremation promoted; villages keep the funeral song, keeping-the-coffin-awaiting-burial.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Dying**: family keep watch; fire a cannon or blow the lusheng to report death.
2. **Cleanse and dress**: cleanse; wear new clothes (or ethnic dress).
3. **Encoffin and keep the spirit**: into the coffin, set the spirit; the song-master leads the funeral song.
4. **Condole and "block the road"**: kin condole; at the village mouth "block the road" antiphonal song to welcome guests.
5. **Procession and bury**: choose a day to send, earth burial; some areas "keep the coffin awaiting burial", choose another day to bury.
6. **After-burial sacrifice**: sweep at Qingming, Zhongyuan, New-Year; the "sacrifice to Sa" and other village-god sacrifices run parallel.

## 4. Regional and Subgroup Variants

- **Southeast Guizhou (Liping, Congjiang, Rongjiang)**: Dong core area; the funeral song ties to drum-tower culture.
- **Southwest Hunan (Tongdao, Jingzhou)**: mixed with Miao, Han; customs penetrate.
- **North Guangxi (Sanjiang, Longsheng)**: near Zhuang, Yao; varied.
- **Relation to other peoples**: the Dong have no five-degree system; mourning is limited by taboos and term, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Instruments**: lusheng, pipa, "ox-leg" zither, drum.
- **Funeral goods**: ethnic dress, wine, glutinous rice, sacrifice animal.
- **Public buildings**: drum-tower, village-gate, wind-rain bridge (tying to funeral gathering, seeing off).
- **Documents**: oral funeral song, "kuan" words (the Dong "kuan" is customary law, including funeral norms).
