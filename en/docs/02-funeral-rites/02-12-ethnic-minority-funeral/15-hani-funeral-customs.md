# Hani Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

The Hani mainly distribute in Yunnan's Honghe, Puer, Yuxi, Xishuangbanna etc. The burial is "**earth burial**" main; some areas cremate.

**Core elements**:
- **"Mopi" (beima) presides** — the priest "mopi" chants scripture, sends the soul back to the northern homeland of the ancestral migration ("sending the soul" matches the Hani migration memory).
- **"Call the soul" and "send the soul"** — "call the soul" and "point the road" for the dead, and hold the "village-gate" rite to isolate the unclean.
- **Sing the funeral song and "jump the mourning"** — sing the funeral song on the vigil; some areas circle the coffin dancing.
- **"Long-street feast" etc. public activity** — village public rites tie to the funeral.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Tang-Song**: the Hani ancestors ("He Man" etc.) appear in histories.
- **Ming-Qing**: gazetteers (Lin'an prefecture gazetteer) record their funeral; the terrace-rice society's village rites stably inherit.
- **Modern**: influenced by the Dai (Southern Buddhism), Yi custom.
- **Contemporary**: cremation promoted; villages keep the mopi-led soul-sending rite.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Dying**: family keep watch; fire a gun or beat a drum to report death.
2. **Cleanse and dress**: cleanse; wear new clothes (or ethnic dress).
3. **Encoffin and keep the spirit**: into the coffin, set the spirit; the mopi chants.
4. **Send the soul and condole**: the mopi chants the "soul-sending scripture"; kin condole, sing the funeral song.
5. **Procession and bury**: choose a day to send, earth burial; the grave mostly in the near forest.
6. **After-burial sacrifice**: round the mound; "ancestor sacrifice"; sweep at festival, the June festival (Kuzhazha) etc.

## 4. Regional and Subgroup Variants

- **Honghe (Yuanyang, Honghe, Luchun)**: Hani core area; mopi system complete.
- **Puer, Yuxi (Mojiang, Yuanjiang)**: mixed with Yi, Dai; customs penetrate.
- **Xishuangbanna, Lancang**: under Dai Southern-Buddhist influence; rites with Dai traces.
- **Relation to other peoples**: the Hani have no five-degree system; mourning is limited by taboos and term, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Religious objects**: mopi instruments, scripture (oral).
- **Funeral goods**: coffin, ethnic dress, wine, glutinous rice, sacrifice animal.
- **Public facilities**: village-gate, "moqiu" ground (village public ground).
