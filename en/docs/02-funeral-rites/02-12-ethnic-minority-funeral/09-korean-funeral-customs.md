# Korean Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

The Korean people mainly distribute in Jilin, Heilongjiang, Liaoning; their traditional funeral "**is deeply influenced by the *Family Rituals***", the closest among the peoples to the Confucian mourning-dress system, while keeping their own traits (e.g. the "bier song").

**Core elements**:
- **Confucian funeral system** — has the rites "first passing, dressing, minor encoffining, great encoffining, assuming the dress, minor *xiang*, major *xiang*, *dan*", practising the "three-year mourning".
- **Mourning dress** — wear hemp mourning dress; the mourning grades mark near/far.
- **"Bier" and bier song** — a wooden bier (spirit-carriage) at the procession; the bearers sing the "bier song".
- **Sacrifice rites** — on the death-day hold the "death-day sacrifice"; on festivals hold the "tea rite" and "grave sacrifice".
- **Earth burial main** — modern mostly earth burial; now cremation and ashes-hall are main.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Joseon period**: the *Family Rituals* was the state rite model; the funeral highly institutionalised, and came with immigrants to China's Northeast.
- **Ming-Qing to modern**: Koreans moved to the Northeast; the funeral and the family-rite text transplanted together.
- **Contemporary**: cities mainly mourn at the funeral home; traditional dress rites much simplified, but family rites like "sacrifice" and "tea rite" continue.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **First passing**: move to the main room ("move to the proper bed"), test the breath, summon the soul ("fu").
2. **Dressing and minor encoffining**: cleanse, put on grave clothes, minor encoffining.
3. **Great encoffining and assuming the dress**: into the coffin; kin assume the dress by grade.
4. **Procession**: choose a day to send; the bier goes first; bearers sing the "bier song".
5. **Bury**: earth burial (or cremation), build the mound.
6. **After-burial sacrifice**: hold "first, second, third condolence" and "end of wailing", then minor *xiang*, major *xiang*, *dan*, remove the dress.
7. **Seasonal sacrifice**: on the death-day the "death-day sacrifice"; on festivals the "tea rite"; at Qingming, Mid-Autumn the "grave sacrifice".

## 4. Regional and Subgroup Variants

- **Yanbian Korean Autonomous Prefecture**: concentrated; more traditional custom kept.
- **Heilongjiang, Liaoning Korean areas**: mixed with Han; rites simplified.
- **Urban Koreans**: mourn at the funeral home; family sacrifice main.
- **Relation to other peoples**: Koreans and Han both belong to the Confucian mourning tradition, but the dress detail differs from the "five degrees", and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Burial gear**: coffin ("longevity wood"), bier (spirit-carriage).
- **Mourning dress**: hemp mourning dress, mourning belt.
- **Sacrifice objects**: offering table, vessels, spirit-tablet ("god-master", "paper placard").
- **Documents**: obituary, family-rite manuscript, sacrificial text, genealogy ("clan genealogy", "family record").
