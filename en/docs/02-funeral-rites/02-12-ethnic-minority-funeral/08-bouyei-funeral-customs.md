# Bouyei Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

The Bouyei distribute in south and southwest Guizhou and parts of Yunnan, Sichuan. The burial is "**earth burial**" main, generally doing "**secondary burial**" ("pick the bones" into a jar re-bury).

**Faith and rites**:
- **"Bumo" (shaman) presides** — the bumo (priest) chants scripture, chooses the day, sends the soul; the Bouyei have native script and scripture manuscripts.
- **"Fell the ox" (do the zhai)** — some areas "fell the ox" (slaughter for the dead) for the dead, an important rite.
- **Sing the filial song and "jump the mourning"** — sing the filial song on the vigil; some areas do the "turn-the-ground" dance.
- **Han influence** — the "sevens", "hundred days", "anniversary" are common; invite Daoists, monks for rites.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Tang-Song**: the Bouyei ancestors ("Liao" etc.) burial appears in histories.
- **Ming-Qing**: gazetteers (Guizhou general gazetteer, Xingyi prefecture gazetteer) "customs" record the rites; the "fell the ox" custom much recorded.
- **Modern**: mixed with Han, Miao; customs blended.
- **Contemporary**: cremation promoted; villages keep doing the zhai, secondary burial.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Dying**: family keep watch; fire a cannon to report death.
2. **Cleanse and dress**: cleanse; wear new clothes (or ethnic dress).
3. **Encoffin and keep the spirit**: into the coffin, set the spirit; invite the bumo to chant.
4. **Do the zhai, fell the ox** (some areas): slaughter the ox for the dead; kin share.
5. **Procession and bury**: choose a day to send; earth burial.
6. **Pick bones re-bury**: after years pick the bones into a jar, re-bury.
7. **After-burial sacrifice**: sweep at Qingming, Zhongyuan, New-Year; "hundred days", "anniversary" set the offering.

## 4. Regional and Subgroup Variants

- **South Guizhou (Qiannan prefecture)**: Bouyei concentrated; the do-the-zhai, fell-the-ox custom seen.
- **Southwest Guizhou (Xingyi, Anlong)**: mixed with Miao, Yi; customs penetrate.
- **Central Guizhou (Guiyang, Anshun)**: near Han; higher sinicisation.
- **Yunnan (Qujing, Wenshan)**: near Zhuang, Zhuang branches' custom.
- **Relation to other peoples**: Bouyei mourning is limited by taboos and term, not taking the five degrees, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Burial gear**: coffin, bone-picking pottery jar.
- **Religious objects**: bumo instruments, scripture manuscripts, bronze drum.
- **Funeral goods**: paper effigies, paper money, ox (sacrifice), wine, glutinous rice.
- **Documents**: obituary, scripture manuscripts, genealogy.
