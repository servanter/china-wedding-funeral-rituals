# Shui Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

The Shui gather in south Guizhou (Sandu, Libo, Dushan) and parts of Guangxi, Yunnan. The burial is "**earth burial**" main; some areas keep "**keeping the coffin awaiting burial**".

**Core elements**:
- **"Shui script" choosing the day** — the Shui have their own script and classic "Shui Shu" (held by the "Shui Shu master"); the funeral day-choosing and ground-choosing both follow the Shui Shu.
- **"Shui Shu master" presides** — the Shui Shu master hosts the choosing of the auspicious and the rites; also invites the "shigong" for the altar.
- **Sing the funeral song and "open the charge"** — keep the night singing the funeral song; the rite has "open the charge" (calling and sending the soul).
- **"Respect Xia" and "Duan festival"** — the Shui "Duan festival" (New-Year) worships ancestors, akin to the funeral ancestor-worship idea.
- **Han-Zhuang influence** — do the "sevens", "hundred days", "anniversary"; secondary burial too.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Tang-Song** : the Shui ancestors ("Liao" etc.) appear in histories.
- **Ming-Qing** : gazetteers (Dushan prefecture gazetteer, Sanhe county gazetteer) record the funeral; the Shui Shu documents inherited among the folk.
- **Modern** : living with Han, Bouyei, Miao; customs blended.
- **Contemporary** : the Shui Shu was listed in the *Memory of the World Register* (document heritage); the funeral day-choosing custom remains.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Dying** : family keep watch; fire a cannon to report death.
2. **Cleanse and dress** : cleanse; wear new clothes.
3. **Encoffin and keep the spirit** : into the coffin, set the spirit; invite the Shui Shu master to choose the day.
4. **Do the altar / open the charge** : the shigong does rites, sings the funeral song, "open the charge" to send the soul.
5. **Procession and bury** : choose a day to send; earth burial; some areas keep the coffin awaiting burial, choose another day to bury.
6. **After-burial sacrifice** : round the mound, do the sevens, "anniversary"; at the Duan festival and Qingming worship ancestors.

## 4. Regional and Subgroup Variants

- **Sandu, Libo** : Shui core area; the Shui Shu inheritance most complete.
- **Dushan, Duyun** : living with Han, Bouyei; customs deeply sinicised.
- **Guangxi (Nandan, Huanjiang)** : neighbour Zhuang, Maonan; customs penetrate.
- **Relation to other peoples** : the Shui have no five-degree system, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Classics and documents** : Shui Shu (Shui-script copy), obituary, genealogy.
- **Religious objects** : shigong instruments, sacrificial vessels.
- **Funeral goods** : coffin, sacrifice animal (ox, pig), wine, glutinous rice.
