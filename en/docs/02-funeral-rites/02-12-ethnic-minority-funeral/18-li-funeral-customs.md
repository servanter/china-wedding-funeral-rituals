# Li Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

The Li are the native people of Hainan Island, with dialect branches "Ha, Qi, Run, Meifu, Sai" etc. The burial is "**earth burial**" main; villages mostly have a public graveyard.

**Core elements**:
- **Public graveyard** — each clan or village has a fixed graveyard, zoned by branch and family.
- **"Aoya" and daogong preside** — the clan elder "Aoya" and the daogong host the funeral, choosing the day, sending the soul.
- **Sing the funeral song** — women sing the funeral song ("crying the death"), telling the dead's life.
- **"Make the ghost" and sacrifice** — after burial "make the ghost" (sacrifice) for the dead, tying to ancestor worship.
- **Taboos** — in mourning, village taboos (no farming, no song-dance, no wedding); the dead's things mostly buried or discarded.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Pre-Qin to Tang-Song** : the Li ancestors (Louyue descendants) burial appears in histories and archaeology (e.g. urn coffins, slab tombs).
- **Ming-Qing** : gazetteers (Qiongzhou prefecture gazetteer, county gazetteers) "Li custom" record the funeral.
- **Modern** : living with Han and Miao, some customs sinicised (e.g. doing the sevens, burning paper).
- **Contemporary** : funeral reform advanced; village traditional burial simplified.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Dying** : family keep watch; fire a gun or beat a drum to report death.
2. **Cleanse and dress** : cleanse; wear new clothes (or ethnic dress).
3. **Encoffin and keep the spirit** : into the coffin, set the spirit; sing the funeral song.
4. **Condole and sacrifice** : kin condole; the daogong does rites.
5. **Procession and bury** : choose a day to send; bury in the village public graveyard.
6. **After-burial sacrifice** : round the mound, "make the ghost" sacrifice; sweep at Qingming, New-Year; in the taboo term no farming, no song-dance.

## 4. Regional and Subgroup Variants

- **Wuzhishan, Qiongzhong (Qi, Sai dialect areas)** : more traditional custom kept.
- **Baisha, Changjiang (Run dialect area)** : customs near Han areas.
- **Ledong, Sanya (Ha dialect area)** : most populous; clear within-branch differences.
- **Meifu dialect area (Dongfang, Changjiang)** : customs with other traits.
- **Relation to other peoples** : the Li have no five-degree system; mourning is limited by taboos and term, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Burial gear** : coffin (some areas use bamboo-wood mat-wrapped).
- **Religious objects** : daogong instruments, sacrificial vessels.
- **Funeral goods** : ethnic dress, brocade (Li brocade), wine, offerings.
- **Grave marks** : grave marker, stele (more recent).
