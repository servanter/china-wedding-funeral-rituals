# She Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

The She mainly distribute in the mountain areas of Fujian, Zhejiang, Jiangxi, Guangdong, Anhui. The burial is "**earth burial**" main; generally do "**secondary burial**".

**Core elements**:
- **"Doing merit"** — the most representative She funeral, hosted by the "shigong" (or "daoshi"); the liturgy includes "inviting the gods", "walking the gate", "reporting the death", "washing the altar", "doing the funeral", "seeing off the gods", held all night.
- **"Merit song"** — the song of meriting (telling merit, urging filial duty) sung with the rite, part of the She song custom.
- **"Ancestor picture" and "passing-teacher"** — the She "passing-teacher" (ancestor-worship rite) and the funeral come from the same shigong system.
- **Han influence** — do the "sevens", "hundred days", "anniversary" sacrifice terms.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Tang-Song** : the She ancestors ("She people") appear in histories, tying to mountain migration and settlement.
- **Ming-Qing** : gazetteers (Fujian, Zhejiang county gazetteers) record the funeral and "doing merit".
- **Modern** : long living with Han, customs deeply sinicised, but "doing merit" and "merit song" keep She traits.
- **Contemporary** : "doing merit" listed in some provincial intangible-heritage lists.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Dying** : family keep watch; fire a cannon to report death.
2. **Cleanse and dress** : cleanse; wear new clothes (some in festive dress).
3. **Encoffin and keep the spirit** : into the coffin, set the spirit.
4. **Do merit** : the shigong hosts the liturgy, sings the merit song, all night.
5. **Procession and bury** : choose a day to send; earth burial.
6. **Pick bones re-bury** : after years pick the bones into a jar, choose a place to re-bury.
7. **After-burial sacrifice** : do the sevens, "hundred days", "anniversary"; sweep at Qingming, Third of March.

## 4. Regional and Subgroup Variants

- **East Fujian (Ningde, Fu'an)** : She concentrated; the doing-merit custom most flourishes.
- **South Zhejiang (Jingning, Lishui)** : living with Han; rites simplified.
- **West Fujian, east Guangdong** : penetrate with Hakka custom.
- **Relation to other peoples** : the She custom is deeply blended with Han, but "doing merit" and "passing-teacher" are their own tradition, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Religious objects** : shigong instruments (bell, drum, hu-board), ancestor picture, ancestor staff.
- **Funeral goods** : coffin, gold jar (bone-picking), ethnic dress (phoenix dress), offerings.
- **Documents** : merit-song copy, genealogy, liturgy text.
