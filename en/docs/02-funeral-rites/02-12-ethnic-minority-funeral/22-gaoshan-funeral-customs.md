# Gaoshan Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

"Gaoshan" is the collective name for the Austronesian-language peoples of Taiwan (including Amis, Atayal, Paiwan, Bunun, Tsou, Tao/Yami etc.); the mainland also has Gaoshan compatriots. The funeral modes differ vastly by group:

| Mode | Groups | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Indoor burial** | Some Paiwan, Rukai | Buried in the house floor or under stone slabs |
| **Outdoor burial** | Amis, Atayal etc. | Buried in the village-outside graveyard, mostly earth burial |
| **Flexed burial** | Some groups | The body in squatting or flexed position |
| **Cliff burial, tree burial** | Some groups (by cause, status) | For special cases |

**Common trait**: "**ancestor-spirit**" faith is core (e.g. the Paiwan "five-year festival" worships ancestor-spirits); mourning taboos are many (no touching others, no eating certain foods, no collective activity); the funeral is mostly hosted by close kin and the clan elder.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Prehistory to Ming-Qing** : Taiwan archaeology's slab-coffin tombs and indoor-burial remains match the groups' customs; Ming-Qing gazetteers (Taiwan prefecture gazetteer) record "barbarian custom" funerals.
- **Modern** : outside religions (Christianity) entered some groups, changing the funeral form.
- **Contemporary** : the groups' cultural-revival movement brought attention and partial recovery of traditional customs.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Dying** : clan keeps watch; by group, or move to a set position.
2. **Cleanse and dress** : cleanse; wear group dress (some in finery, with ornaments).
3. **Keep the spirit** : lay the body in the house; clan keeps the vigil; no noise.
4. **Procession and bury** : by group custom (indoor or outdoor burial); no coffin or stone slabs, bamboo-wood as gear.
5. **After burial** : hold a purification rite (water, fire or plants purify kin and dwelling); sacrifice the dead clan in the ancestor-spirit festival (e.g. Paiwan five-year festival).
6. **Taboos** : in mourning taboo farming, hunting, song-dance; taboo calling the dead's name.

## 4. Regional and Subgroup Variants

- **Paiwan, Rukai** : indoor burial and slab tombs; deep ancestor-spirit faith.
- **Amis** : outdoor earth burial; the settlement joins collectively.
- **Atayal, Seediq** : mountain earth burial; strict taboos.
- **Tao (Yami)** : island group; with own burial custom and taboos.
- **Relation to other peoples** : the Gaoshan groups belong to the Austronesian cultural system, with no succession relation to the Han five degrees, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Burial gear** : slab coffin, bamboo-wood frame, wooden coffin (modern).
- **Rite goods** : group dress and ornaments, offerings (millet, wine, betel).
- **Faith objects** : ancestor-spirit house, altar (some groups).
