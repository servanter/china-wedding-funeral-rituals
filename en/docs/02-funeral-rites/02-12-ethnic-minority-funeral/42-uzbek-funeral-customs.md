# Uzbek Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

The Uzbek are scattered across Xinjiang (Urumqi, Yining, Kashgar, Shache, etc.). Mostly engaged in commerce and handicrafts, they believe in Islam, and their funerals follow the norms of **prompt burial, earth burial and a simple burial**.

**Core elements**:
- **Prompt burial** — interment on the day of or the day after death.
- **Cleansing and wrapping** — specialists cleanse the body and wrap it in white cloth (three pieces).
- **Funeral prayer (standing prayer)** — the ahong leads the prayer; the congregation forms ranks.
- **The "Nazir"** — on the 7th, 40th and anniversary days, an ahong is invited to chant and guests are received.
- **Taboos** — taboo loud wailing, taboo burning paper money, taboo reading the land's feng shui.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Yuan–Ming**: Uzbek merchants came east along the Silk Road and gradually settled in Xinjiang.
- **Qing dynasty**: concentrated in towns; mosques and the community managed funerary affairs.
- **Contemporary**: the rites continue; urban funeral services are standardised.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Dying**: invite the ahong to chant; family keep watch.
2. **After "returning to truth"**: close the eyes, cover with white cloth.
3. **Cleanse and wrap**: specialists cleanse the body, wrap it in white cloth.
4. **Standing prayer**: hold the standing prayer at the mosque or the graveyard.
5. **Send the burial**: convey to the graveyard for interment, oriented toward Mecca (west).
6. **Nazir**: chant and receive guests on the 7th, 40th and anniversary days.

## 4. Regional and Subgroup Variants

- **Yining, Urumqi**: more concentrated, with standardised rites.
- **Kashgar, Shache**: customs adjacent to those of the Uyghur.
- **Relation to other peoples**: details differ from other peoples who also follow Islamic burial, and its source is not the same as the Han five-degree system; it must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Funeral supplies**: white cloth (shroud), washing bed, corpse-carrying wooden box, spices.
- **Religious objects**: the *Quran*, prayer beads, mosque.
- **Graveyard**: mosque-affiliated burial ground, tombstone.
