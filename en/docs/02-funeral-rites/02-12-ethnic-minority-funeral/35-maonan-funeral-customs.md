# Maonan Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

The Maonan are mainly concentrated in Hechi, Guangxi (the Huanjiang Maonan Autonomous County and nearby). Earth burial is the main mode, and the **second burial** (collecting the bones into a jar for reburial) is widely practised.

**Core elements**:
- **Presided over by the "Shigong" (Daogong)** — the shigong performs the rites of "beating the fast" and "breaking the prison" to deliver the dead.
- **Singing the funeral song** — sing funeral songs during the wake, in Maonan or Zhuang language.
- **Marked influence from Han and Zhuang** — observe the "seventh days", "hundredth day" and "anniversary", close to Zhuang and Han customs.
- **"Fenlong Festival" ancestor worship** — the Maonan "Fenlong (Divide-the-Dragon) Festival" relates to the ancestor-worship tradition.
- **Mourning taboos** — forbid song, dance and marriage; purification rites after burial.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Tang–Song to Ming–Qing**: Maonan forebears (recorded as "Maotan", "Maonan" and similar) appear in histories and gazetteers (the Qingyuan Prefecture gazetteer, etc.).
- **Modern**: intermingled with Zhuang and Han; the funeral customs are highly sinicised.
- **Contemporary**: cremation is being promoted; the rural second burial still survives.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Dying**: family keep watch; fire crackers to announce the death.
2. **Cleanse and dress**: cleanse the body, dress in new clothes.
3. **Encoffin and keep the spirit**: place in the coffin, set up the spirit; the shigong performs rites and sings the funeral song.
4. **Beat the fast and observe the seventh days**: set offerings every seven days and perform the rites.
5. **Funeral and burial**: choose a day to send the burial, earth burial.
6. **Collect the bones for reburial**: after several years, collect the bones into a jar and choose a site for reburial.
7. **After-burial sacrifice**: observe the seventh days and the "anniversary"; sweep tombs at Qingming and the Fenlong Festival.

## 4. Regional and Subgroup Variants

- **Huanjiang (Shangnan, Zhongnan, Xianan)**: the core Maonan settlement, with a complete shigong system.
- **Other parts of Hechi**: intermingled with Zhuang and Han; customs merge.
- **Relation to other peoples**: the Maonan have no five-degree mourning system; mourning is bounded by taboos and the mourning period, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Burial gear**: coffin, bone-collecting pottery jar.
- **Religious objects**: shigong ritual implements, scriptures.
- **Funeral supplies**: paper effigies, paper money, sacrificial livestock, wine, the floral-bamboo hat (a distinctive Maonan object).
