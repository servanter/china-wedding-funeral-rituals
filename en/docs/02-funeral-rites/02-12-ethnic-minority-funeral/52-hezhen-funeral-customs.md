# Hezhen Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

The Hezhen are distributed across Heilongjiang (Tongjiang, Raohe, Fuyuan) and Inner Mongolia, historically living by fishing and hunting. Earth burial is the main mode, with the old custom of **wind burial and tree burial** as well (for special cases).

**Core elements**:
- **The "Liaodangzi" (soul-sending rite)** — the most distinctive Hezhen funeral rite: after a set period (one year, three years, etc.), a shaman performs the "liaodangzi" to send the deceased's soul into the underworld, using ritual implements to "lead the way" and displaying the deceased's belongings.
- **Shaman presides** — the shaman sends the soul and chooses the burial site.
- **The old dog-sacrifice custom** — in old custom a dog was sacrificed (the dog being a fishing/hunting helper, or the idea of "the dog pulling the sledge to lead the way").
- **Mourning garments and taboos** — relatives wear mourning garments (white cloth); during mourning, song/dance and fishing/hunting are forbidden for a set period.
- **Han-cultural influence** — in recent times paper-burning and observing the seventh days are practised.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Ming–Qing**: the Hezhen ("Heijin", "Hezhen", etc.) appear in histories (the *Liubian Jilüe*, etc.); funeral customs are recorded in ethnographies.
- **Modern**: close contact with Han and Manchu; the funeral customs grew more sinicised.
- **Contemporary**: after settling, earth burial is main; the "liaodangzi" rite survives in ethnographic and artistic records.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Dying**: family keep watch; inform the clan.
2. **Cleanse and dress**: cleanse the body, dress in new clothes (or fish-skin/animal-hide garments).
3. **Encoffin and keep the spirit**: place in the coffin, set up the spirit; the shaman chants to the gods.
4. **Funeral and burial**: choose a day to send the burial, earth burial.
5. **Liaodangzi (soul-sending)**: when the period ends, hold the soul-sending rite; the shaman leads the soul into the underworld.
6. **After-burial sacrifice**: sweep tombs at the New Year festival and Qingming.

## 4. Regional and Subgroup Variants

- **Tongjiang (Jiejinkou)**: the settlement area, with more custom records.
- **Raohe (Sipai), Fuyuan**: intermingled with Han; rites simplified.
- **Relation to other peoples**: the Hezhen have no five-degree mourning system; mourning is bounded by taboos and the mourning period, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Religious objects**: shaman drum, shaman robe, spirit idols.
- **Burial gear**: wooden coffin, birch-bark or animal-hide covering.
- **Funeral supplies**: fish-skin/animal-hide garments, bow and crossbow, fishing gear (buried with the dead), sacrificial livestock (dog, pig).
