# Uyghur Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

The Uyghur believe in Islam; the funeral follows "**quick burial, earth burial, plain burial**", carried by the uniquely Uyghur "**nizir**" (post-death memorial rite) for the sacrifice function.

**Points**:

| Content | Note |
| --- | --- |
| **Quick burial** | Bury as soon as possible, mostly same or next day; taboo long staying |
| **Earth burial** | No coffin; white-cloth wrap; grave side-chamber |
| **Plain burial** | No grave goods; no grave adornment |
| **Nizir** | On the 3rd, 7th, 40th, anniversary invite the imam to chant, host guests |
| **Mazar** | The tomb of a saint or worthy; folk go to pray |

**Taboos**: taboo loud crying and chest-beating, taboo bright dress, taboo burning paper money, taboo fengshui.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Karakhanid period**: Islam entered; burial norms gradually set, merging with native custom.
- **Ming-Qing**: under the Yarkand Khanate etc. the "mazar" pilgrimage tradition formed; tomb architecture flourished.
- **Modern**: urban funeral under standard management; religious rites still core.
- **Sources**: the *Quran* and *Hadith*, Chagatai-language documents and local ethnographies.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Dying**: invite the imam to chant, remind to say the shahada; family keep watch.
2. **After returning to truth**: close the eyes, shut the mouth, cover with white cloth, move to the washing place.
3. **Wash and wrap**: by a specialist (man washes man, woman washes woman) wash; wrap in white cloth (three pieces).
4. **Funeral prayer (standing)**: hold the standing prayer at the mosque or graveyard; the imam leads.
5. **Bury**: to the graveyard; grave side-chamber facing Mecca (west).
6. **Nizir**: on the 3rd, 7th, 40th, anniversary invite the imam to chant, host guests.
7. **Mazar pilgrimage**: some families go to the mazar to pray at set times.

## 4. Regional and Subgroup Variants

- **South Xinjiang (Kashgar, Hotan, Aksu)**: strong religious life; solemn nizir; flourishing mazar pilgrimage.
- **North Xinjiang (Ürümqi, Ili)**: town customs simpler; rites still by the law.
- **Relation to other peoples**: the Uyghur funeral is not the same source as the Han five degrees or earth-burial rites, and must be recorded separately; the Islamic-burial peoples differ in detail.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Funeral goods**: white cloth (wrap), water bed, carrying frame ("arawus"), spices.
- **Religious objects**: the *Quran*, rosary, mosque.
- **Graveyard buildings**: mazar (tomb), mosque-attached graveyard, stele (mostly Arabic carved).
