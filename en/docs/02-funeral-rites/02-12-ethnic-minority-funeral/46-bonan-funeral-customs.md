# Bonan Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

The Bonan are mainly concentrated in the Jishishan Bonan, Dongxiang and Salar Autonomous County of Linxia, Gansu. They believe in Islam, and their funerals follow the principles of **prompt burial, earth burial and a simple burial**.

**Core elements**:
- **Prompt burial** — interment on the day of or the day after death.
- **Cleansing and the "Kafan"** — specialists cleanse the body and wrap it in white cloth (three pieces).
- **The "standing prayer" (funeral prayer)** — the ahong leads the prayer; the congregation forms ranks.
- **The "Nazir"** — on the 7th, 40th and anniversary days, an ahong is invited to chant and guests are received.
- **Taboos** — taboo loud wailing, taboo burning paper money, taboo reading the land's feng shui, taboo prolonged laying-out.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Yuan–Ming**: the Bonan formed in the Jishishan area of Hezhou; Islam held a central place in community life.
- **Ming–Qing**: mosques took charge of funerary and memorial affairs.
- **Contemporary**: the rites continue; the mosque community manages funerals.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Dying**: invite the ahong to chant; family keep watch.
2. **After "returning to truth"**: close the eyes, cover with white cloth, inform the community.
3. **Cleanse and wrap**: specialists cleanse the body, wrap it in white cloth.
4. **Standing prayer**: hold the standing prayer at the mosque or the graveyard.
5. **Send the burial**: convey to the graveyard; the grave has a side chamber, oriented toward Mecca (west).
6. **Nazir**: chant and receive guests on the 7th, 40th and anniversary days.

## 4. Regional and Subgroup Variants

- **Jishishan (Dahejia, Liuji)**: the core settlement area, with standardised rites.
- **Other parts of Linxia and Qinghai**: intermingled with Hui, Dongxiang and Salar; similar customs.
- **Relation to other peoples**: details differ from other peoples who also follow Islamic burial, and its source is not the same as the Han five-degree system; it must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Funeral supplies**: white cloth (kafan), washing bed, corpse-carrying wooden box, spices.
- **Religious objects**: the *Quran*, prayer beads, mosque.
- **Graveyard**: mosque-affiliated burial ground, tombstone.
