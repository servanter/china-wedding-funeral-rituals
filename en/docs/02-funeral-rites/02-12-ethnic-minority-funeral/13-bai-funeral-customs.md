# Bai Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

The Bai mainly gather in the Dali Bai Autonomous Prefecture, Yunnan. The burial is "**earth burial**" main (historically cremation), the funeral "**under multiple influence of Buddhism (Azhali), Daoism and Confucian custom**", with refined rites.

**Core elements**:
- **"Do the sevens" and "do the altar"** — the sevens strictly done; invite Daoists or Azhali monks for deliverance rites.
- **"Dongjing society" music** — the Dali Bai "Dongjing society" plays music and chants at the funeral, a marked trait.
- **"Circle the coffin" and "sing the funeral song"** — on the vigil circle the coffin, sing the funeral song.
- **"Benzhao" faith** — the village "benzhao" worship ties to the funeral and sacrifice.
- **Clan and genealogy** — the Bai clan and genealogy system is developed; the funeral hosted by the clan.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Nanzhao, Dali Kingdom**: Buddhism (Esoteric Azhali) flourished; cremation and Buddhist deliverance ran parallel.
- **Yuan-Ming-Qing**: under Han culture and Daoism, gradually turned to earth burial; did the "sevens", "altar".
- **Ming-Qing gazetteers**: Dali prefecture gazetteer, county gazetteers record the rites and "benzhao" sacrifice.
- **Contemporary**: cremation promoted; villages keep doing the sevens, Dongjing music.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Dying**: family keep watch; fire a cannon to report death.
2. **Cleanse and dress**: cleanse; wear grave clothes.
3. **Encoffin and keep the spirit**: into the coffin, set the spirit; invite Daoists or Azhali monks for rites.
4. **Do the sevens / circle the coffin**: an offering every seven days; kin keep the night circling the coffin, singing the funeral song; the Dongjing society plays.
5. **Procession and bury**: choose a day to send, earth burial; the grave mostly by the mountain.
6. **After-burial sacrifice**: round the mound; "hundred days", "anniversary"; sweep at Qingming, Zhongyuan, the Benzhao festival.

## 4. Regional and Subgroup Variants

- **Dali flatland**: Dongjing society, doing the sevens typical.
- **Eryuan, Jianchuan**: mixed with Han, Yi; customs penetrate.
- **Nujiang, Baoshan Bai (Lemo, Nama)**: branches more archaic; near Lisu, Nu custom.
- **Relation to other peoples**: Bai custom blends with Han, but mourning does not take the five degrees, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Religious objects**: instruments, scriptures, Dongjing instruments (flute, xiao, three-string, gong-drum).
- **Funeral goods**: coffin, grave clothes, paper effigies, paper money, offerings.
- **Documents**: obituary, sacrificial text, genealogy.
