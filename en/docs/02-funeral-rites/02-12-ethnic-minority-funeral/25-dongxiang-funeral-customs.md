# Dongxiang Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

The Dongxiang gather in Gansu's Linxia (Dongxiang Autonomous County) and parts of Xinjiang, believing in Islam; the funeral follows "**quick burial, earth burial, plain burial**", with no coffin, white-cloth wrap, the imam presiding.

**Core elements**:
- **Quick burial** — bury the same or next day.
- **Wash and "kafan"** — by a specialist wash, white-cloth wrap (three pieces).
- **"Standing prayer" (funeral prayer)** — the imam leads, all line up.
- **"Nizir"** — on the 7th, 40th, anniversary invite the imam to chant, host guests.
- **Taboos** — taboo loud crying, taboo burning paper money, taboo fengshui, taboo long staying.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Yuan-Ming** : the Dongxiang formed in Gansu's Hezhou area; Islam (with the menhuan order system) was core in community life.
- **Ming-Qing** : the mosque and the menhuan "gongbei" (saint tomb) bore the funeral and memorial affairs.
- **Contemporary** : rites continue; both city and village have mosque funeral services.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Dying** : invite the imam to chant; family keep watch.
2. **After returning to truth** : close the eyes, cover with white cloth; notify the mosque-quarter.
3. **Wash and wrap** : by a specialist wash; white-cloth wrap.
4. **Standing prayer** : hold the standing prayer at the mosque or graveyard.
5. **Send and bury** : to the graveyard; grave side-chamber facing Mecca (west).
6. **Nizir** : on the 7th, 40th, anniversary chant and host guests.

## 4. Regional and Subgroup Variants

- **Gansu Dongxiang Autonomous County** : concentrated; rites standard.
- **Other Linxia areas and Lanzhou** : neighbour Hui, Bonan, Salar; customs alike.
- **Xinjiang Dongxiang** : neighbour Uyghur, Kazakh; customs near them.
- **Relation to other peoples** : the Islamic-burial peoples differ in detail; not the same source as the Han five degrees, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Funeral goods** : white cloth (kafan), water bed, carrying frame ("tabut"), spices.
- **Religious objects** : the *Quran*, rosary, mosque, gongbei.
- **Graveyard** : mosque-attached graveyard, stele (mostly Arabic-carved).
