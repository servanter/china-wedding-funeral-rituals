# Daur Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

The Daur gather in Inner Mongolia's Hulunbuir (Moli Dawa etc.) and Heilongjiang's Qiqihar, Xinjiang's Tacheng. The burial is "**earth burial**" main; the old custom had "**wind burial (sky burial)**" — placing the coffin on a high platform or tree, and after years picking the bones for earth burial.

**Core elements**:
- **Shaman presides** — old custom invited the shaman to send the soul; later under Buddhism (lama) influence, also invited lamas to chant.
- **Horse-sacrifice old custom** — old custom sacrificed the dead's riding horse (or "horse in place of sacrifice", a symbolic rite).
- **"Mourning the spirit" and "sending the soul"** — during the wake mourn the spirit, sing the funeral song; at the procession "send the soul".
- **Mourning taboos** — taboo song-dance and entertainment; the mourning term has dress marks and taboos.
- **Han influence** — modern do burning paper, doing the sevens etc.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Ming-Qing** : the Daur ("Dahur") distributed in the Heilongjiang valley; the funeral recorded in ethnographies.
- **Qing** : moved south to the Nenjiang valley; with Manchu, Han, Ewenki exchange, customs mutually influenced.
- **Xinjiang Daur (Tacheng)** : west-migrated in the Qianlong reign to garrison; funeral near local custom.
- **Contemporary** : cremation gradually promoted; traditional rites remain in villages.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Dying** : family keep watch; notify the clan elder.
2. **Cleanse and dress** : cleanse; wear new clothes.
3. **Encoffin and keep the spirit** : into the coffin, set the spirit; old invited the shaman, later lamas.
4. **Mourn the spirit and send the soul** : during the wake mourn the spirit, sing the funeral song; hold the "send the soul" rite.
5. **Procession and bury** : earth burial main; the old wind-burial put the coffin on a high platform, later pick the bones for earth burial.
6. **After-burial sacrifice** : round the mound, anniversary sacrifice; sweep at Qingming, New-Year.

## 4. Regional and Subgroup Variants

- **Inner Mongolia Moli Dawa, Heilongjiang Qiqihar** : concentrated; more old custom kept.
- **Xinjiang Tacheng** : the west-migrated branch; funeral near Kazakh, Mongolian.
- **Relation to other peoples** : the Daur have no five-degree system; mourning is limited by taboos and term, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Religious objects** : shaman drum, shaman coat (old); lama scriptures, instruments (later).
- **Funeral goods** : coffin, ethnic dress, sacrifice animal (horse, ox).
- **Documents** : obituary, genealogy.
