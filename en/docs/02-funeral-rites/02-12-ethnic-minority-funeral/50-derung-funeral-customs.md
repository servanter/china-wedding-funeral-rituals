# Derung Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

The Derung are concentrated in the Dulong River valley of the Derung ethnic Gongshan Autonomous County, Nujiang, Yunnan. Earth burial is the main mode, with **cremation and water burial** as well (depending on the cause of death and age).

**Core elements**:
- **The "Wu" (shaman) presides** — the shaman "Wu" or "Namusa" chants to send the soul.
- **Zoned burial** — those who died natural deaths are buried near (or inside) the house; those who died violently (difficult childbirth, infectious disease, accident) are buried or water-buried elsewhere, a notable taboo.
- **"Cattle-impaling" sacrifice for the dead** — a bull is sacrificed to the deceased; cattle-impaling is an important Derung rite (also used for sacrificing to heaven and ancestors).
- **Funeral songs and condolence** — sing funeral songs through the night; relatives and friends offer condolences.
- **Mourning taboos** — forbid farming and song/dance; purification rites after burial.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Tang–Song to Ming–Qing**: the Derung forebears ("Qiu people", etc.) and their burial customs appear in histories and local gazetteers.
- **Modern**: because of the closed gorge, traditional customs were preserved longer.
- **Contemporary**: after transport improved, customs grew simpler; the traditional rites still survive in the villages.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Dying**: family keep watch; fire a gun to announce the death.
2. **Cleanse and dress**: cleanse the body, dress in new clothes (or wrap in hemp cloth).
3. **Keep the spirit and condole**: lay out the corpse, set up the spirit; the shaman chants, sing funeral songs.
4. **Cattle-impaling sacrifice for the dead** (some areas): sacrifice a bull.
5. **Funeral and burial**: choose a site for earth burial (violent deaths buried separately or water-buried).
6. **After-burial sacrifice**: round the grave and perform purification rites; sweep tombs at the New Year festival and when sacrificing to heaven and ancestors.

## 4. Regional and Subgroup Variants

- **Upper and lower Dulong River villages**: the upstream and downstream branches differ slightly in custom.
- **Other parts of Gongshan**: intermingled with Nu and Lisu; customs interpenetrate.
- **Relation to other peoples**: the Derung have no five-degree mourning system; mourning is bounded by taboos and the mourning period, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Religious objects**: Wu, Namusa ritual implements, sacrificial vessels.
- **Funeral supplies**: hemp cloth, wooden coffin or bamboo mat, sacrificial livestock (ox, pig, chicken), wine.
- **Distinctive item**: the Derung blanket (a traditional Derung weave, used to wrap the body or for burial).
