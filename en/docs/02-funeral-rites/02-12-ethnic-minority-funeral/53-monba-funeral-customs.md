# Monba Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

The Monba are mainly concentrated in Medog, Nyingchi and Cona of Tibet, believing in **Tibetan Buddhism** alongside primitive beliefs. The modes of burial are diverse — water burial, earth burial, cremation, sky burial, etc. — with **water burial fairly common**.

| Mode | For | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Water burial** | Ordinary people | The body is cast into a river; a common Monba mode |
| **Earth burial** | Some areas, violent deaths | Dug grave burial |
| **Cremation** | Monks, the upper class | Burn, collect the bones |
| **Sky burial** | Some areas | Performed on the sky-burial site |

**Core elements**: invite the lama to chant for deliverance; within 49 days after death, chant, give alms; mourning taboos (forbid song/dance, forbid farming).

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Tang–Song to Ming–Qing**: the Monba forebears' burial customs appear in Tibetan-language documents and local gazetteers.
- **Modern**: Tibetan-Buddhist influence deepened in Monba areas.
- **Contemporary**: the funeral grows simpler, but the religious rites remain at the core.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Dying**: family keep watch; invite the lama to chant.
2. **Lay out the corpse**: wrap in white cloth, place in a corner of the house; the lama chants.
3. **Funeral**: choose an auspicious time to send out; a corpse-bearer (or specialist) conveys it to the burial site.
4. **Perform the burial**: water burial casts into the river; earth burial digs a pit; cremation builds a pyre to burn.
5. **After-burial deliverance**: within 49 days, chant, give alms, offer lamps.
6. **Sacrifice**: chant on the death-day; worship ancestors at the New Year festival.

## 4. Regional and Subgroup Variants

- **Medog (Monba settlement)**: water burial fairly common.
- **Cona, Nyingchi**: intermingled with Tibetan; sky and cremation coexist.
- **Relation to other peoples**: the Monba funeral belongs to the Tibetan-Buddhist cultural system, unrelated to the Han five-degree system; it must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Religious objects**: prayer beads, butter-lamp, prayer flag, mani pile.
- **Funeral supplies**: white cloth, tsampa, barley wine, juniper twigs (smoke offering).
- **Documents**: deliverance scriptures, prayer texts.
