# Kazakh Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

The Kazakh mainly distribute in north Xinjiang (Ili, Altay, Tacheng) and Gansu, Qinghai. The funeral follows "**Islamic norms**" (quick burial, earth burial, white-cloth wrap, no coffin), while keeping the grassland-nomadic "**anniversary sacrifice**" tradition.

**Core elements**:
- **Quick burial and earth burial** — bury the same or next day; white-cloth wrap; grave side-chamber.
- **Imam presides** — washing, standing prayer by the imam.
- **Women sing the elegy** — kin (especially women) sing the elegy ("crying song") in grief.
- **"Nizir"** — on the 7th, 40th, anniversary invite the imam to chant, host guests.
- **Anniversary sacrifice and horse-race** — the anniversary sacrifice ("asu") is solemn; old custom had horse-race, wrestling, goat-grabbing to sacrifice the dead.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **After Islam entered**: burial norms replaced some old grassland customs.
- **Qing**: Kazakh tribes moved into Ili, Altay etc.; funeral matched the faith.
- **Modern**: nomadic society changed; the anniversary sacrifice scaled down.
- **Contemporary**: urban funeral simpler; religious rites still core.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Dying**: invite the imam to chant; family keep watch.
2. **After returning to truth**: close the eyes, cover with white cloth; notify kin and the imam.
3. **Wash and wrap**: by a specialist wash; white-cloth wrap (three pieces).
4. **Standing prayer (funeral prayer)**: hold the standing prayer at the mosque or graveyard.
5. **Send and bury**: to the graveyard; grave side-chamber facing Mecca (west).
6. **Nizir and anniversary**: on the 7th, 40th, anniversary invite the imam to chant, host guests; old anniversary had horse-race etc.

## 4. Regional and Subgroup Variants

- **Ili, Altay, Tacheng**: Kazakh core; rites complete.
- **Gansu Aksai, Qinghai Haixi**: mixed with Hui, Mongol; customs alike.
- **Urban Kazakhs**: funeral simpler; family sacrifice continues.
- **Relation to other peoples**: the Islamic-burial peoples differ in detail; not the same source as the Han five degrees, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Funeral goods**: white cloth (wrap), water bed, carrying frame, spices.
- **Religious objects**: the *Quran*, rosary, mosque.
- **Sacrifice objects**: the anniversary sacrifice animal, yurt furnishings (old custom).
