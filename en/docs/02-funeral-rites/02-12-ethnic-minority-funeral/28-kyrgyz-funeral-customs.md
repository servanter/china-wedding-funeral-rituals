# Kyrgyz Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

The Kyrgyz mainly distribute in Xinjiang's Kizilsu Kyrgyz Autonomous Prefecture and Ili, Aksu. They believe in Islam; the funeral follows "**quick burial, earth burial, plain burial**", keeping the nomadic-society "**elegy and anniversary sacrifice**" tradition.

**Core elements**:
- **Quick burial and earth burial** — bury the same or next day; white-cloth wrap; grave side-chamber.
- **Imam presides** — washing, standing prayer by the imam.
- **Women sing the elegy** — kin (especially women) sing the elegy, telling the dead's life.
- **"Nizir"** — on the 7th, 40th, anniversary invite the imam to chant, host guests.
- **Anniversary sacrifice ("Asi")** — old custom was solemn; with slaughtering and gathering.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **After Islam entered** : burial norms set, merging with old grassland custom.
- **Qing** : Kyrgyz tribes distributed north and south of the Tianshan; the funeral matched the faith.
- **Contemporary** : urban funerals simplified; religious rites core.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Dying** : invite the imam to chant; family keep watch.
2. **After returning to truth** : close the eyes, cover with white cloth.
3. **Wash and wrap** : by a specialist wash; white-cloth wrap.
4. **Standing prayer** : hold the standing prayer at the mosque or graveyard.
5. **Send and bury** : to the graveyard; grave side-chamber facing Mecca (west).
6. **Nizir and anniversary** : on the 7th, 40th, anniversary chant and host guests.

## 4. Regional and Subgroup Variants

- **Kizilsu (Artux, Wuqia)** : concentrated; rites standard.
- **Ili, Aksu** : neighbour Uyghur, Kazakh; customs near them.
- **Relation to other peoples** : the Islamic-burial peoples differ in detail; not the same source as the Han five degrees, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Funeral goods** : white cloth (wrap), water bed, carrying frame, spices.
- **Religious objects** : the *Quran*, rosary, mosque.
- **Sacrifice objects** : the anniversary-sacrifice animal.
