# Jingpo Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

The Jingpo gather in Yunnan's Dehong (Mangshi, Yingjiang, Longchuan etc.). The burial is "**earth burial**" main; "**cremation and sky burial**" for special cases.

**Core elements**:
- **"Dumsa" presides** — the priest "dumsa" hosts the funeral, chants scripture, sends the soul.
- **"Send the soul" along the migration route** — the Jingpo "send the soul" rite guides the dead soul north by the ancestral migration route, matching the group's migration memory.
- **"Bengdong" (mourning dance)** — people circle to dance the mourning dance, all night.
- **"Stab the ox" to sacrifice** — some areas offer an ox ("stab the ox"), sharing the meat with guests.
- **Mourning taboos** — village taboos on farming and song-dance; after burial a purification rite.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Tang-Song to Ming-Qing** : the Jingpo ancestors ("Xunchuan Man" etc.) burial appears in histories (*Man Shu* etc.).
- **Modern** : under Dai, Han influence, the funeral blended somewhat.
- **Contemporary** : traditional rites better kept in mountain areas; towns simpler.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Dying** : family keep watch; fire a gun to report death.
2. **Cleanse and dress** : cleanse; wear new clothes.
3. **Encoffin and keep the spirit** : into the coffin (or bamboo-wood mat), set the spirit; the dumsa chants.
4. **Jump the mourning and stab the ox** : people dance the mourning dance; stab the ox to offer.
5. **Procession and bury** : choose a day to send; earth burial.
6. **Send the soul and purify** : chant the soul-sending scripture, hold the purification rite; sweep at festivals and Qingming.

## 4. Regional and Subgroup Variants

- **Dehong mountains (Longchuan, Yingjiang)** : more traditional rite kept.
- **Flatland (Mangshi, Ruili)** : neighbour Dai; customs penetrate.
- **Relation to other peoples** : the Jingpo have no five-degree system; mourning is limited by taboos and term, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Instruments** : elephant-foot drum, mang-cymbal, flute.
- **Religious objects** : dumsa instruments, sacrificial vessels.
- **Funeral goods** : coffin, sacrifice animal (ox, pig), wine, bamboo-weave gear.
