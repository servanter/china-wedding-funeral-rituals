# Hui Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

The Hui funeral follows "**Islamic burial norms**", with the three principles "**quick burial, earth burial, plain burial**" as the core:

| Principle | Content |
| --- | --- |
| **Quick burial** | Bury as soon as possible after death, generally within three days, mostly the same or next day |
| **Earth burial** | No coffin; the body is placed directly in the grave side-chamber ("lahad"), facing Mecca (west) |
| **Plain burial** | No display, no grave goods; rich or poor all wrapped in white cloth |

**Key rites**: washing the body ("zhuoshui"), wearing the "kafan" (white-cloth shroud, three pieces for men, five for women), the funeral prayer "standing the janaza", sending to the graveyard. **Taboos**: no loud wailing, no burning paper money or effigies, no setting offerings, no choosing days or fengshui.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Tang-Song**: Muslims ("foreign guests", "Huihui") entered China; Islamic burial came with them.
- **Yuan-Ming**: the Hui community formed; mosques and "Hui imams" took the funeral affairs; Hui charity graveyards (Hui public cemeteries) were built.
- **Qing to Republic**: high autonomy in Hui communities; the imam and the mosque-quarter hosted the rites.
- **Contemporary**: Hui funeral follows both religious rites and the state funeral system (traditional Hui earth burial is respected and protected in some cremation zones).

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Dying**: invite the imam to recite "tawbah" (repentance), remind the dead to say the "shahada".
2. **After returning to truth**: close the eyes, tidy the beard, cover with white cloth, move to the "water bed".
3. **Zhuoshui (washing)**: by a specialist (man washes man, woman washes woman) bathe the dead with clear water.
4. **Wear the kafan**: wrap in white cloth — men three pieces (great sheet, small sheet, shirt), women five (plus chest-wrap, head-cover).
5. **Funeral prayer (standing the janaza)**: hold the standing prayer at the mosque or home; the imam leads, all line up.
6. **Send and bury**: to the graveyard; place the dead in the side-chamber (lahad), seal with earth blocks, fill to a low mound (no high tomb).
7. **After-burial memorial**: on the 7th, 40th, 100th day, anniversary invite the imam to "visit the grave" and chant, and slaughter an animal for guests ("arma").

## 4. Regional and Subgroup Variants

- **Northwest (Ningxia, Gansu, Qinghai, Xinjiang)**: highly institutionalised; the mosque-quarter (jiaofang) hosts.
- **Central plains and North China**: mixed with Han; strictly keep religious norms, not adopting Han custom.
- **Southeast (Fujian, Guangdong, Hainan)**: coastal Hui communities; charity graveyards and old tombs remain.
- **Relation to other peoples**: Dongxiang, Salar, Bonan, Uyghur, Kazakh, Kyrgyz, Uzbek, Tajik, Tatar etc. also follow Islamic burial, with local differences, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Burial gear and goods**: water bed, kafan (white cloth), spices (camphor, borneol), box (the reusable "tabut" wood coffin for carrying the body).
- **Religious objects**: mosque, the *Quran*, incense burner.
- **Graveyard**: Hui public cemetery, charity land; steles mostly carved with Arabic and Chinese names, birth-death.
