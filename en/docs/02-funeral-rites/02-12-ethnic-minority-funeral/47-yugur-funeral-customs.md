# Yugur Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

The Yugur are mainly concentrated in the Sunan Yugur Autonomous County of Zhangye, Gansu, divided into the eastern ("Enger") and western ("Yaohuer") branches, which differ in language and custom. Traditionally cremation was the main mode, and in modern times some areas switched to **earth burial**.

**Core elements**:
- **Tibetan-Buddhist influence** — lamas are invited to chant for deliverance; the funeral carries a Tibetan-Buddhist colour.
- **Cremation** — after burning, the ashes are collected and put in a jar or scattered on the hills; some areas have a composite "cremate first, then earth-bury" form.
- **"Sheep offering" for the dead** — some branches offer a sheep as sacrifice.
- **"Hair-cutting" in mourning** — relatives cut their hair (or the horse mane, ornaments) to show grief, a Yugur characteristic.
- **Mourning taboos** — forbid song and dance; the mourning period has dress markers.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Yuan–Ming**: the Yugur forebears ("Sari Uighur") migrated east to the Hexi Corridor; Tibetan Buddhism gradually became the main faith.
- **Ming–Qing**: local gazetteers and ethnographies record their funeral customs.
- **Contemporary**: cremation and earth burial proceed side by side; the religious rites are simplified.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Dying**: family keep watch; invite the lama to chant.
2. **Cleanse and dress**: cleanse the body, dress in new clothes.
3. **Keep the spirit and chant**: lay out the corpse, set up the spirit; the lama chants for deliverance; relatives cut their hair to show grief.
4. **Cremation**: choose a day to send the body for cremation, collect the ashes into a jar or scatter them on the hills.
5. **Earth burial (some areas)**: the ashes or the body are interred and a mound built.
6. **After-burial sacrifice**: chant to transfer merit; sweep tombs at the New Year festival and Qingming.

## 4. Regional and Subgroup Variants

- **East (Sunan Huangcheng, Kangle)**: intermingled with Tibetan and Han; customs interpenetrate.
- **West (Sunan Minghua, Dahe)**: the branch has its own customary features.
- **Relation to other peoples**: Yugur funeral customs are deeply influenced by Tibetan Buddhism, and their source is not the same as the Han five-degree system; they must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Religious objects**: lama ritual implements, scriptures, butter-lamp.
- **Funeral supplies**: firewood (cremation), ash jar, sacrificial livestock (sheep), wine.
- **Documents**: deliverance scriptures, clan genealogy.
