# Mulao Funeral Customs

## 1. Definition

The Mulao gather in Guangxi's Hechi (Luocheng Mulao Autonomous County etc.). The burial is "**earth burial**" main; generally do "**secondary burial**" (pick the bones into a jar re-bury).

**Core elements**:
- **"Shigong" presides** — the shigong does the deliver-the-dead rite; also invite the daogong for the altar.
- **Sing the funeral song** — keep the night singing the funeral song, in both Zhuang and Han.
- **Han-Zhuang influence** — do the "sevens", "hundred days", "anniversary"; the funeral near the Zhuang custom.
- **"Yifan festival"** — the Mulao "Yifan festival" ancestor-worship tradition ties to the funeral ancestor-worship idea.
- **Mourning taboos** — taboo song-dance, taboo weddings; hold a purification rite.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Tang-Song to Ming-Qing** : the Mulao ancestors ("Liao", "Ling" etc.) burial appears in histories and gazetteers (Qingyuan prefecture gazetteer etc.).
- **Modern** : living with Zhuang, Han; customs highly sinicised.
- **Contemporary** : cremation gradually promoted; village secondary burial remains.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Dying** : family keep watch; fire a cannon to report death.
2. **Cleanse and dress** : cleanse; wear new clothes.
3. **Encoffin and keep the spirit** : into the coffin, set the spirit; the shigong does rites, sings the funeral song.
4. **Do the sevens and altar** : an offering every seven days; "beat the zhai" liturgy.
5. **Procession and bury** : choose a day to send; earth burial.
6. **Pick bones re-bury** : after years pick the bones into a jar, choose a place to re-bury.
7. **After-burial sacrifice** : do the sevens, "anniversary"; sweep at Qingming, Yifan festival.

## 4. Regional and Subgroup Variants

- **Luocheng** : Mulao core area; the shigong system complete.
- **Yizhou, Liucheng** : living with Zhuang, Han; customs blended.
- **Relation to other peoples** : the Mulao have no five-degree system; mourning is limited by taboos and term, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Burial gear** : coffin, bone-picking pottery jar.
- **Religious objects** : shigong instruments, scriptures.
- **Funeral goods** : paper effigies, paper money, sacrifice animal, wine.
