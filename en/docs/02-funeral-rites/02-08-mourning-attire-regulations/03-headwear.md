# Headwear: Crown, Fillet and Belt

## 1. Definition

"Headwear" means the head ornaments in mourning dress, with the body-binding "die" and "belt" as one set. The terms:

| Term | Position | Material |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Mourning crown** (guan) | On the head | Cloth; *zhan cui* uses a "rope-knot" (hemp-rope crown-knot), *zi cui* a "cloth-knot" |
| **Head-fillet** (shou jíng) | On the brow, round the head | Hemp; *zhan cui* head-fillet coarsest ("the die is great and droops") |
| **Waist-fillet** (yao jíng) | At the waist | Hemp, twisted |
| **Twisted belt** (jiao dai) | At the waist, binding the body | *Zhan cui* twists raw hemp; *zi cui* down uses a cloth belt |
| **Cloth belt** (bu dai) | At the waist | Cloth |

**Ritual meaning**: the die and belt mark rank by the coarseness of the hemp — "**the die is the solid**" (the *Liji* "Jianzhuan"), meaning the hemp binds the body solid, showing the reality of grief; the crown-knot and belt coarsen and fine by grade.

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Pre-Qin**: the *Yili* "Sangfu" details the crown, die and belt's form and size (e.g. the head-fillet's girth, the waist-fillet's girth, each with grades).
- **Han-Tang**: exegetes fix the sizes; the *Tang Kaiyuan Rites* promulgated them as fixed.
- **Song-Ming**: the *Family Rituals* records the "crown, die, belt, shoes" rite; folk mostly simplified.
- **Modern**: only forms like "mourning cap", "mourning belt", "mourning hoop" survive.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Add the crown**: at assuming the dress first wear the mourning crown.
2. **Tie the head-fillet**: bind hemp round the brow.
3. **Bind the waist-fillet, twisted belt**: bind at the waist, the twisted belt knotted in front.
4. **Change and remove**: at the minor *xiang* change (trained crown), at the major *xiang* remove the fillet.
5. **Remove the dress**: at term remove.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **North China**: the "mourning cap" (white-cloth sewn) and "mourning belt" (white waist-band) are the common forms.
- **Min-Tai (Fujian-Taiwan)**: the filial son "hemp crown", the woman "mourning head" (white-cloth head-wrap), clearly distinguished.
- **Jiangnan**: mostly a white-cloth mourning cap, a white belt at the waist.
- **Ethnic minorities**: mourning head ornaments vary (head-wrap, hair-binding, hair-cutting); their meaning differs from the Han crown-fillet system, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Mourning crown, head-fillet, waist-fillet, twisted belt, cloth belt**.
- **Mourning cap, mourning hoop, mourning belt** (modern forms).
- **Ritual books and dress charts**: *Yili* "Sangfu", *Liji* "Jianzhuan", *Family Rituals* mourning-dress charts.
