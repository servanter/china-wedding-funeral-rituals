# Materials and Grades of Mourning Dress

## 1. Definition

The grade of mourning dress is chiefly realised in **the coarseness of the hemp cloth**, measured by "**thread-count**" (*sheng*): a cloth width of two feet two inches (about 50 cm), within which eighty warp-threads make "one *sheng*". **The fewer the *sheng*, the coarser the cloth**; the more the *sheng*, the finer.

| Grade | Hemp thread-count | Raw/cooked | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Zhan cui** | 3 *sheng* | Raw hemp, not scoured | Coarsest, barely cloth |
| **Zi cui** | 4, 5, 6 *sheng* | Raw hemp (3-year 4, staff-period 5, no-staff 6) | Increasing by grade |
| **Da gong** | 7, 8, 9 *sheng* | Cooked hemp (scoured, ashed) | Coarser work |
| **Xiao gong** | 10, 11, 12 *sheng* | Cooked hemp | Finer |
| **Si ma** | 15 *sheng* minus half (about 14½) | Cooked hemp | As fine as court dress but with half the warps drawn |

**Key principle**: the material of mourning dress is inversely proportional to the mourning period — **the decreasing of grief**: the lighter the dress, the finer the cloth and the more refined the work (the *Liji* "Jianzhuan": "zhan cui three *sheng* … si ma fifteen *sheng* minus half").

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Pre-Qin**: the *sheng*-count system was fixed in the *Yili* "Sangfu" text and commentary, the finest graded measure.
- **Han-Tang**: exegetes detail the conversion of "sheng", "thread", "width"; the Tang *Kaiyuan Rites* set the material by edict.
- **Song-Ming**: in practice mourning was mostly not strictly by *sheng*-count; the *Family Rituals* simplified by custom; Ming-Qing ritual books still keep the *sheng* record for reference.
- **Modern**: the *sheng* concept survives only in ritual study; folk mourning material is replaced by the intuitive "coarse white cloth / fine white cloth" distinction.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Prepare the cloth**: by grade prepare the hemp (or white) cloth of the corresponding *sheng*.
2. **Make the robe**: cut the cui and shang; *zhan cui* leaves the hem unhemmed.
3. **Scour** (*duanzhuo*): from *zi cui* down, *da gong* up, by rite "scour and ashing" (wash, beat, boil in ash-lye) to soften the hemp.
4. **Assume the dress**: wear and perform the rite.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Han, all regions**: folk replace *sheng*-count with the intuitive "coarse cloth for heavy dress, fine cloth for light"; Min-Tai divides into three grades "hemp, ramie, white cloth".
- **Ethnic minorities**: mostly make mourning dress of local cloth (hemp, wool, pulu, homespun); their material system is not measured by *sheng*-count, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Hemp cloth**: raw hemp (*ju ma*), cooked hemp (*mu ma*).
- **Measure terms**: *sheng*, thread, width (two feet two inches).
- **Ritual basis**: the *Yili* "Sangfu" text and commentary, the *Liji* "Jianzhuan", the *Tang Kaiyuan Rites*.
