# Contemporary Mourning Dress (Filial Cloth, Black Armband, White Flower)

## 1. Definition

Contemporary mourning dress is the **folk-simplified form** of the traditional five-degree system, with "**filial cloth**" as the core material, with filial cap, filial belt, black armband and white flower as adjuncts, marking the mourner's status and near/far.

| Form | Shape | Near/far mark |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Filial cloth** | White cloth strip, tied at waist or arm | Width and length mark near/far |
| **Filial cap / hoop** | White-cloth cap or white head-wrap | Used by lineal kin |
| **Filial belt** | White waist-band | Filial son and daughter |
| **Black armband** | Black cloth armband | General kin and friends, common in cities |
| **White flower** | Paper or silk flower, worn on the breast | Common |
| **Filial shoe** | White-cloth shoe-cover ("sealing the filial shoe") | Children |

**Grades**: folk divide into "great mourning" (filial son/daughter: mourning robe, cap, belt), "lesser mourning" (grandchildren, nephews: white cloth, white flower), "light mourning" (other kin/friends: black armband, white flower).

## 2. Historical Origins

- **1930 Civil Code**: replaces the dress system with degrees of kinship; the five degrees lose legal effect.
- **After the 1950s**: funeral reform and custom-change simplified the filial cloth and period; cities use black armband and white flower.
- **Since the 1980s**: rural traditional filial dress (robe, cap, belt) spread again; the old elaborate dress did not return.
- **Contemporary**: city funeral homes offer unified "mourning-dress rental" (robe, cap), and promote black armband, white flower, breast flower.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Assume the dress (wear mourning)**: after the great encoffining, distribute filial cloth, cap, black armband by near/far.
2. **Wear**: the filial son ties the hemp rope or white belt, the cap covers the head; general kin/friends wear the black armband and white flower.
3. **Perform**: use the worn mourning dress to mark the order and status in the rite.
4. **Remove mourning** (take off the dress): nodes are the procession, hundred days, anniversary, three years; Min-Tai ends with "joining the furnace".
5. **Dispose of the dress**: some areas burn or keep the filial cloth and dress as a memento.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **North China**: mourning robe, cap, hemp rope, white shoe "sealing the filial shoe".
- **Min-Tai (Fujian-Taiwan)**: three grades "hemp shirt, ramie shirt, white shirt", with a "filial belt".
- **Guangdong**: white-cloth mourning dress, mourning staff; the filial cloth's width marks near/far.
- **Cities**: black armband, white flower, armband; funeral-home unified mourning-dress rental.
- **Ethnic minorities**: mourning marks vary (filial cloth, hair-cutting, forbidding ornaments, specific-colour clothes), with no succession relation to the Han filial-dress system, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Mourning-dress class**: mourning robe, cap (hoop), belt, shoe, sleeve.
- **Mark class**: black armband, white flower, breast flower, armband.
- **Documents**: the "mourning-degree line" in the obituary (e.g. "unfilial son so-and-so, term-mourning grandson so-and-so"), the written remnant of the five degrees.
