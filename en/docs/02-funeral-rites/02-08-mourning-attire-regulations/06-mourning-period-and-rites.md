# The Mourning Period and Its Rites

## 1. Definition

The mourning period, counted in months, is the most intuitive measure of the five-degree grades:

| Grade | Nominal period | Actual (with change-and-removal) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Zhan cui** | Three years | 27 months (with the *dan*) |
| **Zi cui three years** | Three years | 27 months |
| **Zi cui staff-period** | One year (qi) | 13 months |
| **Zi cui no-staff-period** | One year | 13 months |
| **Zi cui three months** | Three months | Three months |
| **Da gong** | Nine months (some seven) | Nine months |
| **Xiao gong** | Five months | Five months |
| **Si ma** | Three months | Three months |

**"The three-year mourning" is actually 25/27 months**: the *Liji* ("Sannian Wen") explains "three years" as a number of "**added weight**" — "the three-year mourning is finished in twenty-five months", because "grief is not yet spent, longing not yet forgotten", so the name "three years" is taken as the utmost dress ("**the three years is the universal mourning under heaven**").

## 2. Historical Origins

- **Pre-Qin**: the *Yili* ("Sangfu") fixes the months; the *Liji* ("Sannian Wen") expounds the meaning of the three-year mourning.
- **Han-Wei**: the "twenty-five months" vs "twenty-seven months" dispute (Zheng Xuan held twenty-seven, Wang Su twenty-five).
- **Tang**: the *Kaiyuan Rites* fixed the 27-month system (with the *dan*), and adjusted "father living, for the mother".
- **Ming**: "father living, for the mother" also *zhan cui* three years (mother's mourning elevated); "mother for the eldest son" changed to *zi cui* no-staff.
- **Qing**: the *Comprehensive Rituals* followed the Ming.
- **Modern**: the actual period simplifies to the procession, hundred days, first anniversary.

## 3. Ceremonial Procedure

1. **Assuming the dress**: the day after the great encoffining, assume the dress; the period counts from then (or from the day of death, varying by age).
2. **Once buried (third month)**: receive the dress, end the wailing.
3. **Minor *xiang* (13th month)**: trained crown and robe, perform the minor-*xiang* sacrifice.
4. **Major *xiang* (25th month)**: remove the heavy dress, perform the major-*xiang* sacrifice.
5. ***Dan* (27th month)**: perform the *dan* sacrifice, remove the dress.
6. **Auspicious sacrifice**: after the *dan*, perform the auspicious sacrifice, resume normal life.

## 4. Regional and Ethnic Variants

- **Han, all regions**: take "to the procession", "fifth seven", "hundred days", "anniversary", "three years" as nodes, roughly matching the ritual system.
- **Ethnic minorities**: the length and counting of the mourning period differ, mostly by religious rite or farming season, and must be recorded separately.

## 5. Objects and Documents

- **Sacrifice furnishings**: the offerings and prayers of the minor *xiang*, major *xiang*, *dan*.
- **Time terms**: *qi*, nine months, five months, three months, *dan*, minor *xiang*, major *xiang*.
- **Documents**: the "mourning-degree line" in the obituary, the genealogy's mourning record.
